3. Do you rebuild transmissions at your Springfield shop?

Absolutely! Ace Transmission Service in Springfield performs professional transmission rebuilds. We disassemble, clean, repair, and reassemble transmissions to restore them to excellent working condition using high-quality parts.

4. What kind of warranty do your transmission repairs come with?

We stand behind the quality of our work. Most transmission repairs and rebuilds performed at Ace Transmission Service in Springfield come with a warranty on both parts and labor, giving you peace of mind.

5. How often should I get a transmission fluid change for my vehicle?

It's generally recommended to get a transmission fluid change and filter replacement every 50,000 to 100,000 miles, depending on your vehicle's make and model and the type of transmission. Our technicians can advise you on the best maintenance schedule for your specific car.
