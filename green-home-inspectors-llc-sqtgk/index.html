What We Inspect:

Our comprehensive home inspection typically includes:

Structural elements: Foundation, walls, floors, and roof structure. Exterior components: Roof, gutters, downspouts, siding, windows, doors, decks, and landscaping drainage. Interior features: Walls, ceilings, floors, stairs, and HVAC systems (heating, ventilation, and air conditioning). Electrical systems: Panels, wiring, outlets, and circuit breakers. Plumbing systems: Water supply lines, drainage, fixtures, and water heater. Appliances: Oven, refrigerator, dishwasher, and disposal (if applicable). Other systems: Fireplaces, insulation, moisture issues, and more.

While the specific scope can be tailored upon request, our standard inspection is designed to provide a holistic view of the property's above-ground structure and systems, identifying potential issues that might not be immediately visible.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What exactly does a home inspection cover?

A standard home inspection from Green Home Inspectors LLC at our Miami location examines the visible components of a property's structure and systems, including the foundation, roof, walls, floors, ceilings, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, and more. It provides an overview of the property's condition as of the inspection date.

2. How long does a typical home inspection take?

Typically, a comprehensive home inspection takes between 2.5 to 3.5 hours, depending on the size and age of the property. Our experienced inspectors work efficiently to ensure a thorough examination.

3. When should I schedule a home inspection?

The ideal time to schedule a home inspection is typically within 3-5 days before closing on your new property in the Miami area. This allows you sufficient time to review the report and address any concerns with the seller.

4. What happens after the inspection? Will I get a written report?

Yes! Within 24-48 business hours, you will receive a detailed, professionally written report outlining the findings from our inspection at 12485 SW 137th Ave STE 212. The report includes descriptions, photos, and our assessment of the property's condition.

5. Do you offer inspections for new construction homes?

Absolutely. Green Home Inspectors LLC provides specialized pre-drywall or pre-finish inspections for new construction homes throughout the Miami area. These inspections are crucial to catch any potential issues early.
