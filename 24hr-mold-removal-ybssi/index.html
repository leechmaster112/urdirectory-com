24hr Mold Removal

24hr Mold Removal connects property owners with a national network of IICRC AMRT-certified mold remediation contractors, available 24 hours a day. Every project is performed under the ANSI/IICRC S520-2024 standard, the consensus standard of care for professional mold remediation in the United States. Unlike contractors who rely on biocide spraying or surface treatment, our network performs source removal: physical removal of contaminated porous materials, HEPA cleaning of surrounding surfaces, drying to ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, and post-remediation verification by an independent third-party Indoor Environmental Professional. All work is documented in Xactimate format for insurance claim files. Services include emergency mold remediation for residential and commercial properties, black mold (Stachybotrys chartarum) remediation, post-water-damage mold prevention, HVAC system mold remediation, mold inspection, and laboratory mold testing. Service area covers all 50 states with regional dispatch hubs in Florida, Texas, Virginia, Colorado, Nevada, and Utah. Credentials documented on every scope of work: IICRC Certified Firm status, AMRT-certified technician credentials, ANSI/IICRC S520-2024 standard reference by edition, Florida MRSR/MRSA license verification where applicable, and acceptance of independent post-remediation verification on every project. Response time: under 60 minutes for emergency dispatch. Typical project duration: 3 to 10 days. Cost range: $500 for single-bathroom Condition 2 remediation up to $30,000 or more for whole-home Condition 3 contamination, with full documentation supporting insurance claims under HO-3 sudden-and-accidental coverage and Florida Statute 627.7142 mold sublimits. Call (888) 311-4399 for 24/7 dispatch.
