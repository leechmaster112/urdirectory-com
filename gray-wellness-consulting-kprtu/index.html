Gray Wellness Consulting

Gray Wellness Consulting, LLC provides online mental health counseling and workplace consulting throughout Massachusetts and Rhode Island. The practice is led by Lisa A. Gray, MEd, LMHC — a Licensed Mental Health Counselor with 15 years in private practice and 18 years in healthcare. Clinical services specialize in anxiety, depression, ADHD, and trauma. Offerings include individual therapy for adults, teens, and children age 6 and up; couples counseling; family therapy grounded in family-systems and structural approaches; and child and teen counseling with a strong family component. Every session is conducted by Lisa personally — no rotating clinicians, no intake coordinators. Sessions are delivered over secure video for clients physically located in Massachusetts (LMHC #8945) or Rhode Island (License MHC01493). Modalities include Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Internal Family Systems (IFS), mindfulness-based practices, family systems, structural family therapy, person-centered work, and trauma-focused approaches, tailored to each client. Gray Wellness Consulting also provides workplace consulting for small and mid-sized businesses, healthcare practices, and leadership teams. Programs include Performance Coaching, Interpersonal Skills Training, Wellness in the Workplace, and Custom Programs — delivered virtually, onsite, or in hybrid formats. Programs starting at $500. Session fees: $150 individual, $200 couples or family. Free 15-minute consultations available. Out of network with all insurance plans; superbills provided for clients seeking out-of-network reimbursement. Accepted payment methods: HSA, Visa, Mastercard, Venmo. Lisa A. Gray earned her Master of Education from Bridgewater State University in 2011 and holds Massachusetts elementary teacher (2003) and school adjustment counselor (2013) certifications. Her teaching background informs her work with children, teens, and school-related issues. Gray Wellness Consulting is based in Westport, Massachusetts. All therapy sessions are conducted online; no in-person clinical office.
