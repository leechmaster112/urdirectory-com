Halo Roofing

Halo Roofing is a premier, full-service roofing contractor based in Bolton, renowned for delivering elite craftsmanship across Greater Manchester and Lancashire. Owned and operated by Calvin O’Neill, the company has built a formidable reputation through word-of-mouth excellence, consistently maintaining a two-month lead time due to high demand. Halo Roofing specializes in high-revenue full re-roofing and roof replacements, providing homeowners with durable, weather-tight solutions backed by a personal, non-corporate service touch. As a versatile specialist, Halo Roofing handles all aspects of the roofline, excluding metal work. Their expertise spans traditional pitched slate and tile roofing to modern flat roof systems (including EPDM rubber, GRP fibreglass, and felt). Notably, they are one of the few local firms providing comprehensive end-to-end chimney services, including structural repairs, removal, and specialist re-leading. Whether addressing an emergency leak or executing a large-scale commercial maintenance contract, Halo Roofing combines local reliability with the capacity to scale, currently expanding their fleet to meet the growing needs of the North West.
