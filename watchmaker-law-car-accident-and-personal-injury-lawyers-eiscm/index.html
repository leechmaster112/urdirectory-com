Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of car accidents do you handle in Edmonton?

A1: We handle all types of car accidents in Edmonton, including rear-end collisions, T-bone accidents, multi-vehicle pile-ups, intersection accidents, and accidents involving pedestrians or cyclists. Our focus is on providing legal support regardless of the specific circumstances of your crash.

Q2: How long do I have to file a claim after a car accident in Edmonton?

A2: In Edmonton, the limitation period for filing a personal injury claim, including car accidents, is generally two years from the date of the accident. It is crucial to consult with us as soon as possible to ensure your claim is filed within this timeframe.

Q3: Do I need to go to court for my car accident case?

A3: Many car accident cases are resolved through settlement negotiations with the insurance company without needing to go to court. However, if a fair settlement cannot be reached, our firm is prepared to represent you in court and fight for your entitled rights.

Q4: What should I do immediately after a car accident in Edmonton?

A4: After ensuring everyone's safety, check for injuries and call emergency services if needed. Exchange information with the other driver(s), gather contact and insurance details. Avoid admitting fault or making recorded statements to insurance companies without consulting us first. Seek medical attention promptly, even for seemingly minor injuries.

Q5: How much does it cost to hire Watchmaker Law for my Edmonton car accident or injury case?

A5: We offer a free initial consultation. For your case, we operate on a contingency fee basis. This means you don't pay anything out-of-pocket unless we successfully secure a settlement or verdict on your behalf. Our fee will be a percentage of the amount recovered, as agreed upon during your consultation.
