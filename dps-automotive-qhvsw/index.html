Frequently Asked Questions:
Q1: What is paint protection film (PPF) and how does it benefit my vehicle?

A1: Paint protection film (PPF) is a transparent, thermoplastic urethane film applied to painted surfaces of a new or used vehicle in order to protect them against stone chips, bug splatters, and minor abrasions. It acts as a sacrificial layer, preserving your vehicle's original paintwork from everyday damage.
