Location

Dr. Kelly's Surgical Unit - North Phoenix, located at 4727 E Bell Rd #11, Phoenix, AZ 85032, is a specialized veterinary practice dedicated exclusively to surgical care for companion animals. We pride ourselves on providing advanced surgical techniques and compassionate care within our state-of-the-art surgical facility located right here in North Phoenix.

Our mission at Dr. Kelly's Surgical Unit is to deliver the highest standard of surgical care for pets requiring operative intervention. We understand that bringing your pet for surgery can be a stressful time for owners, and our team is committed to making this experience as smooth and anxiety-free as possible, from the initial consultation through recovery.

Our facility is designed specifically for surgical procedures, ensuring a safe and controlled environment for your beloved companion. We utilize the latest advancements in veterinary surgical technology and maintain rigorous infection control protocols to provide the safest surgical outcomes possible.

Our Surgical Services include, but are not limited to:

Spay and Neuter Procedures (Ovariohysterectomy and Castration) Dental Surgery (Extractions, Root Canal Therapy, Gum Disease Treatment) Orthopedic Surgery (Fracture Repair, Luxating Patella Correction, TPLO) Soft Tissue Surgery (Mass Removal, Biopsy, Foreign Body Removal) Mass Removal and Biopsy Incision and Drainage of Abscesses or Wounds Foreign Body Removal Exploratory Surgery

We work closely with referring veterinarians and primary care providers to ensure seamless coordination of care for your pet. Our focus is solely on surgical intervention, allowing us to concentrate our expertise and resources on achieving the best possible results for your animal.

At Dr. Kelly's Surgical Unit, we believe in utilizing minimally invasive surgical techniques whenever appropriate to reduce pain, speed up recovery, and minimize scarring. Our team includes experienced surgical technicians and support staff dedicated to patient comfort and safety throughout the surgical process.

For appointments, consultations, or to learn more about our surgical services, please contact us directly. We are committed to providing exceptional surgical care right here at our Phoenix location.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What types of surgeries do you perform?

A: We specialize in a wide range of surgical procedures including spay and neuter, dental work, orthopedic surgeries (like fracture repair), soft tissue surgeries (like mass removal), exploratory surgery, and foreign body removal. Our focus is on surgical intervention.

Q2: How do I schedule a surgical consultation?

A: You can schedule a consultation by calling our office at (602) 641-4861. We will gather necessary background information and determine if surgery is the appropriate treatment option for your pet.

Q3: What should I expect before my pet's surgery?

A: We will discuss the procedure, anesthesia, and post-operative care during your consultation. Typically, pre-operative bloodwork is required. We will provide specific instructions regarding fasting and any other preparations needed for the day of surgery.

Q4: How long does anesthesia typically last?

A: The duration of anesthesia depends on the complexity and length of the surgical procedure. We continuously monitor your pet during anesthesia and adjust the anesthetic protocol as needed to ensure safety and comfort.

Q5: What is the typical recovery process after surgery?

A: Recovery varies based on the type of surgery performed. We will provide detailed post-operative instructions including pain management, activity restriction, and follow-up care. Most pets require a few days to a week or two for full recovery, depending on the procedure.
