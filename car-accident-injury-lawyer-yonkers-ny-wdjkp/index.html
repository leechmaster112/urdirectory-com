Q: Do I have a case if I was partially at fault for my Yonkers car accident?

In New York, which follows a comparative negligence system, you may still be able to recover damages even if you were partially at fault, as long as your fault is less than 50%. The amount you can recover may be reduced by your percentage of fault.

Q: What should I do immediately after a car accident in Yonkers?

Ensure safety first. Move to a safe location if possible. Exchange information with the other driver(s), including names, contact details, insurance information, and vehicle details. Report the accident to the police and seek medical attention even if you feel fine initially.
