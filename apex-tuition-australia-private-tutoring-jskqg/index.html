Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What subjects do you tutor?

Apex Tuition Australia offers comprehensive tutoring across a wide range of subjects, including but not limited to English, Mathematics (including Algebra, Geometry, Calculus, Statistics), Science (Biology, Chemistry, Physics), Humanities (History, Geography, Civics and Citizenship), Languages (ESL/English as a Second Language, Mandarin, French, Spanish), and preparation for major exams like the HSC, VCE, IB, andATAR.

Do you only offer in-home tutoring?

No, we offer both in-home and online tutoring services. Our online platform provides a flexible and interactive alternative for students who prefer learning remotely or require tutoring outside their local area.

How do I enrol my child?

Enrolment is simple! You can contact us via phone at 0405 532 487 or visit our website https://www.apextuitionaustralia.com/ to request a consultation. During this consultation, we will discuss your child's needs, schedule a trial session, and finalise the details of their personalised tutoring plan.

Are your tutors qualified?

Absolutely. All of our tutors undergo a rigorous selection process. They hold relevant teaching qualifications, university degrees, or significant subject matter expertise, and many have previous tutoring or teaching experience. We are committed to employing only the most dedicated and effective educators.

How long does a typical tutoring session last?

Our standard tutoring sessions are typically 50 minutes long, which aligns with common school recess schedules and allows for focused learning periods. We can also accommodate longer or shorter sessions based on your specific requirements.
