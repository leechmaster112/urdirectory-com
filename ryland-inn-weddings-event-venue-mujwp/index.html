Ryland Inn Weddings & Event Venue

Classic tavern with a modern twist - think locally sourced ingredients, craft cocktails, bold flavors, and comforting favorites reimagined. Landmark Taverns is thrilled to bring you three locations: Logan Inn in New Hope, PA, Stone House in Warren, NJ, and Ryland Inn in Whitehouse Station, NJ. Weddings & Events: Romantic, luxurious, and perfectly designed for your special day, The Ryland Inn offers a stunning blend of equestrian charm and refined elegance. Built in 1796 as a former country home and stagecoach stop, this historic venue has been thoughtfully redesigned to be a one-of-a-kind wedding and event space.
