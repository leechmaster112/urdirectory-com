Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer unlimited rental time for your photobooths?

Yes, absolutely! At Marabooth, we understand that events can be unpredictable, and you want your photobooth available whenever your guests want to use it. That's why our rental price includes unlimited time. You pay one fixed price, and the photobooth is yours for the duration of your event, whether it's a few hours or longer.

What types of events do you cater to?

We specialize in a wide variety of events! Our primary focus is on weddings, but we also excel at corporate events (like conferences, meetings, and team building), anniversaries, birthdays, fundraisers, block parties, engagements, quinceañeras, and many other special occasions. Basically, if you need a fun, interactive photo experience, we're your go-to!

What styles or themes of photobooths do you offer?

We pride ourselves on offering a diverse selection of photobooths to match any event's theme or desired aesthetic. Our collection typically includes classic black and white models, vibrant pop-up booths, elegant wedding-themed units, fun and quirky options, and sometimes seasonal or novelty themes. We can discuss your preferences and find the perfect fit.

How far in advance should I book my photobooth?

It's always best to book as early as possible, especially for popular dates and during peak wedding season! While we can sometimes accommodate last-minute requests, securing your photobooth early ensures availability for your specific date and preferred model. We recommend booking at least a few weeks in advance for weddings and larger corporate events.

What does the rental include? Do you handle setup and takedown?

Our rental package includes the photobooth unit itself, professional setup by our team upon arrival, a full roll of high-quality photo paper, props (often included or available for rent), operation assistance, printing services throughout the event, and professional takedown after the event. We handle everything so you can simply enjoy your celebration!
