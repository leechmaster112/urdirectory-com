California Specialty Pharmacy - Pomona Infusion Center

California Specialty Pharmacy, an Acelpa Health company, is a specialty infusion center and pharmacy in Pomona, CA. Our infusion suite is on North Towne Avenue in North Pomona. We provide infusion therapy on-site and at home across the San Gabriel Valley and Inland Empire. Therapies include immune globulin (IVIG and SCIG), intravenous antibiotics, parenteral nutrition (TPN), heart failure infusions, biologic therapies, and oncology infusion support. Our pharmacists and nurses build a treatment plan for every patient, support benefits verification and prior authorization, and stay in close contact with your physician. Accredited by ACHC and URAC. Referrals are accepted from hospitals, clinics, and physician offices.
