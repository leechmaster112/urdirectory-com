Q2: Do I need X-rays for my car accident injury treatment?

A: While X-rays are a standard diagnostic tool in chiropractic care to visualize spinal structure and identify potential fractures or abnormalities, they are not always required for initial treatment. Our primary assessment relies heavily on physical examination and patient history. X-rays may be recommended if your symptoms suggest a more serious underlying condition or if your chiropractor deems it necessary for your specific case.

Q3: How many chiropractic visits will I need after a car accident?

A: The number of visits required varies significantly depending on the severity of your injury, your overall health, your body's response to treatment, and your commitment to the recommended care plan, including any prescribed exercises. Some patients experience significant improvement within a few weeks, while others may require several months of consistent care. Our treatment plan is tailored to your individual needs and progress.

Q4: Is chiropractic care covered by insurance for car accident injuries?

A: Insurance coverage for chiropractic care related to car accidents can vary widely depending on your specific insurance policy, the details of your accident, and the state regulations in Washington. Additionally, if your car accident was not your fault, the at-fault party's insurance may cover chiropractic treatment. We can assist you in understanding your options.
