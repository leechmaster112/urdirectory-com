Welcome to Eastside Modern Dentistry, located at 640 NW Gilman Blvd #100, Issaquah, WA 98027. At Eastside Modern Dentistry, we believe that exceptional dental care should be accessible, comfortable, and effective. We leverage the latest advancements in dental technology and techniques to ensure you receive the best possible treatment. Our state-of-the-art facility and skilled team are designed to make your visit smooth and stress-free, from your initial consultation to your follow-up appointments.

Our comprehensive range of services includes everything from preventative care to restorative and cosmetic procedures, ensuring all your dental needs are met under one roof.

Preventative Care: We emphasize the foundation of healthy smiles with thorough dental cleanings, professional teeth examinations, and personalized oral hygiene guidance. Regular check-ups and cleanings are crucial for maintaining long-term dental health and preventing future issues.

Restorative Dentistry: We restore damaged or decayed teeth to their full function and appearance. Our services include durable and natural-looking dental fillings, essential crowns (caps), reliable dental bridges, and state-of-the-art dental implants to replace missing teeth.

Cosmetic Dentistry: Enhance the beauty and confidence of your smile with our cosmetic services. We offer teeth whitening for a brighter grin, professional teeth contouring and reshaping for a more harmonious appearance, and custom veneers to mask imperfections and create a stunning smile.

Advanced Treatments: Addressing complex dental concerns, our practice offers InvisalignⓇ clear aligners for discreet orthodontic correction, effective gum disease treatment, and comprehensive TMJ/TMD (temporomandibular joint disorder) therapy for jaw pain and discomfort.

Choosing Eastside Modern Dentistry means choosing a practice that prioritizes your well-being and comfort. We take the time to listen to your concerns, explain your options clearly, and tailor our approach to meet your specific needs. We believe in building lasting relationships based on trust, respect, and exceptional care.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Do you accept new patients?

Yes, we welcome new patients! We invite you to schedule a consultation to meet our team and learn more about how we can help you achieve optimal oral health. You can book your appointment by calling us at (425) 472-1044.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept a variety of payment options, including cash, major credit cards, and dental insurance. Our team will work with you to understand your coverage and ensure a smooth claims process.

How often should I schedule dental check-ups?

We generally recommend scheduling a comprehensive dental check-up and cleaning every six months. However, the frequency may vary based on your individual oral health needs, so we will determine the best schedule for you during your visit.

Do you offer emergency dental services?

While we strive to accommodate all patients, we understand dental emergencies can arise. Please call our office at (425) 472-1044 during regular business hours, and we will do our best to assist you promptly. For after-hours emergencies, you may need to contact a local emergency dental service.

Are financing options available for dental work?

Yes, we understand that dental care can be a significant investment. We offer financing options through third-party providers to help make treatment more affordable. Please inquire about our financing plans when you schedule your appointment.
