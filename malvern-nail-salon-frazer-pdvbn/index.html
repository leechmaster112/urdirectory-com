Q2: What kind of gel products do you use at your Malvern location?

A2: We use high-quality professional gel systems known for their durability, longevity, and beautiful finish. Our products are chosen for their ability to provide strong, chip-resistant results and are applied by our trained technicians following best practices.

Q3: Can I get a gel builder service if I have weak or damaged nails?

A3: Yes, the Gel Builder service is often recommended for clients with weak or damaged nails. It strengthens the nail plate while adding length. However, a consultation is always recommended to assess your nail condition and determine if this service is the best option for you.

Q4: How long should I wait before getting my nails wet after a Gel Builder service?

A4: For optimal curing and to ensure the strength of your Gel Builder nails, we recommend avoiding submerging your hands in water (like baths, swimming, or even excessive handwashing) for at least 24 hours after your service. After this period, gentle care is still advised.
