NStar Builds

NStar Builds is an established building company based in North London, providing high-quality construction, renovation, and refurbishment services to homeowners, landlords, and commercial property owners across London and Essex. With a fully managed, single-point-of-contact approach, NStar Builds takes on projects of all sizes — from minor home improvements through to complete property transformations. Core services include loft conversions, house extensions, full property refurbishments, kitchen and bathroom renovations, roofing, drylining, painting and decorating, skylight installations, and window fitting. Operating predominantly across Haringey, North London, and the wider Essex area, every project is carried out by experienced, skilled tradespeople who prioritise quality workmanship, transparent communication, and on-time delivery. Whether adding square footage through a rear extension, converting an unused loft into a functional living space, or breathing new life into an outdated interior, each build is approached with careful planning and attention to detail. For residential and commercial clients seeking a reliable, all-trades building contractor in London and Essex, NStar Builds offers a streamlined, stress-free experience from first consultation through to project completion.
