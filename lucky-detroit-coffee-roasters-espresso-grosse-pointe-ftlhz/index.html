Location

Welcome to Lucky Detroit Coffee Roasters Espresso Grosse Pointe, located at 17001 Kercheval Ave, Grosse Pointe, MI 48230. We are proud to serve the Grosse Pointe community with our passion for exceptional coffee and a welcoming atmosphere.

At our roastery and espresso location, we specialize in crafting high-quality coffee experiences right here in Grosse Pointe. Our skilled roasters carefully transform green coffee beans into our signature, freshly roasted beans, ensuring every cup bursts with rich flavor and aroma. We take pride in our commitment to quality, sourcing beans from ethical producers and roasting them to perfection on a state-of-the-art machine right here at our Grosse Pointe facility.

Our espresso program is at the heart of what we do. We meticulously prepare our espresso using our top-grade equipment to create velvety crema and balanced shots, the foundation for our delicious beverages. From classic espresso and cappuccino to intricate latte art and refreshing cold brews, our skilled baristas are dedicated to crafting drinks that delight your taste buds. We offer a rotating selection of house-made pastries and light snacks, all prepared with locally sourced ingredients whenever possible, providing a perfect complement to your coffee.

Step into our cozy and inviting space, designed to be a comfortable haven for coffee lovers in Grosse Pointe. Whether you're here for a quick caffeine boost, a meeting, or simply to enjoy a moment of relaxation, we aim to make you feel welcome. We believe in the power of community and strive to be a positive part of the Grosse Pointe neighborhood.

For more information about our coffee, our roasting process, and to view our current menu, please visit our website: https://www.luckydetroit.com/pages/grosse-pointe-location. You can also reach us directly at 2486866955.
