Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is Botox?

A: Botox is a purified form of Botulinum Toxin Type A, a neurotoxin that, when injected by a professional, temporarily reduces muscle activity in the treated areas, diminishing the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles.

Q2: How does Botox work at Botox La Belle Peau?

A: At our Henderson spa, our trained professionals administer Botox injections precisely. They target specific muscles causing dynamic wrinkles, leading to a smoother, more relaxed look for our clients in Henderson.

Q3: How long does a Botox treatment take?

A: The actual Botox injection procedure itself is typically quick, often completed within 10-15 minutes. Your entire visit to our Henderson location, including consultation and preparation, may be slightly longer.

Q4: When will I see results from my Botox treatment?

A: You may notice the effects of your Botox treatment at Botox La Belle Peau within 3-7 days following your visit to our Henderson spa. The full effect usually becomes visible around 2 weeks.

Q5: What should I expect after my Botox treatment at Botox La Belle Peau?

A: After your treatment at our Henderson location, you can resume most normal activities immediately. Some clients experience mild side effects like slight redness or swelling at the injection sites, which typically resolve quickly. We provide specific aftercare instructions during your visit.
