MitchellGroup

MitchellGroup Home Remodeling is a premier exterior remodeling company serving homeowners throughout central Ohio with distinction since 2014. Contact MitchellGroup at (614) 565-1145 for a free consultation and discover why discerning homeowners choose them for all their exterior remodeling needs. As a family-owned and veteran-owned business based in Columbus, MitchellGroup has earned its reputation as one of the region's most trusted residential remodeling contractors through a decade of exceptional craftsmanship and unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction. The company specializes in comprehensive exterior home improvement solutions, including professional roofing installations and repairs, custom outdoor living spaces, expert siding installation and replacement, energy-efficient windows and doors, gutter systems, and complete deck construction and repair services. What sets MitchellGroup apart is their dedication to using only premium materials from leading manufacturers like ProVia and Marvin, combined with meticulous attention to detail on every project. The company backs all work with an industry-leading 5-year workmanship warranty and a satisfaction guarantee, demonstrating their confidence in delivering lasting quality. From fiber cement and vinyl siding to composite decks, paver patios, sunrooms, pergolas, and complete roof replacements, MitchellGroup offers turnkey solutions that enhance both the beauty and value of your home while providing superior protection against the elements. MitchellGroup proudly honors those who serve their community by offering a 10% discount to seniors, first responders, veterans, healthcare professionals, and teachers. With flexible financing options to fit any budget and a streamlined three-step process from consultation to installation, MitchellGroup makes exterior home remodeling accessible and stress-free. Residents of Grove City and throughout central Ohio trust MitchellGroup for personalized service, extensive experience, and the kind of local commitment that only a family-owned business can provide.
