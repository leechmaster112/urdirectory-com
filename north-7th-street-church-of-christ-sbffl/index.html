North 7th Street Church of Christ

We warmly invite you to join us in worship, fellowship, and spiritual growth at the North 7th Street Church of Christ, a welcoming Christian congregation in the heart of Harlingen. Our hearts and doors are open to all who seek to deepen their faith, grow closer to God, and study the Bible. As a vibrant community dedicated to New Testament restoration, we emulate the purity, unity, and devotion of the early church. Our mission is to foster a sacred space for worship services, Bible study, and Christian fellowship, where believers can connect meaningfully with God’s Word and one another. Join us for Sunday worship, prayer, and community outreach as we strive to live out the teachings of Jesus Christ and build a faithful, loving church family committed to spiritual growth and service.

Location

The North 7th Street Church of Christ warmly welcomes you to join us in our community at 2205 N 7th St, Harlingen, TX 78550. We are a congregation dedicated to living out our faith through meaningful worship, authentic fellowship, and dedicated spiritual growth. Located in the heart of Harlingen, our church provides a supportive environment for individuals seeking to deepen their relationship with God and connect with others who share this journey.

Our Worship Experience

At North 7th Street Church of Christ, our worship services are designed to be engaging and reverent. We gather regularly to sing hymns and worship songs, pray, listen to the Word of God proclaimed through the teaching of our pastors, and respond in faith and obedience. Our services aim to create a space where the Holy Spirit can work powerfully, blessing our congregation and those who visit us. We believe that corporate worship is a vital expression of our faith and a means through which we draw closer to God.

Fellowship and Community

Beyond the corporate worship service, we place a strong emphasis on fellowship. Getting to know one another, supporting each other, and sharing life together are essential parts of the Christian experience at North 7th Street Church of Christ. We encourage you to participate in our various fellowship opportunities, which may include prayer meetings, small group gatherings, community outreach initiatives within Harlingen, and social events hosted at our location on N 7th Street. These gatherings help build the body of Christ locally, fostering deep relationships and mutual encouragement.

Spiritual Growth and Discipleship

We are committed to helping each member of our congregation grow in their understanding of God's Word and develop a Christ-like character. Our teaching ministry focuses on the Bible, covering both foundational doctrines and practical applications for daily life. We offer various opportunities for spiritual growth, including regular Sunday school classes, Bible studies, and discipleship programs tailored to different age groups and life stages, all conducted at our Harlingen facility. We believe in equipping believers for service and encouraging them to live out their faith actively in their homes, workplaces, and community on Seventh Street.

Connecting with Us

Whether you are a lifelong member of the Church of Christ, new to the faith, or simply curious about joining us for a service, you are invited to experience the welcoming atmosphere at North 7th Street Church of Christ. Our doors are open to everyone. You can learn more about our beliefs and schedule by visiting our official website, https://harlingenchurch.com/, or contacting us directly at (956) 320-7755. We look forward to the opportunity to welcome you to our church family right here in Harlingen.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What days and times are services held at North 7th Street Church of Christ?

Answer: Our regular worship services are held every Sunday morning at 10:30 AM right here at our location on North 7th Street in Harlingen.

2. Is the North 7th Street facility accessible for people with disabilities?

Answer: We are committed to accessibility for all who wish to join us. Please call us at (956) 320-7755 to discuss specific accessibility needs so we can ensure a comfortable experience for you at our Harlingen church.

3. Are children welcome during services at 2205 N 7th St?

Answer: Absolutely! Children are a vital part of our congregation. We provide a nurturing environment for children of all ages during our Sunday worship services at our North 7th Street location.

4. What should visitors bring or wear to a service at the Harlingen church?

Answer: We simply ask that you come as you are. There is no specific dress code. Please bring an open heart and a willingness to worship at our church on N 7th Street.

5. How can I learn more about the Church of Christ beliefs taught at North 7th Street?

Answer: You can learn more about our beliefs by attending a service at our Harlingen location or by visiting our website, https://harlingenchurch.com/. You can also call us at (956) 320-7755 to speak with a member of our congregation.
