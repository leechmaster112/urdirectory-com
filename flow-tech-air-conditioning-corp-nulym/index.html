Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What types of AC systems do you install?

A1: We install a variety of AC systems suitable for Miami homes and businesses, including central air conditioning units, ductless mini-split systems, and specialized commercial-grade equipment. We recommend consulting with us to determine the best fit for your specific property.

Q2: How long does an AC repair typically take?

A2: The duration of an AC repair depends on the specific issue. Our experienced technicians work efficiently to diagnose and fix problems promptly. In many cases, repairs can be completed within a few hours, but we always provide you with a clear timeline.

Q3: Do you offer emergency AC repair services?

A3: Yes, we understand that air conditioning problems can occur unexpectedly, especially during Miami's hot season. Our emergency service ensures we respond quickly to urgent AC needs at your location.

Q4: How often should I schedule AC maintenance?

A4: We generally recommend scheduling professional AC maintenance at least once a year, preferably before the hottest months begin. Regular maintenance ensures your system operates efficiently and helps prevent costly breakdowns.

Q5: What is the average lifespan of an AC unit?

A5: With proper maintenance and care, a well-maintained residential AC unit can typically last between 10 to 15 years. Commercial units may have different expectations. Our maintenance plans are designed to help maximize the lifespan of your investment.
