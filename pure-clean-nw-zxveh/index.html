Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How often should I get my roof professionally cleaned?

A: The frequency can depend on several factors unique to your Portland property, such as the type of roofing material, the local weather exposure (including rain and humidity levels), and the presence of trees nearby. Generally, most homeowners benefit from a professional roof cleaning every 2-5 years. Our team can inspect your roof and recommend the best cleaning schedule during your consultation at our Portland location.

Q2: What cleaning methods do you use for roof cleaning?

A: At Pure Clean NW, we primarily use specialized low-pressure washing systems designed specifically for roofs. This method effectively removes moss, algae, and debris without damaging the shingles or tiles. We also utilize environmentally friendly cleaning solutions that are safe for your home and the surrounding area. Our approach is gentle yet powerful, ensuring thorough cleaning without compromising your roof's integrity.

Q3: Do I need to be home during the roof cleaning process?

A: While it's not strictly necessary for you to be home during the actual cleaning process (which takes place on your roof), we recommend being available for a brief pre-inspection and post-cleaning walk-around. This allows us to confirm the scope of work and show you the results. You can reach us at our Portland location phone number if you have any questions during the process.

Q4: How long does a typical roof cleaning take?

A: The duration of a roof cleaning project depends on the size of your roof and the extent of the cleaning required. On average, a standard residential roof cleaning can take anywhere from 2 to 4 hours. Our team will provide you with a more accurate estimate after inspecting your roof during your initial visit to our Portland service location.

Q5: Do you provide attic ventilation cleaning services?

A: Yes, as part of our commitment to comprehensive home care, we offer attic ventilation cleaning and maintenance services from our Portland location. This includes clearing debris from vents and ensuring proper airflow to protect your roof and prevent moisture buildup in the attic space.
