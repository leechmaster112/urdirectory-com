Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a good Annapolis accident attorney?

A good Annapolis accident attorney possesses strong negotiation skills, in-depth knowledge of Maryland personal injury laws, a history of successful case resolutions, excellent communication with clients, and a commitment to advocating tirelessly for their clients' best interests.

How much does an Annapolis accident attorney cost?

Most Annapolis accident attorneys work on a contingency fee basis, meaning they only get paid if you win your case. Their fee is a percentage of the settlement or award, typically between 33% and 40%. It's crucial to discuss fee structures upfront.

When should I hire an Annapolis accident attorney?

You should consider hiring an Annapolis accident attorney as soon as possible after an accident, especially if you have sustained injuries, if fault is unclear, or if the other party's insurance company is being difficult. Early intervention can be critical.

What types of cases does an Annapolis accident attorney handle?

An Annapolis accident attorney typically handles cases involving motor vehicle accidents (cars, trucks, motorcycles), slip and fall incidents, premises liability, and other incidents resulting in personal injury due to negligence.

What is the statute of limitations for personal injury claims in Annapolis, Maryland?

In Maryland, the general statute of limitations for personal injury claims is three years from the date of the injury. However, there can be exceptions, so it's always best to consult with an experienced attorney.

Can I find the best Annapolis accident attorney without a referral?

Yes, you can find the best Annapolis accident attorney through online research, reading client reviews, checking bar association listings, and looking for attorneys with specialized experience in personal injury law.

What information should I gather before contacting an Annapolis accident attorney?

Gather all accident reports, photos of the scene and injuries, medical records and bills, insurance information, and any contact details of witnesses or the at-fault party.

What if I was partially at fault for the accident in Annapolis?

Maryland follows a "contributory negligence" rule, which can be complex. If you are found to be even 1% at fault, you may be barred from recovering damages. This is where a skilled Annapolis accident attorney is vital to protect your rights.

How long does it take to settle an accident case with an Annapolis accident attorney?

The timeline varies greatly depending on the complexity of the case, the severity of injuries, and the willingness of insurance companies to negotiate. Some cases settle in months, while others can take years.

Why is it important to choose a local Annapolis accident attorney?

A local Annapolis accident attorney is familiar with the local courts, judges, and legal landscape in Maryland, which can be advantageous. They also have established relationships within the community.
