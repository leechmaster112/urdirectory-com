FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions About Tutoring

Here are answers to some of the most common questions about selecting a tutor:

What makes a good tutor?

A good tutor possesses subject matter expertise, strong communication skills, patience, and the ability to adapt their teaching methods to suit individual students' needs. They should also be able to build a rapport and create a supportive learning environment.

How do I find a good tutor near me?

You can search online tutoring platforms, ask for recommendations from schools and other parents, and consider tutoring agencies. Ensure the tutor's qualifications, experience, and teaching style align with your child's needs.

What subjects do tutors typically provide help with?

Tutors provide help with a wide range of subjects, including math, science, English, history, and foreign languages. Most tutors specialize in one or two subjects. Some also tutor at the university level.

What's the difference between online and in-person tutoring?

Online tutoring offers flexibility and convenience, with sessions often available from anywhere. In-person tutoring offers a more personal connection and direct interaction. The best choice depends on your preference and access.

How much does a tutor cost in Australia?

Tutoring rates in Australia vary widely depending on the tutor's experience, qualifications, and the subject taught. Expect to pay anywhere from $40 to $150+ per hour.

How can I make sure my child is getting the most out of tutoring?

Communicate with the tutor regularly, stay involved in your child's learning, and make sure the tutor is providing the right kind of support—for example, not just doing homework for them.

How long should my child have tutoring for?

The duration of tutoring depends on the student’s needs. Some students benefit from short-term help to get through a specific topic, while others may need long-term support. Discuss this with the tutor and student.

What questions should I ask a potential tutor?

Ask about their experience, qualifications, teaching approach, and how they assess student progress. Involve your child in the interview process to see if they feel comfortable with the tutor.

What qualifications should a tutor have?

A good tutor will hold relevant qualifications, such as a degree in their subject area. But, they should also have previous teaching or tutoring experience and excellent communication skills. Check your tutor for a current Working With Children Check.
