Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What are the common signs of concrete damage in Montreal?

Common signs include cracking (from hairline to large fissures), spalling (surface flaking), scaling (loss of surface mortar), heaving or settlement, discoloration, and exposed rebar. Freeze-thaw cycles and de-icing salts are significant contributors in Montreal.

How much does concrete repair cost in Montreal?

The cost of concrete repair in Montreal can vary widely based on the extent of the damage, the type of repair needed, and the contractor's rates. Simple crack sealing might cost a few hundred dollars, while major structural repairs or resurfacing can range from thousands to tens of thousands of dollars.

What is the best concrete repair method for cracks?

For small, non-structural cracks, epoxy or polyurethane injection is often effective. For larger cracks or spalling, removing damaged material and patching with a compatible concrete or epoxy mortar is common. The best method depends on the crack's width, depth, and cause.

When should I hire a professional concrete repair contractor?

You should hire a professional when concrete damage affects the structural integrity of your property (e.g., foundation cracks), when repairs are extensive or complex, or when you need assurance of a durable, long-lasting fix, especially concerning Montreal’s climate.

Can concrete repair add value to my property in Montreal?

Yes, professional concrete repair significantly enhances curb appeal and structural soundness, which are key factors in property value. Addressing issues like cracking and spalling prevents further deterioration and costly future repairs.

What should I look for in a concrete repair contractor?

Look for experience, proper licensing and insurance, good customer reviews, a clear understanding of your specific problem, transparent pricing, and a portfolio of similar successful projects. Specialization in local conditions, like Montreal's weather patterns, is a plus.

How long do concrete repairs typically last?

The lifespan of concrete repairs varies greatly depending on the quality of the work, the materials used, and the ongoing maintenance and environmental conditions. High-quality repairs from reputable contractors like Les Services de Béton Universel Ltée can last for many years, often matching or exceeding the lifespan of the original concrete.

What is the difference between concrete patching and resurfacing?

Patching involves filling specific damaged areas or cracks. Resurfacing involves applying a new layer of concrete or a cementitious overlay over an existing concrete surface to restore its appearance and protect it from minor damage.

Are there DIY concrete repair options in Montreal?

For very minor issues like small hairline cracks, some DIY products exist. However, for most concrete repairs, especially those involving structural integrity or exposure to Montreal’s harsh weather, professional services are recommended for durability and effectiveness.

How does Montreal's climate affect concrete?

Montreal's climate with its significant freeze-thaw cycles, heavy snowfall, and use of de-icing salts puts immense stress on concrete. Water seeps into cracks, freezes, expands, and widens them. Repeated cycles cause spalling and scaling. De-icing salts can also accelerate surface degradation.
