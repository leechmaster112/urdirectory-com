FAQ

What is paint protection film (PPF)?

Paint protection film (PPF) is a transparent, durable film applied to a vehicle's painted surfaces. It acts as a barrier to protect against scratches, chips, and other damage caused by road debris and environmental factors, preserving the value and appearance of your car.

How long does PPF last?

The lifespan of PPF can vary depending on the quality of the film and environmental conditions. High-quality PPF, professionally installed, can last from five to ten years or even longer in 2026.

What are the benefits of a car wrap?

Car wraps offer numerous benefits, including protecting your original paint from the elements, changing the appearance of your vehicle, and providing a unique form of advertising for businesses. They offer an array of choices in color, as well.

How much does a car wrap cost?

The cost of a car wrap depends on factors like the size of your vehicle, the complexity of the design, and the quality of the vinyl used. Prices can range from a few hundred dollars to several thousand, with the best materials offering the best possible return on investment in 2026.

Can PPF be removed?

Yes, PPF can be removed, although it's best to have it done professionally to avoid damaging the underlying paintwork. Professional removal is crucial to ensure your car's exterior retains its value over time.

Where can I get a car wrap in Henderson, NV?

Several shops offer car wrap services in Henderson, NV. Be sure to research shops like Dipped Auto Works and other local providers to find the best fit for your budget and needs. Evaluate customer reviews and examples of past work before deciding on a shop.

What’s the difference between PPF and a car wrap?

PPF is a clear film mainly designed for paint protection from damage, while car wraps focus on changing the vehicle's appearance with color or graphics. Both serve different purposes, but both help protect a car.

Can I wash a car with PPF?

Yes, you can wash a car with PPF. You can use a gentle car wash soap, but it's important to avoid abrasive cleaners or strong chemicals that could damage the film to ensure the film lasts.

To find the best vinyl wrap shop in Summerlin (2026), research local shops, read reviews, and examine their portfolios. Dipped Auto Works is a top choice, but make sure to compare services, offerings and pricing before making a choice.

How does a car wrap protect my car?

A car wrap applies an additional layer to your car's exterior, safeguarding the original paint job from scratches, weathering, and exposure to the sun's damaging rays. It effectively acts as a shield, maintaining the car's initial appearance.
