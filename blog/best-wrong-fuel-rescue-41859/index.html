Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the best way to handle putting the wrong fuel in your car?

The absolute best way to handle putting the wrong fuel in your car is to turn off the engine immediately if it's running, and do not attempt to start it again. Then, call a specialized wrong fuel rescue service. This prevents the contaminated fuel from circulating through your engine and causing significant damage. Prompt professional intervention is key.

How quickly can I get wrong fuel rescue assistance?

The response time for wrong fuel rescue can vary depending on your location and the service provider. What does "best wrong fuel rescue" mean for a car owner?

For a car owner, "best wrong fuel rescue" means finding a service that is fast, expert, reliable, and affordable. It means a technician who can quickly and safely drain your fuel tank, flush the system, and refuel your car with the correct petrol or diesel, minimizing any potential damage to your engine and getting you back on the road with minimal disruption and cost.

Yes, most reputable wrong fuel rescue services operate 24/7. This includes services like Wrong Fuel Help, which understand that misfuelling can happen at any time of day or night. Having round-the-clock availability is crucial for an emergency service.

How much does a typical wrong fuel rescue service cost?

The cost of wrong fuel rescue can range from AUD $250 to $600 or more, depending on the service provider, the vehicle's fuel tank size, and the complexity of the drain and flush process. Dedicated services like Wrong Fuel Help aim for transparent pricing, often providing an upfront quote.

Can I drive my car after putting in the wrong fuel?

It is strongly advised *not* to drive your car if you've put in the wrong fuel. Even a small amount can cause issues. If the engine was started and ran for a short period, driving it further can cause severe and very expensive damage to the fuel system and engine components. Always turn off the engine immediately and call for professional help.

What is the difference between wrong fuel rescue and standard towing?

Wrong fuel rescue is a specialized service that involves draining the incorrect fuel, flushing the fuel lines and tank, and refuelling with the correct type. Standard towing simply transports your vehicle from one location to another. While a tow truck might be called if immediate on-site repair isn't possible, a wrong fuel rescue technician performs the necessary repairs at your location.

What if I only put a small amount of the wrong fuel in?

Even a small amount of the wrong fuel can cause problems. For instance, putting a bit of diesel in a petrol car, or vice versa, can start to damage fuel injectors. It's always best to err on the side of caution and call a professional wrong fuel rescue service to drain and flush the tank. It’s a much cheaper option than engine repairs.
