What is the role of Clark County in issuing liquor licenses in Las Vegas?
Clark County plays a significant role in the issuance of liquor licenses for businesses located within its unincorporated areas, which includes many parts of the greater Las Vegas area outside the city limits. The Clark County Department of Business License is responsible for accepting applications, conducting background checks, facilitating public notices, and making recommendations on license approvals to the Board of County Commissioners. Consultants often work directly with this department.
