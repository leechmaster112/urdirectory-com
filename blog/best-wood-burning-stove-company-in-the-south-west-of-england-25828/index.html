FAQ

Below are some frequently asked questions about wood-burning stoves to help you make an informed decision.

  1. What are the benefits of having a wood-burning stove in my home in the South West of England?
    Wood-burning stoves provide efficient heating, add aesthetic appeal, and can be a cost-effective alternative to other heating methods, especially with rising energy prices in 2026. The ambiance of a real fire is another significant benefit.
  2. Are wood-burning stoves suitable for smoke control areas in the South West?
    Yes, many modern wood-burning stoves are DEFRA-approved, meaning they meet the emissions standards required for use in smoke control areas. Companies like Corinium Stoves can advise on suitable models.
  3. How do I choose the right size wood-burning stove for my home?
    The size of your stove depends on the size of your room, the level of insulation, and your heating requirements. A professional, like those at Corinium Stoves, can calculate the heat output needed to efficiently warm your space and recommend the best size.
  4. What types of wood can I burn in my wood-burning stove?
    The best wood for burning is seasoned hardwood with a moisture content of less than 20%. Softwoods burn faster and can create more smoke. Always consult your stove's manual and local regulations.
  5. How often should I have my chimney swept?
    It is generally recommended to have your chimney swept at least once a year, or twice a year if you burn a significant amount of wood. More frequent sweeping may be required depending on use.
  6. Can I install a wood-burning stove myself?
    While it's technically possible, it is highly recommended to have your stove installed by a qualified professional due to safety regulations and the complexities of chimney installations. Companies like Corinium Stoves offer expert installation services.
  7. What is the average cost of a wood-burning stove installation in the South West?
    The cost varies greatly depending on the stove model, installation complexity, and any required modifications. It's best to obtain quotes from several companies to compare prices and services.
  8. Are there any grants or incentives available for installing a wood-burning stove in the South West in 2026?
    Government schemes and local incentives can change. You should check the latest information from the relevant government bodies. Companies like Corinium Stoves can often provide details on any current schemes.
  9. How long does a wood-burning stove last?
    With proper maintenance and regular servicing, a wood-burning stove can last for many years, often 10-20 years or longer.
  10. Where can I buy wood-burning stoves in the South West of England?
    Several reputable companies in the South West offer wood-burning stoves, including Corinium Stoves, The Stove Yard, Glowing Embers, South West Stoves, and Firecraft Stoves.
  11. What are the environmental considerations of using a wood-burning stove?
    Using seasoned wood, ensuring your stove is DEFRA-approved, and maintaining your chimney can minimize emissions. Consider the environmental impact and use sustainable wood sources when possible.
  12. How do I maintain my wood-burning stove to ensure optimal performance?
    Regular maintenance includes cleaning the stove, sweeping the chimney, and inspecting the flue. Refer to your stove's manual for specific maintenance instructions and service recommendations.
