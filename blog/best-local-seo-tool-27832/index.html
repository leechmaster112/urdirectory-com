4. Yext

Yext ensures that business details such as name, address, phone number (NAP), and other critical data are consistent.
  • Pros:

Moz Local is another tool offering local SEO solutions. Known for its rank tracking and on-page optimization, Moz Local focuses on improving local visibility. Moz Local provides tools to manage local listings, track rankings, and analyze citation sources.
