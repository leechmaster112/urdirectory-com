Frequently Asked Questions

  1. What is a full body scan? A full body scan uses advanced imaging techniques to create detailed images of your internal organs and systems, helping to identify potential health issues before symptoms appear.
  2. What can a full body scan detect? Full body scans can detect a wide range of conditions, including early stages of cancer, heart disease, aneurysms, and other abnormalities.
  3. How accurate are full body scans? The accuracy of a full body scan depends on the technology used and the experience of the medical professionals interpreting the results. Advanced scans, can be very accurate, but it is always best to discuss the results with your doctor.
  4. Are full body scans safe? The radiation exposure from full body scans is generally considered low, but it is always wise to discuss the potential risks with your physician, particularly if you're concerned about radiation exposure.
  5. How much does a full body scan cost? The price of a full body scan can vary depending on the provider, the technology used, and the extent of the scan. Prices can range widely, so be sure to check the pricing of your scan.
  6. How do I prepare for a full body scan? Preparation guidelines vary depending on the type of scan. Specific instructions may include fasting for a certain period before the scan, depending upon the type of scan. You should always discuss the prep instructions for any scan with your doctor.
  7. How long does a full body scan take? The duration of a full body scan can vary, but generally, the scanning process takes between 30 and 60 minutes. The entire appointment including prep and consultation can take much longer.
  8. Who should get a full body scan? A full body scan can be beneficial for anyone seeking a proactive approach to their health, especially those with a family history of specific diseases or those experiencing symptoms.
  9. What are the different types of full body scans? Common types include MRI, CT scans, and PET scans, each using different technologies to produce detailed images of the body.
  10. Where can I get a full body scan? Full body scans are available at various imaging centers, hospitals, and specialized clinics. Research different clinics, and ask your doctor.
  11. How do I understand the results of a full body scan? After your scan, a radiologist or medical professional will review the images and provide a report. They will often discuss the results with you and explain any findings in detail.
  12. Is a full body scan covered by insurance? Coverage for full body scans can vary depending on your insurance provider and the reason for the scan. It’s best to verify coverage with your insurance company beforehand.
