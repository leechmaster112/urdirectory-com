Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What services do private investigators in Sydney offer?

Private investigators in Sydney offer a wide range of services. Some of the most common services include surveillance, background checks, infidelity investigations, missing person searches, corporate fraud investigations, and asset tracing.

How much does it cost to hire a private investigator in Sydney?

The cost of hiring a private investigator in Sydney can vary widely depending on the complexity of the case, the amount of time required, and the specific services needed. Rates are typically determined on an hourly basis, with additional fees for expenses such as travel and specialized equipment. It’s always best to obtain a detailed quote from the investigator.

Are private investigators in Sydney licensed?

Yes, all private investigators in Sydney are required to be licensed by the NSW Police Force.

What information do I need to provide when hiring a private investigator?

When you hire a private investigator, you’ll typically need to provide as much information as possible about the case, including the names of individuals involved, relevant dates and locations, and any existing evidence or background information you have. The more information you provide, the better equipped the investigator will be to conduct a thorough investigation.

How long does an investigation typically take?

The length of an investigation can vary significantly depending on the complexity of the case and the scope of the investigation. Simple background checks might be completed within a few days, while more complex cases involving surveillance or extensive research could take several weeks or even months.

Can a private investigator in Sydney access confidential information?

Private investigators in Sydney are limited in what confidential information they can access. They cannot, for example, illegally obtain phone records or financial information. They must operate within the bounds of the law, which dictates that they acquire information through legal and ethical means.

What types of cases can a private investigator handle?

Private investigators can handle a wide variety of cases, including infidelity investigations, missing persons, background checks, surveillance, corporate fraud, asset searches, and due diligence investigations.

How do I choose the right private investigator in Sydney?

Choosing the right private investigator involves considering their experience, reputation, areas of expertise, and pricing. Get a detailed quote, check their license, and make sure they have experience in your specific type of case. It's often helpful to read reviews and testimonials from previous clients to get an idea of their professionalism and the quality of their work.
