Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What should I do immediately after a car accident in Calgary?
A: Ensure everyone's safety, exchange information with the other driver, and report the accident to the police. Seek medical attention, even if you don't feel injured immediately.

Q: When should I contact a car accident lawyer?
A: Contact a lawyer as soon as possible after the accident. A lawyer can help you understand your rights and guide you through the claims process. It's often best to consult with a lawyer before speaking with insurance companies.

Q: How much does it cost to hire a car accident lawyer in 2026?
A: Many personal injury lawyers, including those at Preszler Injury Lawyers, work on a contingency fee basis. This means you only pay if they win your case, and their fee is a percentage of the settlement or court award.

Q: What types of damages can I claim after a car accident?
A: You can claim damages for medical expenses, lost wages, pain and suffering, and other accident-related expenses. The specific types of damages will vary based on the specifics of the accident and your injuries.

Q: How long do I have to file a car accident claim in Calgary?
A: In Alberta, there is a statute of limitations for personal injury claims. Typically, you have two years from the date of the accident to file a claim. It’s important to act quickly to avoid missing this deadline.

Q: What if the other driver was at fault in the accident?
A: If the other driver was at fault, you can file a claim against their insurance company. A lawyer can help you gather evidence, negotiate with the insurance company, and, if necessary, file a lawsuit.

Q: Can I still file a claim if I was partially at fault for the accident?
A: Yes. Alberta follows a system of "comparative negligence." This means you can still recover compensation even if you were partially at fault. The amount of compensation you receive will be reduced based on your percentage of fault. A lawyer can help you navigate this complex process.

Q: What information will I need to provide to a car accident lawyer?
A: You should gather information such as the police report, insurance information for all parties involved, medical records, documentation of lost wages, and any photos or videos of the accident scene. Your lawyer will guide you on what documents are needed.

Q: Where can I find the best car accident lawyers near me in Calgary?
A: The firms listed in this article, like Preszler Injury Lawyers, have offices in Calgary and are well-regarded for their expertise in car accident claims within the city.

Q: How do car accident lawyers handle negotiations with insurance companies?
A: Car accident lawyers have experience in negotiating with insurance companies. They handle all communications, gather evidence to support your claim, and fight for a fair settlement that covers your losses.

Q: What are the benefits of hiring a car accident lawyer?
A: A car accident lawyer can help you understand your rights, gather evidence, negotiate with insurance companies, and file a lawsuit if necessary. Lawyers can maximize the compensation you receive and relieve the stress of managing the legal process.

Q: How will a car accident lawyer help me in 2026?
A: A skilled lawyer is especially helpful in 2026 because they can deal with the specific challenges of claims and adapt to potential changes in laws and technologies. They can evaluate the value of your claim, negotiate aggressively and help you navigate the system efficiently.
