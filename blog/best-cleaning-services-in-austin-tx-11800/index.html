Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What types of cleaning services are available in Austin, TX?

In Austin, TX, you can find various cleaning services, including recurring house cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, office cleaning, and specialized services like window cleaning and post-construction cleaning.

How much does it cost to hire a cleaning service in Austin?

The cost varies based on the size of your home or office, the type of service needed (e.g., standard cleaning vs. deep cleaning), and the frequency of service. Prices can range from $75 to $500 or more per cleaning session. Get a free quote to know the exact pricing.

Are the cleaning products used by Austin cleaning services eco-friendly?

Many cleaning services in Austin offer eco-friendly options, using non-toxic and environmentally friendly cleaning products. Be sure to ask about this when you request a quote.

How do I choose the best cleaning service for my needs?

Consider your specific needs (residential vs. commercial, recurring vs. one-time), read reviews, compare prices, and check the services offered to find a cleaning service that aligns with your requirements.

What does a typical house cleaning service include?

Typical house cleaning services usually include dusting, vacuuming, mopping floors, cleaning bathrooms and kitchens, wiping surfaces, and emptying trash. Deep cleaning services may include more detailed tasks like cleaning appliances or scrubbing baseboards.

Do I need to be home when the cleaning service comes?

No, you do not always need to be home. Many cleaning services, including some of those listed above, allow you to provide access via a key or a lockbox. This is something you should confirm during the booking process.

How far in advance should I book a cleaning service?

It's best to book your cleaning service as early as possible, especially if you have specific dates in mind. Popular services and peak seasons can fill up quickly. Booking a few weeks in advance is generally a good idea.

What areas of Austin does Austin Skyline Cleaning Services cover?

Can I customize a cleaning plan with Austin cleaning services?

How are cleaning services in Austin, TX insured?

Most reputable cleaning services will carry both liability insurance and worker's compensation insurance to protect both you and their employees. Always confirm the insured status when selecting a service.

Do cleaning services in Austin offer move-in/move-out cleaning?

Yes, many cleaning services in Austin provide move-in/move-out cleaning services, which are designed to deep clean a property before new residents move in or after previous residents have vacated it.

What should I do if I am not satisfied with the cleaning service?

If you're not satisfied with a cleaning service, contact the company immediately. Most reputable services, like Austin Skyline Cleaning Services and others featured, will either redo the cleaning or offer a partial refund to resolve the issue. If the initial communications don't resolve the matter, be sure to leave feedback or a review which can help guide changes and help other customers.
