Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What are dental implants?

Dental implants are artificial tooth roots, typically made of titanium, that are surgically placed into the jawbone to replace missing teeth. They provide a stable foundation for replacement teeth (crowns, bridges, or dentures).

2. Am I a suitable candidate for dental implants?

Suitability depends on several factors, including your overall health, the density of your jawbone, and your commitment to good oral hygiene. A consultation with a dentist is necessary to determine if you're a good fit.

3. How long does the dental implant procedure take?

The entire process, from initial consultation to the final placement of the restoration, can take several months. This timeframe varies depending on the need for bone grafting, the healing process, and the complexity of the case.

4. How painful is getting a dental implant?

Most patients experience minimal discomfort during and after the procedure due to the use of local anesthesia and pain management techniques. Any post-operative discomfort can typically be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.

5. What is the success rate of dental implants?

Dental implants have a high success rate, typically around 95% or higher. However, success depends on proper placement, patient health, and diligent oral hygiene practices.

6. How do I care for my dental implants?

Maintaining good oral hygiene is essential. This includes regular brushing, flossing, and check-ups with your dentist. Following a healthy diet and avoiding habits like smoking can also prolong the life of your implants.

7. What is the cost of dental implants in Langley?

The cost varies depending on several factors, including the type of implant, the number of teeth being replaced, and the complexity of the procedure. It's best to consult with a dental professional for a personalized estimate.

8. Can I get dental implants if I have gum disease?

Gum disease (periodontitis) can negatively impact the success of dental implants. Addressing the gum disease prior to implant placement is often necessary to ensure the best possible outcome.

9. Where can I find the best dental implants in Langley?

Research and consultation with dental practices such as Saidi Dental Group is the best way to determine the best option for you. Consider factors such as experience, technology, and patient reviews to make an informed decision.

10. What types of dental implants are available?

Several types of dental implants are available, which typically fall into different sizes and materials. Your dentist can help determine the best one for your particular needs.

11. How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and maintenance, dental implants can last a lifetime. Regular check-ups with your dentist, along with brushing and flossing, will help support longevity.

12. Are there any risks associated with dental implants?

As with any surgical procedure, there are potential risks, like infection, nerve damage, or implant failure. However, these risks are minimal when implants are placed by an experienced and qualified dentist.
