What's the difference between a dealership and an independent auto body shop near me?
Dealerships often specialize in specific makes and use OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) parts, which can be more expensive. How long does auto body repair typically take at a shop near me?

Can I trust reviews for "best auto body shop near me" online?
