FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions About Contractor Insurance (2026)

What does contractor insurance cover?

Contractor insurance typically covers a wide range of potential risks, including property damage, bodily injury, and financial losses resulting from accidents, errors, or negligence. This can include general liability, workers' compensation, commercial auto, and professional liability.

Do I need contractor insurance if I'm a sole proprietor?

Yes, even if you are a sole proprietor, you likely need contractor insurance. General liability insurance can protect you from claims related to property damage or bodily injury. Workers' compensation insurance is essential if you have employees. Commercial auto is vital if you use vehicles for business purposes.

What is general liability insurance for contractors?

General liability insurance protects your business from financial losses if someone is injured or their property is damaged due to your work. This is a core part of protecting your business from the unexpected financial risks of accidents on a job site.

How much does contractor insurance cost?

The cost of contractor insurance varies depending on several factors, including the type of work you do, your business size, your location, your claims history, and your desired coverage limits. It is always wise to get several quotes.

What is the difference between general liability and professional liability insurance?

General liability insurance protects against claims of bodily injury or property damage. Professional liability, also known as errors and omissions (E&O) insurance, protects against claims of negligence or mistakes in your professional work or services.

What is workers' compensation insurance?

Workers' compensation insurance covers medical expenses, lost wages, and rehabilitation costs for employees injured on the job. It is required by law in most states if you have employees.

What does commercial auto insurance cover?

Commercial auto insurance provides coverage for vehicles used for business purposes. This includes liability coverage for accidents, as well as collision and comprehensive coverage for vehicle damage.

What factors affect the price of contractor insurance?

Many factors influence insurance pricing. Location, type of work, your revenue, and your loss history play a large role. Risk factors, such as hazardous materials or practices, affect pricing as well.
