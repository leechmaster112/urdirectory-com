Frequently Asked Questions About Best TMS Therapy in Raleigh (2026)

What is TMS therapy and how does it work for conditions like depression?

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. It's primarily used for depression when other treatments haven't been effective. The magnetic pulses target specific areas of the brain that regulate mood, helping to improve communication and function in those regions, thereby alleviating depressive symptoms.

How long does a typical TMS therapy session last in Raleigh?

A standard TMS therapy session typically lasts between 20 to 40 minutes, depending on the specific protocol being used. Patients usually undergo sessions five days a week for approximately four to six weeks.

Is TMS therapy covered by insurance in Raleigh, NC?

Many insurance providers, including major carriers, do offer coverage for TMS therapy, especially for treatment-resistant depression. However, coverage can vary based on your specific plan and the clinic's billing practices. It's always recommended to check directly with your insurance provider and the TMS clinic you are considering, such as TMS Raleigh, to understand your benefits.

What are the common side effects of TMS treatment in the Raleigh area?

TMS is generally well-tolerated. The most common side effects are mild to moderate scalp discomfort or headache at the treatment site, particularly during the first week of treatment. These usually subside as treatment continues. Serious side effects, such as seizures, are extremely rare.

How do I choose the best TMS therapy provider in Raleigh for my needs?

When selecting a TMS provider, consider factors like the clinic's specialization in TMS, the experience of their staff, the technology they use, their patient-centered approach, and the clarity of their treatment plans and billing. Reading patient testimonials and having an initial consultation can also help inform your decision. For a highly specialized experience, consider clinics like TMS Raleigh.

Can TMS therapy treat conditions other than depression in Raleigh?

While primarily FDA-cleared for major depressive disorder, TMS therapy has also received FDA clearance for Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD) and smoking cessation. Research is ongoing for its potential use in treating other conditions like anxiety disorders, PTSD, and chronic pain, but treatment for these may be off-label or part of a clinical trial.

What is the success rate of TMS therapy for depression in 2026?

Success rates for TMS therapy for depression are encouraging. Clinical studies and real-world outcomes indicate that a significant percentage of patients achieve remission or a substantial reduction in their symptoms, especially those with treatment-resistant depression. Individual results can vary, but many experience lasting relief.

Are there any specific requirements or contraindications for TMS therapy?

Eligibility for TMS therapy is determined by a thorough medical and psychiatric evaluation. Generally, individuals with certain metal implants in the head (other than dental fillings), pacemakers, or a history of seizures may not be suitable candidates. Your provider will discuss all contraindications during your consultation.

How many TMS sessions will I need to see results in Raleigh?

The standard course of TMS treatment typically involves daily sessions (Monday through Friday) for about 4 to 6 weeks. Some individuals may start to feel improvements earlier, while for others, the full benefits become apparent towards the end of the treatment course or even a few weeks after completing it.
