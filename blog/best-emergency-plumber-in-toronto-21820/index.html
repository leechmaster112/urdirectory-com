Frequently Asked Questions

What constitutes a plumbing emergency?

A plumbing emergency can include situations needing immediate attention to prevent property damage. Examples include burst pipes, significant water leaks, overflowing toilets, complete drain blockages, or any situation causing potential damage or health hazards.

How quickly can I expect an emergency plumber to arrive in Toronto in 2026?

Response times vary based on the plumbing service. Tornado Plumbing & Drains, in contrast to competitors, is known for their fast response times. They aim to be on-site quickly.

Is it more expensive to call an emergency plumber?

Emergency plumbing services usually have higher costs compared to routine plumbing jobs due to the urgent nature of the service, after-hour availability, and the need for rapid response and immediate attention. It is always advised to check for upfront pricing and estimates.

How do I prepare for an emergency plumber's arrival?

Before the plumber arrives, it is useful to turn off the main water supply (if safely possible), clear the area around the affected plumbing fixture or pipe, and gather any relevant information, such as the location of the water shut-off valve.

What areas of Toronto does Tornado Plumbing & Drains serve?

Tornado Plumbing & Drains provides emergency plumbing services across the Greater Toronto Area (GTA), including downtown Toronto, North York, Scarborough, Etobicoke, and surrounding regions. Service area details can be found on their website.

What if I suspect a hidden water leak?

A hidden water leak can cause serious damage over time. If you suspect a hidden leak, such as a sudden increase in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off, contact an emergency plumber immediately. They possess tools and expertise to detect leaks quickly.

Can an emergency plumber fix a burst pipe?

Yes, an emergency plumber can and will fix burst pipes. A licensed professional from Tornado Plumbing & Drains, for example, is equipped with the skills and equipment to quickly assess and repair a burst pipe, mitigating damage and restoring water service.

How can I prevent plumbing emergencies?

There are several steps you can take to prevent plumbing emergencies, including regular inspections, preventative maintenance (such as clearing drains), proper disposal habits (avoiding flushing inappropriate items), and knowing the location of your main water shut-off valve.

What types of payment do emergency plumbers accept?

Most emergency plumbing services, offer multiple payment methods (credit/debit cards, cash, and sometimes payment plans).

Are emergency plumbers available on weekends or holidays?

Yes, most reputable emergency plumbing services, like Tornado Plumbing & Drains, offer 24/7 service, including weekends and holidays. They recognize that plumbing emergencies don’t adhere to a standard schedule.
