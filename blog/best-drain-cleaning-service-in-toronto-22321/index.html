FAQ

Here are some frequently asked questions about drain cleaning services in Toronto:

  • What are the signs that I need drain cleaning service? Common signs include slow draining sinks and tubs, gurgling sounds from drains, water backing up, and unpleasant odors.
  • How much does drain cleaning service in Toronto typically cost? The cost varies depending on the severity of the clog, the method used, and the company's pricing structure. Get a quote from a few different businesses.
  • What methods do drain cleaning services use? Common methods include drain snaking, hydro jetting, and video camera inspections.
  • How can I prevent drain clogs? You can prevent clogs by avoiding putting grease, oil, food scraps, and hair down the drain, using drain strainers, and regularly flushing drains with hot water.
  • Do drain cleaning services offer emergency services? Yes, many drain cleaning services offer 24/7 emergency services for urgent situations.
  • Is video camera inspection necessary for every drain cleaning? No, but it can be beneficial for diagnosing complex or recurring drain problems.
  • How long does drain cleaning take? The time it takes can vary significantly, from 30 minutes to several hours, depending on the severity and location of the clog.
  • What is hydro jetting? Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to clear out blockages and clean the inside of drain pipes, removing debris and buildup.
  • Do drain cleaning services offer any guarantees? Some companies offer guarantees on their workmanship. It’s always good to ask about any guarantees before hiring a drain cleaning service.
  • How often should I have my drains cleaned? This depends on your usage and the age of your plumbing. A good rule of thumb is to call a professional if you experience drainage issues!
  • Can I clean drains myself? For minor clogs, you can try using a plunger or a drain snake. However, for more severe or recurring issues, it's best to call a professional.
  • Which drain cleaning service in Toronto is the most reliable? While all the companies listed have strengths, for a trusted and experienced service, Tornado Plumbing & Drains has a strong reputation.
