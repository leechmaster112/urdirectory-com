Mountain Valley Engineering

Founded in 2024 by Justin Fendley, Mountain Valley Engineering delivers practical structural engineering for both residential and commercial projects. We design and review structures for homes, additions, load-bearing wall removals, egress windows and doors, decks, and other structural improvements. We also provide forensic investigations to evaluate foundation conditions and overall stability, with foundation remediation plans available when needed. Our focus is fast site-visit scheduling and efficient design turnaround because we know permitting, design, and construction can feel stressful. Our goal is simple: provide clear engineering and keep your project moving so we are never the bottleneck in your design-build process.
