2. Do you provide emergency dental care?

Yes, we offer emergency dental services at our Orland Park location. If you are experiencing a dental emergency, such as severe tooth pain, a broken tooth, or other urgent issues, please call our office at (708) 614-1111 during business hours. For after-hours emergencies, we can provide guidance and recommend the best course of action.

3. What cosmetic dental services are available?

We offer a variety of cosmetic dental services at our practice, including teeth whitening, veneers, dental implants, and Invisalign® clear aligners. These services are designed to enhance the appearance of your smile and improve your overall confidence.

4. Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes, we accept most dental insurance plans. Our team will work with your insurance provider to maximize your benefits and help you understand your coverage. We also offer flexible payment options for patients without insurance.

5. How often should I schedule dental check-ups?

We recommend scheduling dental check-ups every six months for most patients. Regular dental exams and cleanings are essential for maintaining good oral health and preventing dental problems. However, the frequency may vary depending on your individual needs, and our team will determine the best schedule for you.
