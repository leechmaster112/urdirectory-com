Advanced TMS Treatment in Raleigh

Our Raleigh practice is uniquely equipped to offer Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) therapy. TMS is a non-invasive treatment option for individuals struggling with treatment-resistant depression (TRD). Unlike traditional antidepressant medications, TMS does not require ingesting pills. Instead, it uses focused magnetic pulses to gently stimulate specific areas of the brain involved in mood regulation, which may have become underactive in depression. This targeted approach can lead to significant improvements in mood and symptoms for many patients seeking alternatives or additions to their depression treatment plan.

How TMS Treatment Works

The TMS procedure is typically performed on an outpatient basis at our Raleigh clinic. Before the session, our team will explain the process and ensure you are comfortable. During the treatment, you will sit comfortably while our Raleigh-based psychiatrist or a trained technician operates the TMS device. The device generates a magnetic field through your skull to target specific brain regions. You will hear a clicking sound and may feel a tapping sensation on your head during the pulses. Most patients find the procedure well-tolerated and comfortable. A typical course of TMS treatment involves daily sessions, usually lasting about 20-40 minutes, over a period of several weeks.

MedPsych Integrated - Raleigh FAQs

1. What is TMS therapy, and how is it different from traditional antidepressant medication?

TMS stands for Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation. It's a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain to improve symptoms of depression. Unlike antidepressant medications taken orally, TMS is not ingested and has no risk of systemic side effects like weight gain or sexual dysfunction. It's often used when medication hasn't been effective or when patients prefer a non-pharmacological approach.

2. Is TMS treatment effective for depression?

Research shows TMS can be highly effective for many individuals with depression, particularly those who haven't responded well to previous treatments. It's FDA-approved for treating major depressive disorder, including treatment-resistant depression. Effectiveness can vary, and a consultation at our Raleigh practice is necessary to determine if TMS is appropriate for you.

3. How long does a typical TMS session last?

A standard TMS session at our Raleigh location typically lasts between 20 to 40 minutes. Most patients find they can return to their normal activities immediately after a session, including work or other daily responsibilities.

4. How many TMS sessions are needed for treatment?

A typical course of TMS treatment involves daily sessions, Monday through Friday, for about 4 to 6 weeks. The exact number of sessions needed can vary based on individual factors determined during your evaluation at our Raleigh practice.

5. Does TMS treatment require anesthesia or sedation?

No, TMS treatment is typically performed while you are awake and alert. You will be asked to keep your eyes open and watch a screen during the procedure. Most patients report feeling only a tapping sensation on their head and are able to remain comfortably seated throughout the session without needing any sedation.
