Jacksonville PetSafe Bark Park

The Jacksonville PetSafe Bark Park is a fully fenced, off-leash dog park on roughly 7 acres along the shore of Mauvaisterre Lake in Jacksonville, Illinois. Built with a $100,000 PetSafe Bark for Your Park prize, it reflects the community's commitment to pet wellness and responsible dog ownership. Hours, photos, and directions are on its Doggie Park Near Me listing.

Frequently Asked Questions:

Q: Is there a separate area for smaller dogs?

A: Yes, the Jacksonville PetSafe Bark Park generally provides designated areas to accommodate dogs of different sizes, ensuring a comfortable experience for both our smaller and larger canine visitors.

Q: What kind of fencing can I expect at the park?

A: The entire 7-acre park is fully fenced to ensure a secure off-leash environment for all dogs. The fencing is designed to keep pets safely contained within the park grounds.

Q: Are there water sources available for dogs?

A: While we encourage bringing your dog's favorite portable water bowl, you'll find that the park is equipped with amenities to help keep your pets hydrated during their playtime.

Q: What is the general layout of the park?

A: The Jacksonville PetSafe Bark Park offers approximately 7 acres of open space for dogs to run and play. The expansive layout allows for plenty of room for fetching, exploring, and general canine exertion.

Q: Do I need to be present with my dog at all times?

A: Yes, for the safety and well-being of all dogs and visitors, owners are required to supervise their pets at all times while within the off-leash park area.
