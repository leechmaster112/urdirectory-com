Historic German House

The Historic German House is a historic all-inclusive wedding and event venue located in Rochester, NY's South Wedge neighborhood. Set within a beautifully restored 1908 building, the venue features an elegant ballroom, balcony theater seating, and a private bridal suite. The Historic German House hosts weddings, corporate events, galas, mitzvahs, and social celebrations for up to 200 guests, with seating for up to 160. Couples and event hosts enjoy full-service catering and bar service by Partyman Catering, along with professional day-of coordination. Conveniently located just minutes from downtown Rochester, The Historic German House provides a distinctive setting for memorable events.
