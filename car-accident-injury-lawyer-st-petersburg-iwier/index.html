FAQs about Our St. Petersburg Car Accident Injury Law Services

Q1: What types of car accident injuries do you handle in St. Petersburg?

We handle a wide range of injuries resulting from car accidents in St. Petersburg, including whiplash, broken bones, spinal cord injuries, traumatic brain injuries (TBI), burns, and internal injuries.

Q2: How soon should I contact a lawyer after my St. Petersburg car accident?

It is crucial to contact an attorney as soon as possible after your accident in St. Petersburg. Time limits (statutes of limitations) apply to filing a personal injury claim.

Q3: What should I do immediately after a car accident in St. Petersburg?

Ensure safety first. If possible, move to a safe location. Check for injuries; seek medical attention immediately even if you feel fine. Report the accident to the police and exchange information with the other driver(s).

Q4: Do I have to pay anything upfront to hire your St. Petersburg car accident lawyer?

Our firm typically works on a contingency fee basis. This means you don't pay anything unless we win your case. Our fee is contingent upon securing a settlement or verdict in your favor.

Q5: Can you help me with my St. Petersburg car accident claim if I was partially at fault?

In Florida, which follows a modified comparative negligence rule, you may still be able to recover damages even if you were partially at fault, as long as your negligence is less than 50%. We will fight to maximize your recovery.
