Step into a world of enchantment and playful service at Maidreamin Shibuya Store, where delicious treats are served with a smile and a dash of magic.

What is the typical atmosphere like for a first-time visitor?

Expect a vibrant and welcoming atmosphere filled with cheerful greetings from our maids and a playful, energetic ambiance.

Can I expect any special performances during my visit?

Yes, our maids often perform lively and entertaining routines that are a signature part of the Maidreamin experience, adding a unique flair to your cafe visit.

We offer a delightful selection of cafe-style treats, including visually appealing parfaits, cute character-themed dishes, and refreshing beverages, all designed to be as enjoyable to look at as they are to eat.

Is it possible to interact with the maids?

Interaction is a key part of the experience; our maids are dedicated to making your visit fun and memorable through engaging conversations and service.

What is the best way to find the Shibuya Store?

Our store is located on the B1 floor at 30−1 Udagawacho, Shibuya, Tokyo, 〒150-0042, a easily accessible spot in the heart of Shibuya.
