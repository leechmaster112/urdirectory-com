Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What exactly is a Gold IRA?

A Gold IRA is a type of self-directed Individual Retirement Account that allows you to invest in physical precious metals, such as gold, silver, platinum, and palladium, alongside traditional assets like stocks and bonds. It's designed to provide diversification and potential hedge against inflation within your retirement portfolio.

Are physical gold investments allowed in IRAs?

Yes, the IRS permits certain precious metals to be held within a self-directed IRA. Physical gold, specifically in the form of certain bullion bars and coins meeting purity standards, can be included. However, there are specific rules regarding form, fineness, and storage in an IRS-approved depository.

How does the process of moving funds into a Gold IRA work?

The process typically involves setting up a self-directed IRA with a custodian that specializes in precious metals. You can then initiate a rollover or transfer from your existing retirement account (like a 401(k) or traditional IRA) directly to this custodian. The custodian handles the paperwork and ensures compliance, and you select the metals to be purchased and stored securely.

What are the benefits of diversifying with precious metals?

Diversifying with precious metals like gold offers several potential benefits. Gold is often seen as a safe-haven asset during economic turmoil, a hedge against inflation, and a potential store of value. Including it in a portfolio can reduce overall risk and provide a hedge against the volatility of traditional financial markets.

Where is the gold stored in a Gold IRA?

Gold held within a self-directed IRA must be stored in an IRS-approved depository. These are secure, insured facilities specifically designed for holding retirement assets like precious metals. We assist in selecting compliant storage options that meet all regulatory requirements.
