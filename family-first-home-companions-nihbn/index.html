Family First Home Companions

At Family First Home Companions, we are the Leading Home Care Agency In Long Island, NY, Proudly Serving Seniors Across all of Long Island & NY. Since 2005, we have prided ourselves on providing compassionate, non-medical in-home care that empowers seniors to age in place with dignity. Our bespoke home care solutions range from hourly companionship and meal preparation to specialized Alzheimer’s support and 24/7 live-in care. Unlike other agencies, we offer our unique CareMatch Plus program to guarantee a perfect caregiver fit, and we never require long-term contracts. Serving Nassau, Suffolk, Queens, Brooklyn, and Manhattan, our dedicated team is here to give your family peace of mind. Call us today for your free in-home care assessment!
