Q4: What should I do if my garage door opener isn't working?

If your garage door opener isn't working, first check if the circuit breaker has tripped or if the fuse needs replacing. Next, check the door's manual operation to see if it's properly balanced. Test the safety sensors (they should be aligned and not obstructed). Finally, check the batteries in your remote controls. If these steps don't resolve the issue, contact our Dallas garage door repair service.
