Frequently Asked Questions

1. What types of air conditioning systems do you install?

We specialize in installing a variety of AC systems suitable for Miami homes and businesses, including central air conditioning units, ductless mini-split systems, and specialized commercial-grade equipment.

2. How long does a typical AC repair take?

The duration of an AC repair visit depends on the specific issue. Our technicians aim to diagnose and resolve problems promptly, often completing standard repairs within a few hours.

3. Do you offer emergency AC services?

Yes, we understand AC failures can be disruptive, especially during Miami's hot seasons. We provide emergency repair services to get your system running quickly.

4. How often should I schedule AC maintenance?

We recommend scheduling professional AC maintenance at least once a year, typically before the summer heat intensifies, to ensure optimal performance and efficiency.

5. What is your process for AC installation?

Our installation process begins with a thorough assessment of your space, followed by recommending the most suitable system. We then handle the installation with precision and care, ensuring proper sizing and configuration for maximum efficiency and comfort.
