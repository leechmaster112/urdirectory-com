Vascular medicine and surgery focus on the body's blood vessels outside of the heart. Our Allen practice offers specialized vascular care:

Interventional Procedures: When necessary, our Allen facility offers minimally invasive procedures performed by experienced vascular specialists. These may include procedures like angioplasty and stenting to open narrowed arteries, atherectomy to remove plaque, and treatments for varicose veins.

Vascular Conditions Treated: Our Allen practice addresses a range of vascular issues crucial for overall health.
