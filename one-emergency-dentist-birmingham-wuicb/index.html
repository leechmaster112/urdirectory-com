One Emergency Dentist Birmingham

One Emergency Dentist is a leading dental clinic in Birmingham, situated at 16 Drury Lane, Solihull, Birmingham, B91 3BG, United Kingdom. The clinic specialises in providing round-the-clock emergency dental care, operating 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year, ensuring that patients across the Birmingham area always have access to urgent dental treatment whenever they need it most. With a strong reputation reflected in an impressive 4.9-star rating on Google Reviews, One Emergency Dentist has established itself as a trusted destination for both emergency and cosmetic dental services. The dental clinic proudly serves over 1,000 happy patients every month and employs a team of more than 20 experienced dentists who bring over 80 years of combined professional experience to the practice. Among the key team members are Dr. Mustafa, an Invisalign provider who specialises in smile makeovers and restorative dentistry; Dr. Amir, a cosmetic dentist with expertise in veneers and teeth whitening; Dr. Wakkas, a surgical dentist skilled in oral surgery, cosmetic dentistry, and dental implants; and Dr. Zain, an emergency dentist who focuses on dental trauma and pain management. Together, this team delivers comprehensive care across a wide spectrum of dental needs. One of the standout features of this Birmingham dental clinic is its no-registration policy, meaning patients do not need to be registered to receive treatment. Walk-in appointments are welcome, and patients can often be seen in as little as 15 minutes, making the service particularly valuable for those experiencing sudden dental emergencies such as toothache, broken or chipped teeth, lost fillings or crowns, dental abscesses, trauma, wisdom tooth pain, lost implants or veneers, and the need for root canal treatment or tooth extraction. The clinic also offers a promotional emergency consultation at a reduced price of just £20, down from the standard £50 fee. Beyond emergency care, One Emergency Dentist provides a full range of cosmetic dentistry services designed to enhance patients' smiles and boost their confidence. These include professional teeth whitening, custom porcelain veneers, composite bonding, and Invisalign clear aligners. The clinic's smile gallery showcases impressive before-and-after transformations, demonstrating the quality of results patients can expect from treatments such as composite bonding for broken teeth and porcelain veneers for complete smile restorations. The clinic prides itself on creating a welcoming, judgement-free environment, particularly for nervous patients or those who may not have visited a dentist in years. The team is committed to making dentistry more accessible and less intimidating for everyone. Flexible payment options are also available, including 0% interest financing on cosmetic treatments, helping patients manage the cost of their care comfortably. Located conveniently just minutes from Birmingham city centre, One Emergency Dentist operates Monday through Sunday from 07:00 to 23:00, with emergency services extending beyond these hours. Patients can easily book appointments online, call the clinic directly at 07857 998997, or submit an appointment request through the website's contact form. Whether someone needs immediate relief from dental pain or is looking to transform their smile through cosmetic procedures, One Emergency Dentist stands ready to deliver expert, compassionate dental care at any hour of the day or night.
