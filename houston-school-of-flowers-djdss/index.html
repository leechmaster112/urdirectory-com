FAQs about Houston School of Flowers

Q1: What kind of floral design courses do you offer?

A1: We offer a variety of courses covering topics like Basic Floral Design, Wedding Flowers, Event Design, Ikebana, and specialized techniques. Check our website for the current schedule.

Q2: Do you provide supplies for the classes?

A2: Students are typically expected to bring their own floral supplies unless otherwise specified for a particular workshop. We recommend purchasing from local Houston flower shops.

Q3: Can I learn wedding flower design?

A3: Yes, we offer specific workshops dedicated to wedding flower design, covering bouquets, boutonnieres, and venue decor arrangements.

Q4: Are there beginner classes available?

A4: Absolutely! We have introductory courses designed for students with little to no prior experience in floral design.

Q5: How can I sign up for a course?

A5: You can register online via our website http://www.houstonschoolofflowers.com/ or call our Houston location directly at (713) 681-2400.
