Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):

Q: What types of fondue do you offer?

A: We offer a variety of fondue options, including classic cheese fondue, chocolate fondue, garlic fondue, and specialty blends. Our menu is regularly updated to feature seasonal ingredients and special creations.

Q: Can I make a reservation for a large group?

A: Yes, we welcome large groups and can accommodate parties of any size. Please contact us in advance to make a reservation for your group.

Q: Do you offer catering services for private events?

A: Yes, we provide catering services for private events. Please visit our website or call us for more information on booking a fondue station or full catering package.

A: We maintain a smart casual dress code. Guests are welcome to wear jeans and t-shirts, but overly casual attire may not be permitted during peak hours.

Q: Do you have vegetarian options?
