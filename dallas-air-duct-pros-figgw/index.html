Frequently Asked Questions
Q: Why should I have my air ducts professionally cleaned?

A professional duct cleaning improves indoor air quality by removing dust, pollen, and other allergens trapped in your ducts. It also enhances your HVAC system's efficiency, potentially lowering energy costs, and helps prevent fire hazards caused by accumulated debris.

Q: How often should air ducts be cleaned?

Generally, most experts recommend professional duct cleaning every 3-5 years. However, factors like allergies in household members, presence of pets, smoking, recent renovations, or if you live in a high-pollution area might necessitate more frequent cleaning.
