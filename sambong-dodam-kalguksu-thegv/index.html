Sambong Dodam - Kalguksu

Annyeonghaseyo! Myeongdong Kyoja is not the only restaurant that serves a quality kalguksu in korea. Our chicken noodle soup-style kalguksu is made with a rich chicken broth that has been simmered for over four hours, using fresh, locally sourced ingredients. You can also choose a spicy option to suit your taste! Our kalguksu pairs perfectly with thinly sliced bossam (boiled pork), and don’t miss out on our bibim kalguksu, a refreshing cold and spicy noodle dish loaded with vegetables. Plus, enjoy complimentary makgeolli during lunch hours! Come and taste the difference! We are located in Insadong, right by Jogyesa Temple. If you're on the hunt for authentic local foods in Korea, we warmly invite you to come and visit us!
