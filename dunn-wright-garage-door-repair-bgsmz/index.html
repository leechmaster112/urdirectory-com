Q1: My garage door is making a loud grinding noise. What could be the problem?

A1: A loud grinding noise often indicates an issue with the garage door opener's motor or gears, or potentially a problem with the rollers or tracks. Our technicians at Dunn & Wright Garage Door Repair can diagnose the exact cause and perform the necessary repairs to silence the noise and ensure smooth operation.

Q2: My garage door opener isn't working, but the door itself seems fine. Can you help?

A2: Absolutely. If your garage door opener is unresponsive, it could be due to a variety of issues, such as a faulty remote, a problem with the opener's motor, a tripped circuit breaker, or a damaged safety sensor. We specialize in diagnosing and repairing garage door openers to restore their functionality.

Q3: I think one of my garage door springs is broken. Is this dangerous?

A3: Yes, broken garage door springs can be dangerous and should be addressed immediately. Springs are under immense tension and are crucial for the balanced operation of your garage door.
