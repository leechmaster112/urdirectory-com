2. Do you offer pet boarding services?

Yes, we provide a clean, comfortable boarding facility for dogs and cats at our Oviedo location. Our boarding accommodations include climate-controlled rooms, fresh water, and daily care to ensure your pet's well-being during your absence.

3. How quickly can I get emergency care?

For after-hours emergencies, our facility is equipped to handle urgent cases. Walk-in patients are typically seen within 30 minutes, and we prioritize stabilizing critical conditions promptly.

4. What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, cash, and pet insurance. We also offer payment plans for major procedures to help manage costs.

5. Can you provide pet grooming services?

Yes, our experienced team offers professional grooming services including bathing, nail trimming, ear cleaning, and hairball treatment for cats and dogs at our Oviedo clinic.
