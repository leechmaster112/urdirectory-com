Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is IV therapy?

IV therapy, or intravenous therapy, involves the direct infusion of fluids, vitamins, minerals, and medications into your bloodstream via a vein. This method bypasses the digestive system, allowing for faster absorption and more immediate effects compared to oral supplementation.

How does IV therapy work?

During an IV therapy session, a healthcare professional will clean the skin at the injection site and insert a small, sterile needle into a vein. The IV solution flows through a tube connected to the needle. The process is generally quick and comfortable, with most treatments lasting between 30 minutes to an hour.

What conditions can IV therapy help with?

Infinite Dripz focuses on wellness and preventive care. Our IV therapy can help with: dehydration, fatigue, jet lag, hangover recovery, immune system support, energy boosting, athletic recovery, and general wellness optimization. Consult with us to determine the best option for your specific needs.

How long does an IV therapy session take?

The duration of an IV therapy session typically ranges from 30 to 90 minutes, depending on the specific treatment chosen and your individual needs. This includes the time for the actual infusion and the initial consultation/setup.

Is IV therapy safe?

IV therapy is generally considered safe when administered by qualified professionals using sterile equipment. At Infinite Dripz, we prioritize safety and adhere to strict hygiene protocols. However, like any medical procedure, there can be minor side effects such as slight discomfort at the injection site or, rarely, infection. Our team will discuss any potential risks and contraindications with you before treatment.
