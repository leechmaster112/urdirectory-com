Ivanna Tapia Immigration Services

Ivanna Tapia Immigration Services provides Canadian immigration consulting in Calgary, with personalized support in English and Spanish. Led by Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant Ivanna E. Tapia, we help with spousal and family sponsorship, permanent residence, Express Entry, provincial nominee programs, work and study permits, visitor visas and Super Visas. We also assist with refugee claims, immigration appeals, humanitarian and compassionate applications, and previously refused cases. Alberta notary public services and immigration document translation support are available. Meet with Ivanna by appointment at our Calgary office or online from anywhere in Canada or abroad.
