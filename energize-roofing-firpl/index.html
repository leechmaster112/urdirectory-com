Most issues can be solved with repairs unless the roof has extensive structural damage or widespread deterioration. Signs that suggest repair is enough include isolated leaks, a few cracked or slipped tiles, or localised mortar failure. A full replacement is usually only needed when the roof has reached the end of its lifespan or has multiple failing areas.
