Twin Cities Living Assistance

Twin Cities Living Assistance is Burnsville's premier assisted living and senior care community, dedicated to delivering exceptional, personalized care in a warm, home-like environment. Located at 12920 Highclere Dr in Burnsville, MN, our facility offers comprehensive services including 24-hour professional caregiving, specialized dementia and memory care, chronic disease management, medication administration, and assistance with all activities of daily living. Contact us today at (612) 964-2222 for a complimentary consultation and facility tour, and discover why families trust Twin Cities Living Assistance as Burnsville's most compassionate and professional senior care provider. Our highly trained, compassionate staff maintains a low staff-to-resident ratio, ensuring each resident receives the attentive, dignified care they deserve while fostering independence and quality of life. Our state-of-the-art community features luxurious, thoughtfully designed living spaces that prioritize both safety and comfort, complemented by a robust schedule of social, recreational, and wellness programs. From nutritious chef-prepared meals accommodating special dietary needs to housekeeping, laundry services, and transportation assistance for medical appointments and outings, we handle every detail so families can focus on what matters most—spending quality time with their loved ones. Our secure facility includes round-the-clock monitoring, on-site nursing services, and emergency response systems to ensure peace of mind for residents and families alike. What sets Twin Cities Living Assistance apart is our unwavering commitment to creating a vibrant community where seniors truly flourish. We offer both assisted living and independent living options, allowing residents to choose the level of support that best fits their needs. With individualized care plans tailored to each resident's unique circumstances, engaging wellness programs that promote physical and mental health, and a proactive approach to healthcare coordination, we've established ourselves as the gold standard in senior living throughout the Twin Cities area.
