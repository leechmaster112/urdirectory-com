What is TMS therapy and how does it work at Embracing Life Wellness Center?

TMS therapy uses magnetic pulses to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. At our Coral Gables location, we precisely target specific brain regions involved in mood regulation, offering a non-invasive treatment option.

Is TMS therapy right for me?

TMS is often considered for individuals who haven't found sufficient relief with antidepressant medications. Our expert psychiatrists will conduct a thorough evaluation to determine if it's the appropriate treatment for your specific needs.

What can I expect during a TMS treatment session at 2600 Douglas Rd Suite 711?

Sessions are typically outpatient and involve a magnetic coil placed on your scalp. You'll be awake and alert, and most patients find the sensation to be mild, often described as a tapping or clicking.

How long does it take to see results from TMS therapy?

While some patients experience improvements sooner, many begin to notice positive changes after several weeks of consistent treatment at our Miami TMS facility. The full course of treatment is designed for sustained relief.
