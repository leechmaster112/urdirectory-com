Frequently Asked Questions

What is TMS therapy and how is it performed at your Coral Gables office?

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is an FDA-cleared, non-invasive treatment that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain to improve symptoms of depression. At our Coral Gables location at 2600 Douglas Rd Suite 711, patients relax in a comfortable chair during each session. A magnetic coil is gently placed against the scalp, delivering brief pulses to the brain's prefrontal cortex. The procedure is painless and requires no anesthesia.

How do I know if TMS therapy at your Douglas Road clinic is right for me?

TMS therapy is often recommended for individuals with Major Depressive Disorder who have not experienced satisfactory improvement from antidepressant medications. The best way to determine if you are a candidate is to schedule a consultation with Dr. Melissa Fickey at our Coral Gables wellness center. She will conduct a thorough evaluation to see if TMS is an appropriate treatment option for your specific situation.

What can I expect during my first visit to Embracing Life Wellness Center?

Your first visit to our office at 2600 Douglas Rd Suite 711 is a comprehensive consultation with Dr. Fickey. This appointment involves a detailed discussion of your mental health history, current symptoms, and previous treatments. This allows us to understand your needs fully and determine if our TMS services in Coral Gables are the best path forward for your wellness journey.

Are the TMS treatments at your Coral Gables location covered by insurance?

Many insurance providers do offer coverage for TMS therapy for Major Depressive Disorder. Our administrative team at the Coral Gables office will work directly with you and your insurance company to verify your benefits and explain all associated costs before you begin your treatment program, ensuring complete transparency.

How long does a typical TMS treatment program last at your clinic?

A typical TMS treatment course involves sessions conducted five days a week for approximately 4-6 weeks, with each session lasting about 20-40 minutes. All treatments are administered on-site at our Coral Gables location. The total duration can vary based on individual patient response and the specific protocol developed by Dr. Fickey.
