The K-9 Center — Hauppauge, NY

Nestled in the heart of Hauppauge, Long Island, The K-9 Center stands as a pillar of excellence in dog training and responsible breeding. We understand the unique bond between humans and their canine partners, and it is our mission to support you every step of the way, from bringing a new puppy into your home to refining the skills of your established dog.

At The K-9 Center, our expertise lies in positive reinforcement techniques, ensuring that training is effective, humane, and builds a strong partnership between you and your pet. Our programs are tailored to meet the specific needs of individual dogs and their owners, addressing common behavioral issues, house-training challenges, and promoting socialization. We believe in building trust and respect, creating a positive learning environment where dogs thrive.

Our commitment extends beyond training. The K-9 Center also operates a reputable dog breeding program. We carefully select and ethically breed healthy dogs with known temperaments and sound pedigrees. Our breeding practices prioritize the health and well-being of our breeding stock and the puppies they produce. We take pride in our responsible approach to breeding, ensuring our puppies are raised in a nurturing environment and go to loving homes.

Whether you're a first-time dog owner navigating the joys and challenges of puppyhood, dealing with a behavioral concern, or looking to welcome a new addition to your family through ethical breeding, The K-9 Center at 440 Wheeler Road is your trusted partner.
