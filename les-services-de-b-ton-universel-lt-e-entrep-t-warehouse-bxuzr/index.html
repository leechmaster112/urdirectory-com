Les Services de Béton Universel Ltée (Entrepôt / Warehouse)

Les Services de Béton Universel Ltée is Montreal's premier industrial and commercial concrete contractor, proudly serving the Province of Quebec with over 50 years of combined experience. Specializing in concrete repair, restoration, and reinforced machine foundation construction, our expert team delivers incomparable results for industrial facilities, commercial properties, and underground parking structures. Services include diamond saw-cutting, core drilling, structural concrete repair, concrete slab grinding, surface preparation, pyrite decontamination, and the construction of heavily reinforced concrete bases for heavy machinery. Equipped with tier 4 plant-friendly saws, electric mini excavators, and state-of-the-art grinding technology, we ensure efficient, environmentally conscious project completion. We work closely with property managers and engineers to maintain safe, code-compliant concrete structures. Save money with our proactive yearly maintenance programs designed to extend the life of your concrete assets. Call (855) 613-4773 today for a free, no-obligation quote on your next concrete project!
