Craft cocktails & global cuisine in Las Vegas Step into Alchemist, where craft, flavor, and atmosphere come together in the heart of Las Vegas. Our bar and restaurant is inspired by the spirit of transformation—turning fresh, high-quality ingredients into unforgettable food and cocktails. From refreshing spritzes to signature creations, every drink is designed with creativity and flair. Pair them with our carefully curated menu of elevated dishes, and you’ll find a dining experience that feels both mystical and modern. Whether you’re here for after-work drinks, a night out with friends, or a full culinary adventure, Alchemist is your new go-to spot for bold flavors, vibrant energy, and a touch of magic. Our Story At our bar, we’re redefining the drinking experience. You won’t find the usual syrupy mixes here — we craft each drink with purpose, creativity, and balance. We focus on bold flavors and refined techniques, combining simple yet innovative preparations with a curated selection of diverse, high-quality ingredients. We take pride in sourcing regional spirits, herbs, and botanicals from small, local producers across the country. Every element is thoughtfully chosen to celebrate authenticity and craftsmanship. This is more than just a bar — it’s a space where passion meets flavor, and where every cocktail tells a story worth sipping.
