Camp Shohola For Boys

Camp Shohola is a sleep-away summer camp for boys ages 7–16, located in Greeley, Pennsylvania. We proudly serve families across the New York Tri-State Area, offering an unforgettable summer experience filled with water sports, land sports, outdoor adventure, communications/technology, the arts, and more. Since 1943, we’ve been committed to creating a safe, exciting, and growth-oriented environment for boys. If you’re looking for a well-rounded summer camp that emphasizes adventure, creativity, skill-building, and personal growth, Camp Shohola is the perfect choice. Register with us today: (570) 371-4760.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What ages does Camp Shohola For Boys accept?

A: Camp Shohola accepts boys aged 7 through 16 for our sleep-away summer camp programs held at our location in Greeley.

Q: How can families in Greeley register their sons for Camp Shohola?

A: Registration information and application forms for Camp Shohola can be found on our website https://www.shohola.com/. You can also contact our office directly at +15703714760.

Q: Does Camp Shohola offer any programs for younger boys not yet ready for full sleep-away camp?

A: Camp Shohola focuses exclusively on our sleep-away program for boys entering 2nd grade (age 7) through 16th grade. We do not offer day camp programs at this location.

Q: What is the typical camp session schedule?

A: Camp Shohola typically runs for 8-9 weeks during the summer months. Session schedules vary each year, so please check our website https://www.shohola.com/ for the most current session dates and registration deadlines.

Q: Are there any financial aid or scholarship options available for families in the Greeley area?

A: Camp Shohola offers financial aid and scholarship opportunities to make our programs accessible. Information regarding financial assistance can be obtained by contacting our camp office at +15703714760 or exploring the financial aid section on our website.
