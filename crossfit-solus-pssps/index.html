CrossFit Solus

CrossFit Solus has been Sherman, Texas's premier fitness destination since 2009, transforming the lives of hundreds of community members through expert coaching, functional training, and an unwavering commitment to results. Located at 2001 Skyline Dr., Suite D400, Sherman, TX 75092, this top-rated CrossFit gym has earned an exceptional reputation with over 600 five-star reviews across Google, Yelp, and Facebook. For more information or to schedule your complimentary consultation, call (903) 564-1118 or visit their facility to discover how CrossFit Solus can help you transform your fitness and your life.Their certified coaches deliver personalized attention in every coach-led class, ensuring that members of all ages and fitness levels—from complete beginners to experienced athletes—receive scalable workouts designed to build strength, endurance, and confidence safely and effectively. What sets CrossFit Solus apart is their comprehensive approach to wellness and their diverse program offerings. Beyond traditional group CrossFit classes, they provide specialized programming including Fitness over 50 for active adults seeking safe functional workouts, Pilates for core strength and flexibility, Personal Training for individualized coaching, CrossFit for Kids, and professional Nutritional Coaching. Every membership includes access to nutrition and habits coaching, InBody body-fat testing, and goal-setting sessions, ensuring members receive holistic support for their health journey. The gym's mission is to change lives through functional fitness, nutrition education, and accountability within a positive and motivating environment. The community-driven atmosphere at CrossFit Solus creates an encouraging space where members push their limits, celebrate progress together, and build lasting connections both inside and outside the gym. With flexible membership options starting at $89.50 every two weeks and a free first class for newcomers, CrossFit Solus makes it easy to experience why they're considered Sherman's most trusted and supportive fitness community.
