FAQ

What can I expect during my first ketamine infusion session at your Pompano Beach clinic?

Your first infusion session will involve a private, comfortable treatment room. Our medical team will monitor you closely throughout the infusion, ensuring your safety and comfort. The experience is typically relaxing, and you will be under our care for the duration of the treatment and a recovery period.

Is Spravato a new type of antidepressant?

Spravato (esketamine) is a nasal spray medication approved by the FDA for treatment-resistant depression and depressive symptoms in adults with major depressive disorder. It works differently from traditional oral antidepressants and is administered under strict medical supervision at our clinic.

How is ketamine infusion therapy different from traditional treatments?

Ketamine infusion therapy offers a rapid-acting approach to mood improvement, often providing relief much sooner than traditional antidepressants. It targets different pathways in the brain, offering a novel option for those who haven't found success with other therapies.

Will I be sedated during my ketamine infusion at 41 N Federal Hwy Ste A?

You will not be fully sedated. The ketamine infusion is administered at a controlled dose to induce a dissociative or altered state, which is a key component of its therapeutic effect. You will remain conscious but in a relaxed and potentially introspective state, always under the watchful eye of our medical staff.

What is the recovery process like after a ketamine infusion or Spravato treatment?

After your treatment at our Pompano Beach location, you will spend time in our recovery area while the effects of the medication wear off. You will be monitored until you are stable to leave, and we will provide you with instructions for rest and hydration. It is important to have someone drive you home after your appointment.
