Downtown Durham Dog Park

Durham's most central dog park sits on Elliott Street at the east edge of downtown - a fully fenced off-leash space split into separate small-dog and large-dog sections with double-gated entries, benches, and stocked waste bags. Run by Durham Parks and Recreation, it's free and open 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM daily. Hours, photos, and directions are on its Doggie Park Near Me listing.

Location

Tired of your energetic pup needing an outlet closer to home? Downtown Durham Dog Park on Elliott Street provides the ultimate solution for local dog owners seeking a safe and convenient place for their furry companions to roam and socialize. Nestled right at the east edge of downtown Durham, this fully fenced haven ensures peace of mind for you and boundless fun for your dog, eliminating the long drives to find suitable off-leash space. It's the go-to spot for residents who want their dogs to experience freedom without compromising on security.

Our prime location at 304 Elliott St, Durham, NC 27703, makes us incredibly accessible for downtown dwellers and those working nearby. We understand the importance of a dedicated, secure environment where dogs can expend energy, meet new friends, and simply be dogs. Trust us to be your neighborhood's premier destination for canine recreation.

For more information and to plan your visit, check out our website: https://doggieparknearme.com/parks/downtown-durham-dog-park-durham-nc or call us directly at (919) 560-4355.

What makes Downtown Durham Dog Park a trusted choice for local dog owners?

Our commitment to providing a fully fenced and secure environment on Elliott Street means your dog can play freely without worry. We are deeply integrated into the downtown Durham community, offering a convenient and accessible escape for your pet's exercise needs.

Is the dog park suitable for dogs of all sizes?

While the park offers ample space for all dogs to enjoy, the primary focus is on providing a safe, enclosed area for off-leash play. We recommend owners supervise their dogs to ensure positive interactions.

What are the operating hours for the dog park?

Please refer to our website at https://doggieparknearme.com/parks/downtown-durham-dog-park-durham-nc for the most current operating hours, as these can vary based on seasonal changes and city maintenance schedules.

Can I bring my puppy to the park?

Yes, puppies are welcome! It's a great place for them to socialize and burn off energy in a controlled environment on Elliott Street. We encourage owners to ensure their puppies are up-to-date on vaccinations.

What should I bring for my dog when visiting?

We recommend bringing water and a bowl for your dog, especially during warmer months. Waste bags are also essential to help us maintain the cleanliness of the park at 304 Elliott St, Durham, NC 27703.
