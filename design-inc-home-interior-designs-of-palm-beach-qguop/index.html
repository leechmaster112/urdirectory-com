Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of projects does Design Inc. handle?

We specialize in both residential and commercial interior design projects. For commercial clients, we design retail spaces, restaurants, corporate offices, healthcare facilities, and hospitality venues.

Q2: How do I get started with Design Inc.?

Getting started is simple! You can schedule a complimentary consultation by calling our office at (561) 355-5071 or by reaching out through our website at https://designincpalmbeach.com/. During this initial meeting, we'll discuss your project goals, budget, timeline, and gather inspiration to understand your vision fully.

Q3: How much does interior design cost?

The cost of interior design services varies significantly based on the project's scope, complexity, size, and the level of involvement required. Factors include the number of spaces, design development hours, material selection assistance, project management, and travel expenses. We believe in transparent pricing and typically offer different service levels or retainers to accommodate various budgets. We provide detailed proposals outlining all costs before commencing work.

Q4: How long does a typical interior design project take?

The timeline for a project depends heavily on its scope and the client's responsiveness. A simple room refresh might take a few weeks, while a full home renovation or commercial build-out could span several months. Our process involves multiple phases: initial consultation, concept development, design refinement, material selection, project coordination (if applicable), and implementation. We work closely with you and contractors to establish a realistic timeline and keep you informed throughout.

Q5: Do I need to be involved in the design process?

Your involvement is crucial and exciting! While our team provides expert guidance and design solutions, we collaborate closely with you. We present concepts, gather your feedback, and make adjustments until the design aligns perfectly with your preferences and needs. Being actively involved ensures the final space truly reflects your personality and lifestyle. We empower clients to make informed decisions throughout the process.
