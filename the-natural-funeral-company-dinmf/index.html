What does "natural funeral" mean at The Natural Funeral Company?

A natural funeral prioritises environmentally friendly practices and a return to the earth. This can include biodegradable coffins, natural burial grounds, and avoiding embalming. Our aim is to create ceremonies that are both respectful of the deceased and gentle on the environment.

How can I create a unique service to honour my loved one?

We work closely with families to understand the individual personality, passions, and wishes of your loved one. This allows us to weave personal touches into the ceremony, whether through music, readings, specific rituals, or incorporating elements that were meaningful to them.

Do you offer pre-planning services?

Yes, we do. Pre-planning allows you to make your wishes known in advance, easing the burden on your family when the time comes. We can discuss natural funeral options and help you document your preferences.

What are the options for burial at a natural funeral?

We can guide you through options for natural burial grounds, where the body is interred without preservation and in a biodegradable container, allowing for a natural decomposition process.
