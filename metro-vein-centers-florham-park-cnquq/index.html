Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What specific services do you offer at your Florham Park location?

We provide a comprehensive range of vein treatment services including radiofrequency ablation, endovenous laser treatment, sclerotherapy, and compression therapy. Our treatments are tailored to address spider veins, varicose veins, and chronic venous insufficiency.

Q: How long does a typical vein treatment procedure take?

Most procedures at our Florham Park center take between 30 minutes to three hours, depending on the complexity of the case. Many treatments are performed on an outpatient basis, allowing patients to return to their daily activities with minimal disruption.

Q: What is the recovery process like after vein treatment?

Recovery varies by treatment type, but most patients experience mild discomfort and can resume normal activities within a few days. We provide detailed post-treatment care instructions to ensure optimal healing and results.

Q: Are the treatments at your Florham Park center minimally invasive?

Yes, we specialize in minimally invasive procedures that typically result in less pain, minimal scarring, and faster recovery times compared to traditional surgical options.

Q: How soon can I schedule a consultation at your Florham Park location?

We offer same-day appointments for new patients. You can schedule your consultation by calling (973)3828771 or booking online through our website.
