Heartfulness Meditation Centre Fatehgarh

Heartfulness Meditation Centre in Fatehgarh, near Yaqutganj, offers free meditation classes, one-to-one guided sessions with certified trainers, and regular group meditations for the Fatehgarh community. Located in Fatehgarh, the administrative headquarters of Farrukhabad district on the banks of the Ganges, our centre is an easy stop for anyone seeking relaxation, stress relief, and inner balance. We welcome visitors from across Fatehgarh and neighboring Farrukhabad, including Yaqutganj, Shyam Nagar (Fatehgarh), Kadrigate, Devrampur, and Farrukhabad. Heartfulness meditation is practised in over 160 countries and is always offered free of charge. Visit us to experience guided meditation with certified trainers.
