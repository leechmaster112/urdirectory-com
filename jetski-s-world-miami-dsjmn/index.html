Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of jet skis do you offer for rent?

We provide a range of high-quality jet skis suitable for various skill levels and group sizes. From standard two-seaters to larger capacity models, we have options to ensure everyone has a fantastic time.

Q2: Do you offer guided tours or lessons?

Absolutely! We offer guided tours for those wanting a stress-free adventure and introductory lessons for beginners. Our certified instructors are experienced and passionate about sharing the joy of jet skiing.

Q3: What is the rental process like?

Booking is easy! You can reserve your jet ski(s) online via our website or call us directly. Upon arrival, you'll complete a brief safety orientation and rental agreement before enjoying your ride.

Q4: Are the jet skis well-maintained?

Yes, we take pride in maintaining our fleet meticulously. All equipment undergoes regular inspections and servicing to guarantee peak performance and your safety on the water.

Q5: What safety equipment is provided?

We provide life jackets for all riders, and our safety protocols include mandatory helmet use for beginners and safety instructions for all rentals.
