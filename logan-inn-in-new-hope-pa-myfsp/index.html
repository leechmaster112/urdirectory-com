Logan Inn in New Hope PA

First established in the 1700s, this landmark, historic property is a block from the Delaware River and a 2-minute walk to the Buck's County Playhouse. Logan inn is a wedding and event venue for all looking to have an elegant party of the most romantic day in their life for the New Hope area. The inn's rooms, many with canopy beds and sitting areas, are individually decorated with period furnishings and have free WiFi, TVs and en suite bathrooms. Views are of the river, canal and/or Main Street below. A pair of on-site restaurants have seating on a glass-enclosed porch and a tented patio; one offers upscale Mediterranean cuisine and the other features casual American fare, Belgian beer and cocktails. There's limited free parking
