Advanced Vein Treatment Services

We offer a comprehensive range of services designed to address various types of veins. Our treatments are performed by skilled phlebologists (vascular physicians) and are designed to be effective, comfortable, and with minimal downtime. Some of the innovative services you can expect at our Marlboro center include:

Sclerotherapy: This is a standard and effective treatment for smaller veins like spider and reticular veins. A specially formulated solution is injected directly into the targeted vein, causing it to inflame and eventually fade away, becoming less visible over time.

Laser Vein Treatment (Endovenous Laser Treatment - EVLT, VenaSeal): For larger veins like varicose veins, we utilize cutting-edge laser technology. Using ultrasound guidance, we precisely target the diseased vein with laser energy, closing it shut from the inside. Our suite includes both the endovenous laser technique and the VenaSeal system, which uses a medical-grade adhesive to seal the vein. Both methods are minimally invasive, typically performed on an outpatient basis, and designed for excellent long-term results with minimal discomfort.

Radiofrequency Ablation (RFA): Similar to laser treatment, RFA uses controlled radiofrequency energy delivered through a catheter into the affected vein to seal it shut. It's another effective option for treating larger varicose veins, offering reliable results with a quick recovery period.

Mesotherapy: For diffuse facial veins, particularly on the cheeks and nose (often called "mask veins"), our center offers mesotherapy. This involves micro-injections of a sclerosing solution directly into the visible veins to effectively diminish their appearance.

Compression Therapy: We provide custom-fitted compression stockings, which are an essential part of both treatment and prevention for venous conditions. Our knowledgeable staff can guide you in selecting the appropriate level of compression and ensure proper fitting.
