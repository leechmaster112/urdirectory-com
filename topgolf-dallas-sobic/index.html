Topgolf Dallas

Topgolf Dallas is the ultimate spot to play, eat, and hang out, whatever your skill level. Take your shot in our climate-controlled hitting bays, then keep the fun going with games for the whole group. Challenge your crew to mini golf, step up to the batting cages, toss a few bags in cornhole, or test your steady hand with giant Jenga. Our arcade adds even more ways to play between bites and drinks.
