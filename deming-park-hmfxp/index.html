Deming Park

Deming Park is a 177-acre public park in Terre Haute, Indiana, with walking trails, open green space, and a designated off-leash area for dogs to play and socialize. Amenities include water stations, waste stations, and benches, and leashed dogs are welcome on the Poplar Street Trail. Hours, photos, and directions are on its Doggie Park Near Me listing.
