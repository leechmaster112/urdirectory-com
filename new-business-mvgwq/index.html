FAQ

Q: How long do I have to file a claim after my Cape Coral car accident?

A: In Florida, the statute of limitations for personal injury claims, including car accidents, is generally four years from the date of the accident.

Q: What types of injuries do you handle?

A: We handle a wide range of injuries resulting from car accidents, including whiplash, broken bones, burns, traumatic brain injuries, and spinal cord injuries.

Q: Do I need to go to court?

A: Many car accident cases are resolved through negotiation with the insurance company. We will explore all options to avoid litigation if possible.

Q: How much will it cost to hire you?

A: We work on a contingency fee basis, meaning you only pay if we win your case. Our fee is a percentage of the settlement or award.

Q: Can you help me with medical bill negotiations?

A: Yes, we can assist you in negotiating with medical providers to ensure your medical expenses are properly accounted for in your claim.
