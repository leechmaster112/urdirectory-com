Frequently Asked Questions

Question: Why do I absolutely need a custodian like Gold IRA Custodians to set up a Gold IRA?

Answer: The IRS requires that any Self-Directed IRA holding physical precious metals must be held by a qualified custodian or trustee. This custodian holds legal title to the assets, ensures compliance with IRS rules (like 401(a)(9) and 401(b)(10)), and manages the account. Without a qualified custodian, the IRA could be deemed disqualified, leading to taxes and penalties on the held assets. Gold IRA Custodians provides this essential expertise and infrastructure.

Question: What exactly does a Gold IRA Custodian do?

Answer: A Gold IRA Custodian manages the Self-Directed IRA account for clients investing in physical gold and other approved precious metals. Our responsibilities include: verifying account funding (rollovers, transfers), processing transactions for purchasing eligible metals, arranging secure storage with IRS-approved depositories, maintaining accurate records, and ensuring all activities comply with IRS regulations. We act as the fiduciary overseeing the account.

Question: Are all gold and silver the same for an IRA?

Answer: No. The IRS has specific rules regarding the type and purity of precious metals allowed in a Self-Directed Gold IRA. Only certain forms (like bars, bullion coins, or numismatic coins meeting specific criteria) and specific fineness levels (typically .995 or higher) are permitted. Gold IRA Custodians guides clients in selecting IRS-approved gold and silver products to ensure their investments remain compliant.

Question: How does the process of funding a Gold IRA work?

Answer: Funding typically involves a direct rollover or transfer from an existing retirement account (like a 401(k) or traditional IRA) to the new Gold IRA Custodian. We facilitate this process to ensure it's done correctly and efficiently, often within 60 days to maintain tax-advantaged status. We can also assist with funding using after-tax dollars. Our team handles the paperwork and logistics.

Question: Where is the gold stored in a Gold IRA?

Answer: For IRS compliance, physical gold held in a Self-Directed IRA must be stored in an approved depository. Gold IRA Custodians arranges secure storage through IRS-approved facilities, typically segregated from other clients' holdings. This protects the assets and ensures the custodial requirements are met. You don't personally store the gold at home.
