Bales Law Firm

Bales Law Firm is a trusted legal advocate serving Orange Beach and the greater Baldwin County and Lower Alabama communities. Located at 24951 Perdido Beach Blvd in the Bellator Building in Orange Beach, Alabama, Bales Law Firm is dedicated to being more than just a legal service provider—they serve as a true advocate for their clients' interests. The firm's approach combines prompt, efficient service with thorough legal expertise, making even the most complex legal matters manageable. Whether you're planning for your family's future, settling an estate, or building a business, Bales Law Firm offers the experienced counsel and personalized attention you need. Contact them today at (251) 213-9343 for a free consultation and discover why they're considered one of the premier law firms in Orange Beach, AL. Led by attorney Chris Bales, the firm specializes in comprehensive estate planning, probate administration, and business law services designed to protect clients' fundamental rights and secure their futures. With a commitment to utilizing extensive legal and financial knowledge, Bales Law Firm provides personalized guidance through complex legal matters, ensuring clients understand their rights and options at every step of the process. The firm offers a full spectrum of estate and life planning services, including the creation of wills and trusts, healthcare directives, powers of attorney, and probate administration. Bales Law Firm helps individuals and families make informed decisions about asset protection, estate distribution, and end-of-life planning, ensuring their wishes are honored and their loved ones are protected. Additionally, the firm provides sophisticated business legal services, from business formation and structuring to ongoing legal consultation, helping entrepreneurs and established businesses navigate operational and strategic decisions with confidence.
