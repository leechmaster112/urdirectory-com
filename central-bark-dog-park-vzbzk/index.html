Central Bark Dog Park

Central Bark Dog Park is a community-loved pop-up park in downtown Memphis' South Main district, transforming a vacant lot into a fenced off-leash area with separate spaces for dogs under and over 25 lbs. It features benches, picnic tables, a water fountain, agility obstacles, wood-chip surfacing with grassy areas, and shade from canopies and umbrellas. Hours, photos, and directions are on its Doggie Park Near Me listing.
