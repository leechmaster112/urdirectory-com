Frequently Asked Questions:

Q: What specific type of meditation is taught at this centre?

A: The centre focuses on Heartfulness meditation, a practical system of meditation based on the transmission of a higher state of consciousness from the heart of the trainer to the heart of the aspirant, aiming to help individuals find inner peace and self-improvement.

Q: Is there a fee to attend the meditation classes?

A: No, all meditation classes offered at the Free SRCM Heartfulness Meditation Centre in Sector 16, New Panvel East, are completely free of charge.

Q: What should I expect during my first meditation session?

A: Your first session will likely involve an introduction to the Heartfulness method and a guided meditation, allowing you to experience the practice firsthand in a supportive atmosphere.

Q: Do I need any prior meditation experience to join?

A: Absolutely not. The Heartfulness meditation technique is designed to be accessible to everyone, regardless of their previous experience with meditation. Beginners are most welcome.

Q: What are the benefits of practicing Heartfulness meditation regularly?

A: Regular practice can lead to reduced stress, improved focus, enhanced emotional well-being, greater self-awareness, and a general sense of inner calm and contentment.
