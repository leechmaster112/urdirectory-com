Roland's Swiss Pastry & Bakery

Roland's Swiss Pastry & Bakery is a premier Swiss-inspired commercial bakery located in Houston, TX, specializing in artisan baked goods made with traditional European techniques. As a trusted wholesale bakery, we proudly serve local businesses, restaurants, and events with high-quality pastries and desserts. Our bakery is known for expertly crafted custom cake designs, including elegant creations from our wedding cake bakery and beautifully customized birthday cake options for all occasions. We also operate as a full-service muffin bakery, offering fresh, flavorful muffins baked daily. For added convenience, Roland's Swiss Pastry & Bakery provides reliable pastry delivery throughout the Houston area, ensuring every order arrives fresh, delicious, and on time.
