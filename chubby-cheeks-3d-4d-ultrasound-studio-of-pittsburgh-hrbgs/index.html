Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What kind of ultrasound services does Chubby Cheeks offer at its Pittsburgh location?

A1: Our Pittsburgh studio exclusively offers elective 3D and 4D ultrasound imaging services. These advanced imaging techniques provide detailed, moving pictures of your baby.

Q2: Is the 3D/4D ultrasound safe for my baby?

A2: Yes. The 3D and 4D ultrasounds we perform at our Pittsburgh studio use sound waves, similar to traditional ultrasounds, and are considered safe for both mother and baby when performed by trained professionals using appropriate equipment.

Q3: What is the difference between 3D and 4D ultrasound?

A3: 3D ultrasound creates still, three-dimensional images of your baby. 4D ultrasound builds upon this technology by adding motion, allowing you to see your baby moving in real-time.

Q4: How early can I get a 3D/4D ultrasound at Chubby Cheeks in Pittsburgh?

A4: Generally, a 3D or 4D ultrasound can be performed starting around weeks 14-15 of pregnancy. We recommend scheduling your appointment after the first trimester for the best image quality.

Q5: Do I need a prescription or doctor referral for a 3D/4D ultrasound at Chubby Cheeks?

A5: While a 3D/4D ultrasound is considered an elective service and not medically mandatory, we require a valid prescription or referral form signed by your obstetrician or healthcare provider for your visit.
