NStar Builds

NStar Builds is a premier, full-service construction and renovation contractor specializing in high-end residential transformations across North London and select affluent areas of West and East London. Under the leadership of Shane Jones, the firm has earned a reputation for delivering "turnkey" excellence, managing complex projects from structural inception to the final interior flourish. NStar Builds is specifically designed for homeowners who demand organized, transparent, and superior craftsmanship, offering a seamless experience where clients simply "move their furniture in" upon completion. The company excels in full house refurbishments and large-scale renovations, with a particular expertise in the architectural challenges of London’s period properties. Their portfolio includes high-value Victorian home conversions into multiple flats, sophisticated loft conversions, and expansive house extensions. By maintaining a strictly organized project management style, NStar Builds ensures that structural works—such as chimney and wall removals—are executed with the same precision as their bespoke kitchen and bathroom installations. Driven by a results-oriented approach and a commitment to transparency, NStar Builds leverages real-time project photography and a robust portfolio of verified Checkatrade success to demonstrate their "on-the-ground" expertise. Serving a 5-mile radius of North London’s most sought-after neighborhoods, they are the trusted partner for homeowners looking to significantly increase property value through expert remodeling.
