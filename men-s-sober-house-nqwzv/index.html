Location

Men's Sober House at 1501 S Newkirk St, Philadelphia, PA 19146

At Men's Sober House located at 1501 S Newkirk St, Philadelphia, PA 19146, we provide a dedicated space for men committed to sobriety. Our facility offers a structured environment designed to support recovery and personal growth within the heart of South Philadelphia.

Our approach focuses on creating a supportive community within a community. Men in our program benefit from peer support networks and guidance from experienced staff members. We offer daily activities including group discussions, educational sessions, and wellness programs tailored to individual needs.

Program Structure

Residents participate in regular meetings and personal counseling sessions. We maintain a strict code of conduct that promotes sobriety and healthy living. The facility provides comfortable living quarters and nutritious meals to support physical well-being during the recovery process.

Community Integration

While maintaining boundaries for recovery, we encourage positive engagement with the local Philadelphia community. Men have opportunities to participate in local events and activities that foster connection and growth.

Our Commitment

At our South Philadelphia location, we are dedicated to providing a safe, structured environment that supports long-term sobriety. Our team works closely with each resident to develop personalized recovery plans.
