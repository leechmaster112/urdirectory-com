Through its collaboration with Cowrent, ADTRANSPORT integrates advanced digital solutions, allowing users to easily book, manage, and modify their reservations online. ADTRANSPORT and Cowrent share a commitment to sustainability and efficiency, offering newer, eco-friendly models like hybrid and electric vehicles for clients seeking greener travel options. Their mission is simple yet ambitious — to make transportation across Lithuania as easy, safe, and comfortable as possible. With years of experience, a trusted network, and a customer-first approach, ADTRANSPORT and Cowrent continue to set the standard for quality vehicle rental services in Lithuania.
