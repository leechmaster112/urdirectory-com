Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is NeuroStar® TMS therapy?

A: NeuroStar® TMS is a non-invasive treatment for depression that uses focused magnetic pulses to stimulate brain regions associated with mood regulation.

Q2: How does TMS treatment work at the Charlotte center?

A: During a TMS session at our Charlotte location, our trained technicians will precisely target specific brain areas using the NeuroStar® device while you comfortably sit in a chair.

Q3: Is TMS therapy effective for depression?

A: Clinical studies demonstrate that TMS can be an effective treatment option for depression, particularly for individuals who have not responded well to previous treatments.

Q4: What can I expect during a TMS treatment session in Charlotte?

A: Each TMS session at our Charlotte center typically lasts about 20-40 minutes and is performed while you relax in a chair. You'll hear clicking sounds and may feel gentle tapping sensations.

Q5: Does TMS treatment require anesthesia or medication?

A: No, TMS is a completely non-sedated treatment. You remain awake and alert throughout the entire session at our Charlotte facility.
