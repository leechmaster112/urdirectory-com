Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of therapy do you offer at your White Plains location?

We primarily offer individual talk therapy using evidence-based approaches such as Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) skills, mindfulness techniques, and other modalities tailored to the client's specific needs and goals.

Q2: How long does a typical therapy session last?

Standard therapy sessions at our White Plains office are typically scheduled for 50 minutes. Session length can sometimes be adjusted based on individual needs, discussed during your initial appointment.

Q3: What is the process for scheduling an appointment at 75 Virginia Road Suite 105?

Scheduling is easy. You can call our phone number, 914-340-4553, directly. Alternatively, you can visit our website, https://www.sagetalktherapy.com/, where we provide an online scheduling option. We will work with you to find a convenient time for your first appointment.

Q4: Do you accept insurance for therapy sessions held at your White Plains office?

We strive to provide accessible care. While we do not directly bill insurance companies, we can provide necessary documentation for you to submit to your insurance provider for potential reimbursement. Please contact our office to discuss your specific insurance coverage options.

Q5: What should I expect during my first therapy session at your White Plains location?

Your first session, often called an intake session, will involve a conversation with your therapist to understand your concerns, goals for therapy, and background information. This session typically lasts longer than subsequent sessions. It's an opportunity for us to learn about you and for you to learn about our therapeutic approach. We aim to make you feel comfortable and heard from the start.
