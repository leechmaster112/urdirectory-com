Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How often should I get an alignment service for my vehicle?

A1: It's generally recommended to get a wheel alignment service every 6,000 to 10,000 miles or as specified in your vehicle's owner's manual. However, signs like uneven tire wear, a crooked steering wheel, or the vehicle pulling to one side often indicate the need for an alignment sooner.

Q2: What are the signs that my brakes need repair?

A2: Common signs include squealing or grinding noises when braking, longer stopping distances than usual, the brake warning light illuminating on the dashboard, vibrations in the brake pedal or steering wheel during braking, and a spongy or soft brake pedal feel.

Q3: How long does a typical brake repair service take?

A3: The time required for brake repair depends on the specific issue. Simple tasks like replacing brake pads might take 30-60 minutes per axle, while more complex repairs like brake caliper service or ABS system diagnosis could take longer, typically ranging from 1 to 3 hours or more.

Q4: Can you do brake and alignment services on all types of vehicles?

A4: Yes, our skilled technicians are experienced with a wide variety of vehicles, including domestic, import, and commercial vehicles commonly found in the Raleigh area. We use specialized equipment suitable for different makes and models.

Q5: Is it necessary to get both brake services and alignments regularly?

A5: While not strictly required at the same time unless a related issue is found, regular maintenance for both brakes and wheel alignment is crucial for your vehicle's safety and performance. Following the recommended service schedule for both helps ensure reliable operation and extends the life of your components.
