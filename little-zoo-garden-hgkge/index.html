Little Zoo Garden Capybara Cafe Bangkok

Little Zoo Garden Capybara cafe is a family-friendly animal cafe and mini-zoo located in Bangkok, Thailand, where visitors can interact with a variety of exotic and domestic animals in a relaxed setting, including capybaras, goats, rabbits, hedgehogs, dogs like Chihuahuas and corgis, cats, meerkats, deer, wallabies, birds such as cockatiels and cockatoos, tortoises, nutria, ducks, turtles, musk rats, bamboo rats, and more. It's designed as a petting and feeding garden at our capybara cafe, offering hands-on experiences like feeding, petting, and holding animals, making it popular for families, kids, and animal lovers. The venue combines outdoor and indoor areas with a cafe for drinks and snacks, allowing unlimited stay once inside.

FAQ

What animals can we meet at your cafe?Our mini-zoo at 276 On Nut 17 Alley is home to capybaras, meerkats, raccoons, and other friendly animals you can interact with.

Do I need to make a reservation?We highly recommend making a reservation by calling 092 435 1115 to ensure entry, especially on weekends.

Is there an entry fee for the animal zone?Yes, there is an admission fee for the animal interaction area, which helps with the care of our animals. Cafe purchases are separate.

What are your operating hours?Our hours can vary. Please check our official website at https://littlezoogarden.com for the most current schedule before your visit.

Is the venue suitable for young children?Yes, Little Zoo Garden Capybara Cafe is a family-friendly destination. Children must be supervised by an adult at all times for their safety and the animals' well-being.
