M V & Sons

MV & Sons Forklift Training Services is a leading, multi-accredited vocational training provider specializing in forklift operation, construction plant machinery, and powered access equipment. With over a decade of industry expertise, we bridge the skills gap in the UK industrial sector by providing high-quality, safety-compliant training tailored for both individual career seekers and corporate workforces. As a premier NPORS, IPAF, CPCS, and BRITTOp accredited center, we ensure that every candidate meets the rigorous safety standards required on modern UK construction sites and warehouse environments. Our training portfolio is comprehensive, ranging from Counterbalance and Reach truck operation to heavy machinery like 360° Excavators, Telehandlers, and Forward Tip Dumpers. We take pride in our inclusivity; our multilingual instructors offer training in Lithuanian, Polish, Russian, and Romanian, ensuring that language barriers never compromise workplace safety or certification quality. Whether you are a novice looking for a career change, an experienced operator requiring a refresher course, or a professional seeking an NVQ Level 2 assessment to transition from a Red to a Blue Competent Operator Card, MV & Sons provides the technical knowledge and hands-on experience necessary for success.
