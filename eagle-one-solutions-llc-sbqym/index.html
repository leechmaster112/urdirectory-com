Eagle One Solutions, LLC

Eagle One Solutions, LLC is a fully certified biohazard and trauma scene cleanup company serving Maryland, Washington DC, Virginia, and surrounding areas with 24/7 emergency response services. With over two decades of specialized experience in biohazardous cleanup and hoarding remediation, Eagle One Solutions provides compassionate, regulation-compliant restoration services during life's most challenging moments.Located at 6400 Cherrywood Lane, Unit 2424, Greenbelt, MD 20770, Eagle One Solutions works closely with families, property managers, insurance providers, and social services to deliver seamless, insurance-billable solutions. Whether responding to a residential emergency or handling commercial decontamination projects, Eagle One Solutions brings technical expertise, compassionate service, and unwavering professionalism to restore safety and peace of mind. For immediate 24/7 assistance or a free consultation, contact Eagle One Solutions at (240) 733-2295. Led by owner Lonnie Johnson, a Temple University alumnus with extensive expertise in Project Management, Risk Management, and Human Resources, the company combines technical precision with a people-focused approach that prioritizes dignity, discretion, and safety for every client. Eagle One Solutions specializes in comprehensive biohazard cleanup services performed in strict accordance with OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens Standard (29 CFR 1910.1030) and CDC guidelines. Their professional services include blood and biohazard cleanup, crime scene decontamination, suicide and unattended death cleanup with decomposition remediation, hoarding cleanup and remediation, pest contamination cleanup, and advanced odor remediation that eliminates odors at the molecular level. All cleanup operations utilize EPA-approved disinfectants, HEPA-filtered equipment, and proper medical waste disposal through licensed facilities. The company also provides certified emergency vehicle decontamination services for law enforcement, fire, and EMS agencies, ensuring compliance with regulatory standards.
