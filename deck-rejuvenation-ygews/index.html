### Our Core Service: Timber Deck Restoration and Maintenance

At Deck Rejuvenation, our expertise lies in the complete lifecycle management of timber decks. We offer a range of services, from initial assessment and deep cleaning to comprehensive restoration and ongoing maintenance. Our process is meticulous, ensuring that your deck is not only revitalised in appearance but also structurally sound and protected against future damage.

Deep Cleaning: The foundation of any effective deck restoration begins with a thorough clean. Over time, decks accumulate dirt, grime, pollen, algae, moss, and residue from weather exposure. Our specialised cleaning solutions and techniques are designed to remove these contaminants without damaging the wood grain, preparing your deck perfectly for the next stages.

Deck Repair and Refurbishment: We identify and address any structural issues, such as loose boards, splintering, deep scratches, or minor rot. Using appropriate wood fillers and techniques, we restore damaged areas, ensuring your deck is safe and level for walking and relaxing.

Sealing and Protection: This is where Deck Rejuvenation's focus on long-term performance truly shines. We apply high-quality, UV-stable, breathable sealers or stains that penetrate the wood, providing essential protection against moisture ingress, fungal attack, and the harsh Australian sun. This step is crucial for extending the lifespan of your timber deck.

Maintenance Packages: To keep your deck looking fresh between major refurbishments, we offer tailored maintenance packages. These include regular cleaning, minor repairs, and the reapplication of protective coatings, ensuring your investment continues to perform beautifully.
