Odessa Steel

Odessa Steel is a full-service steel supplier and fabricator serving Midland, Odessa, and the Permian Basin since 2009. We stock structural steel — beams, angle iron, channel, square tubing, pipe, and plate — plus metal roofing panels in R-panel, corrugated, and standing seam profiles with 40+ colors. Contractors, ranchers, and builders count on us for metal building components, steel fencing supplies, garage doors, and precision waterjet cutting from our in-house fabrication shop, usually turned around the same week. Free delivery within 50 miles of our Midland yard, written quotes within 4 business hours, price matching on identical material, and tiered contractor pricing.
