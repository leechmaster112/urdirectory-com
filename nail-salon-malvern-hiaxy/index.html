## Our Services

We offer a comprehensive range of nail services designed to cater to both fingernails and toenails:

Manicures: Experience the art of nail care with our signature manicures. We offer classic, deluxe, and express options, including nail shaping, cleaning, cuticle care, nail polishing, and more. Choose from a wide array of vibrant colors, sophisticated ombre effects, or classic French manicures to perfectly match your style.

Pedicures: Indulge your feet with our luxurious pedicure services. From basic shaping and filing to deep cleansing and nourishing treatments, our pedicures are designed to leave your feet feeling soft, smooth, and revitalized. We offer express pedicures for quick touch-ups and deluxe options including paraffin wraps and exfoliation for ultimate relaxation.

Nail Extensions: Elevate your nail game with our professional nail extension services. Using techniques like acrylic, gel, or dip powder, we can create the perfect length and shape for your desired look. We offer meticulous nail art and design services to transform your extended nails into a canvas of beauty.

Color and Design: We specialize in nail coloring and intricate designs. From classic solid colors to ombre gradients, French tips, marble effects, and elaborate nail art, our creative team can bring your nail vision to life. We use high-quality, long-lasting polish and gel systems to ensure vibrant, chip-resistant results.

Regular Maintenance: Keeping your nails looking their best requires consistent care. We recommend regular touch-up appointments to maintain your polish, shape, and overall nail health. Our friendly team is always ready to assist you with scheduling these essential maintenance visits.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Do you offer nail services for both men and women?

A1: Absolutely! At Nail Salon Malvern, we welcome everyone. While our services are tailored to enhance the natural beauty of all clients, we offer a range of options suitable for both men and women, including classic shapes, colors, and basic maintenance.

Q2: What is the typical duration of a standard manicure or pedicure?

A2: A standard manicure usually takes around 30-45 minutes, while a standard pedicure typically lasts 45-60 minutes. Deluxe or express options may vary slightly in duration.

Q3: Do you provide nail polish and other products for take-home use?

A3: Yes, we offer a selection of high-quality nail polishes and other take-home products for your convenience. You can inquire about our specific offerings when you visit or call us.

Q4: Can I schedule appointments online?

A4: While we primarily manage appointments via phone, our website provides contact information and details. You can call us at (484) 26-5290 to schedule your appointment directly.

Q5: What should I do to prepare for my nail appointment?

A5: For your comfort and the best results, please bring your old nail polish remover if you have sensitive skin. We recommend avoiding nail polish for 24-48 hours before your appointment to allow your natural nails to breathe. We will handle the rest!
