Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What conditions do you treat at your Grand Rapids location?

A: Our Grand Rapids center specializes in treating all types of venous conditions, including varicose veins, spider veins, chronic venous insufficiency, and telangiectasias. We provide comprehensive care for both cosmetic and medical vein concerns.

Q: How long is the recovery period after vein treatment?

A: Recovery time varies by treatment type. Most minimally invasive procedures allow patients to return to normal activities within 24-48 hours. We provide detailed post-treatment care instructions during your consultation.

Q: Are your treatments covered by insurance?

A: We coordinate with most insurance providers to determine coverage for medically necessary treatments. Our staff will verify your benefits during your initial consultation.

Q: What is the success rate for your vein treatments?

A: Our minimally invasive procedures boast high success rates with minimal recurrence. The exact outcome depends on individual factors, which we discuss during your personalized consultation.

Q: How often are follow-up appointments needed?

A: Most patients require only one follow-up visit within 30 days of treatment. We schedule these appointments based on your specific treatment plan and progress.
