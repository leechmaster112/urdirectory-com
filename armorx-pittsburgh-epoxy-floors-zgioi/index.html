Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of epoxy flooring do you offer at your Pittsburgh location?

We offer a comprehensive range of epoxy flooring systems designed for both industrial and decorative purposes. This includes standard industrial epoxy for high-traffic areas, heavy-duty epoxy resistant to chemicals and impact, decorative solid color epoxies, patterned epoxies, and options integrated with aggregates like quartz or aluminum for added texture and aesthetics.

Q2: How long does an epoxy floor installation typically take at your Pittsburgh business?

The timeline for an epoxy floor installation depends on the size of the area, the specific system chosen, and the level of preparation required. Generally, the actual application process can be completed over a single day, but factors like curing time (which varies by product) and necessary surface preparation mean the total turnaround for a project might span two to five days, depending on the extent of the work.

Q3: Is epoxy flooring suitable for residential garages in Pittsburgh?

Absolutely. Epoxy flooring is an excellent choice for residential garages in Pittsburgh. It provides a durable, protective surface that can withstand vehicle traffic, spills, and general wear. It's easy to clean, available in various colors and finishes, and can even hide minor imperfections or stains common in garage environments.

Q4: How do I maintain an epoxy floor installed by ArmorX Pittsburgh Epoxy Floors?

Maintaining an epoxy floor is relatively simple. Regular sweeping or dust mopping is sufficient for light cleaning. For tougher stains, a pH-neutral cleaner is recommended. Avoid using harsh solvents, abrasive cleaners, or tools that could scratch the surface. Proper maintenance will help preserve the appearance and longevity of your epoxy floor.

Q5: Can you match a specific color or pattern for my Pittsburgh business?

Yes, we can work with you to match a specific color or create a custom pattern for your epoxy flooring project in Pittsburgh.
