Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What kind of tours do you offer at your Las Vegas location?

We offer guided walking tours that showcase our collection of celebrity figures. Tours are available daily and can be tailored for groups. Our knowledgeable guides provide engaging commentary about the figures and the museum.

Q2: How long does a typical tour take?

A standard guided tour takes approximately 45 minutes to an hour, depending on the group size and the pace of the tour. We can adjust the duration based on your specific needs.

Q3: Can I take photos with the figures?

Absolutely! We encourage visitors to take photos with our figures. Several designated spots offer unique photo opportunities. Please be mindful of other visitors and museum rules while taking pictures.

Q4: Are there different ticket options available?

Yes, we offer individual admission tickets and group tour packages. Group rates are available for parties of 8 or more. You can purchase tickets online via our website or upon arrival at the museum.

Q5: What are your museum hours?

The museum is typically open Tuesday through Sunday from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. We recommend checking our website or calling (702) 555-9943 for the most current hours and any special event schedules before planning your visit.
