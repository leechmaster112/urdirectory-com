FAQ

Q: What is Scalp Micropigmentation (SMP)?A: Scalp Micropigmentation is a non-surgical cosmetic procedure that involves depositing pigment onto the scalp to create the illusion of a dense hair follicle pattern. It's designed to camouflage hair loss or thinning areas, giving the appearance of fuller hair.

Q: How long do SMP results typically last?A: SMP results are considered semi-permanent. The pigments fade over time due to natural skin cell turnover and sun exposure. A touch-up session is usually recommended annually to maintain optimal color and density at our Stockton clinic.

Q: Is the SMP procedure painful?A: While individual pain tolerance varies, most clients describe the sensation as a mild tingling or scratching feeling. We offer topical numbing creams before and during the procedure to ensure minimal discomfort at our Robinhood Drive location.

Q: How many SMP sessions are needed?A: Typically, achieving optimal results requires 2-3 sessions spaced several weeks apart. The exact number depends on the desired coverage, the extent of hair loss, and individual healing factors. Our Stockton team will create a personalized treatment plan.
