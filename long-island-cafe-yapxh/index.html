Long Island Cafe

Long Island Cafe is Battle Creek's favorite destination for authentic, homestyle comfort food. As a top-rated local Breakfast Restaurant and Lunch spot, we proudly serve generous portions of your favorites all day long, seven days a week (7:00 AM – 2:00 PM). You'll find us conveniently located on North Helmer Road in the 49037 zip code. Come in and try our famous signature dishes like the cheesy Stuffed Hashbrowns, Cinnamon Roll Pancakes, or a satisfying Reuben Melt. We are dedicated to providing the best breakfast and lunch in Battle Creek, MI, served hot, fresh, and friendly.
