Premium Plumbers

Premier Plumbers is a Gas Safe registered plumbing and heating company with over a decade of experience serving homeowners and landlords across Windsor and the surrounding Berkshire communities. Specialising in boiler installation, boiler repair, and central heating system maintenance, the certified gas engineers are equipped to handle everything from routine annual boiler services to urgent emergency callouts. Whether a property requires a new combi boiler replacement, a Worcester Bosch installation, a Vaillant boiler fitting, or a Viessmann system upgrade, every job is completed to the highest standard by fully qualified heating engineers. The team also covers central heating repairs, power flushes, radiator installation, and hot water system diagnostics — providing a comprehensive solution under one roof. Flexible 0% finance options make new boiler installations accessible for households on any budget, with free no-obligation quotes and transparent, upfront pricing as standard. Customers never face hidden costs or surprise charges. With 24/7 availability for heating breakdowns and boiler faults, Premier Plumbers ensures minimal disruption when it matters most - restoring warmth and hot water quickly and reliably. From first-time boiler replacements to ongoing plumbing maintenance contracts, the experienced team delivers consistent, trustworthy results for every customer.
