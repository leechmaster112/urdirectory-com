California Specialty Pharmacy, an Acelpa Health company

California Specialty Pharmacy (CSP), an Acelpa Health company, is a specialty pharmacy and infusion suite located on Hadley Street in uptown Whittier. We also provide home infusion across Los Angeles and Orange counties. Therapies include immune globulin (IVIG and SCIG), IV antibiotics, parenteral nutrition (TPN), heart failure infusions, biologic therapies, and oncology infusion support. Our pharmacists and nurses create a personalized treatment plan for every patient, help support with prior authorization, and stay in close contact with your physician. Accredited by ACHC and URAC. We accept referrals from hospitals, clinics, and physician offices.
