Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Do you only serve residential clients in New York?

A: While we value residential clients, our services are tailored for both residential and commercial properties throughout New York. We understand the specific needs of businesses located in the city and work closely with property managers and owners to maintain professional and welcoming environments.

Q2: What types of plants thrive best in New York's climate for landscaping?

A: We utilize a mix of hardy and adaptable plants suitable for New York's conditions. Our designs incorporate species known for their resilience to urban environments, varying light exposures (from full sun to shade), and the city's specific microclimates. We select plants that offer seasonal interest and require appropriate care for the local conditions.

Q3: How does your maintenance service work for a typical New York property?

A: Our maintenance plans are customized based on your property's size, plant life, and specific features. A typical service might include regular mowing, trimming hedges and shrubs, removing weeds, fertilizing, mulching beds, cleaning up debris, and inspecting for potential issues. We schedule these services based on your preferences and the changing seasons.

Q4: Can you help with small-space landscaping, like a balcony or rooftop garden in New York?

A: Absolutely! We have experience designing and installing compact yet impactful landscapes for balconies, terraces, and even rooftop gardens. We focus on maximizing greenery, incorporating suitable containers, vertical gardening techniques, and drought-tolerant plants to create beautiful, manageable green oases within the constraints of New York city living.

Q5: What should I consider for my New York garden in the winter?

A: Winter preparation is key. We advise on plant protection from harsh frosts, proper cleanup to prevent disease, and planning for spring growth. Our team can help you select winter-hardy plants, apply appropriate mulch for insulation, and ensure your irrigation system (if applicable) is protected or properly drained to prevent damage.
