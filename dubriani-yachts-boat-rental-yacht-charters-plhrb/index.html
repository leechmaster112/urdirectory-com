Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of yachts are available for rent at Dubriani Yachts?

Our fleet includes a diverse range of luxury yachts, from spacious catamarans and sleek motor yachts to specialized vessels suitable for dining cruises and watersports. We offer options to suit various group sizes and preferences, all meticulously maintained for your comfort.

Q2: How far in advance should I book a yacht for a charter?

While last-minute bookings are sometimes possible, we highly recommend reserving your yacht at least 2-3 days in advance, especially during peak seasons or for weekend charters at the Dubai International Marine Club. Booking online via our website or calling our office ensures prompt availability.

Q3: What is included in a yacht rental package?

All our yacht rental packages include professional crew, Captain, and all necessary safety equipment. Depending on the package, you may also receive complimentary soft drinks, water, and snacks. Luxury packages often include gourmet catering, alcoholic beverages, and watersports equipment. Please inquire for detailed inclusions.

Q4: Are there any age restrictions for guests on the yacht?

Generally, guests must be at least 18 years old to enjoy the yacht fully. Minors are welcome but must be accompanied by an adult. Our crew ensures a safe and enjoyable experience for all ages at the Dubai International Marine Club.

Q5: Can I customize my yacht charter itinerary?

Absolutely! We pride ourselves on offering personalized experiences. Our team works closely with you to design a unique itinerary tailored to your interests, whether it's exploring nearby coves, enjoying specific dining spots along the Dubai Marina coastline, or simply relaxing with panoramic views.
