Sleep.8 Bluewater

Sleep.8 Bluewater stands as the premier mattress store and bed specialist at one of the UK's most celebrated shopping destinations. Located within Bluewater Shopping Centre, this flagship store offers the widest selection of premium mattresses, luxury beds, and innovative sleep solutions designed to transform your rest experience. The expansive showroom features cutting-edge mattress technology including the firm hybrid Bennett 2.0 mattress, the supportive Glen Haven 2.0 mattress, and the luxurious Grether&Wells Heaven De Luxe and Magnificent mattress collections. Expert sleep associates provide personalised consultations to help customers discover the perfect mattress, bed frame, pillows, and sofa beds tailored to individual comfort preferences and support requirements. The store showcases an exceptional range of products including smart adjustable beds, therapeutic massage chairs, upholstered bed frames like the elegant Arina and Rachel beds, and versatile sofa beds such as the Lucky collection. With multilingual staff speaking Hindi, English, Lithuanian, Punjabi, Russian, Turkish, and Urdu, Sleep.8 Bluewater serves the diverse Kent community with unparalleled expertise. Open Monday through Saturday with extended hours, the store offers convenient access with excellent transport links and free parking for up to 13,000 vehicles. This premier mattress and bed store combines innovative sleep technology with personalised service, featuring smart adjustable bed areas where customers can experience customisable comfort solutions. From luxury hybrid mattresses to ergonomic sleep accessories, Sleep.8 Bluewater delivers comprehensive sleep solutions to enhance wellbeing throughout Kent and Greater London.
