Our accreditation reflects our commitment to excellence in patient care, utilizing the latest, minimally invasive techniques to address a wide range of venous conditions. We focus on providing effective solutions for common concerns such as spider veins, which are those delicate, thread-like veins often appearing on the face, legs, and ankles, and varicose veins, which are larger, often bulging veins that can cause discomfort, pain, swelling, or skin changes around the ankles and legs. These conditions, while sometimes purely cosmetic, can sometimes be associated with underlying venous reflux disease.

Our Union practice is equipped with cutting-edge technology and staffed by professionals who prioritize patient comfort and effective outcomes. We believe in a personalized approach, thoroughly understanding each patient's unique situation and goals before recommending a treatment plan. Our treatments are designed to be minimally disruptive to your daily life, often performed in-office with little downtime.

Common procedures offered at our Union center include sclerotherapy, where a solution is injected directly into the targeted veins, causing them to fade away over time. We also utilize various types of laser treatments, such as Nd:YAG laser, which uses targeted light energy to effectively treat spider veins and sometimes other vascular conditions. Radiofrequency ablation is another advanced option, particularly suitable for larger varicose veins, using heat to seal the affected vein shut, allowing blood to be rerouted through healthier vessels.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) - Metro Vein Centers Union

1. What types of vein problems do you treat at your Union location?

We specialize in treating both cosmetic and functional venous issues common in the Union area. This includes spider veins (small, red/blue veins often seen on the face and legs) and varicose veins (larger, twisted veins that can cause pain and swelling). We also address symptoms potentially linked to venous reflux disease.

2. Are the treatments at your Union clinic painful?

We prioritize patient comfort. While some mild tenderness or warmth might be felt during the procedure, our team uses techniques and potentially numbing agents to minimize discomfort. Most patients find the treatments well-tolerated.

3. How long does a typical vein treatment session take at your Union center?

The duration varies depending on the number and size of veins being treated. A simple session might take 30-45 minutes, while more extensive treatments could require up to a few hours. Our staff will provide a specific estimate during your consultation.

4. Is there downtime after vein treatment at your Union practice?

Downtime is typically minimal. You may experience some redness, swelling, or bruising at the treatment site, which usually resolves within a few days. Most patients can return to their normal activities immediately after a simple procedure, though strenuous activities might be advised to avoid for a short period.

5. Do I need a referral to schedule an appointment at your Union clinic?

While a referral is not always mandatory, we welcome patients who have questions or have been referred by their primary care physician. You can schedule your initial consultation directly with our team at the Union location.
