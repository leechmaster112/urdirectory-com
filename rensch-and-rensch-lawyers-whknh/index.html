Q2: How long do I have to file a personal injury claim in Omaha, Nebraska?

A2: In Nebraska, the statute of limitations for most personal injury claims is generally three years from the date the injury occurred or the date you discovered the injury. It is crucial to consult with an Omaha personal injury attorney promptly to ensure your claim is filed within this time frame.

Q3: Do I need to go to court for my personal injury case in Omaha?

A3: Many personal injury cases in Omaha are resolved through settlement negotiations without ever needing to go to trial. Our Omaha team is fully prepared for both settlement and trial, focusing on achieving the best result for you regardless of the path taken.
