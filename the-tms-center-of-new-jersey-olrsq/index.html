The TMS Center of New Jersey

The TMS Center of New Jersey is a specialized mental health clinic in Tinton Falls offering NeuroStar® Advanced Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS Therapy) therapy for depression, OCD, and anxiety. This FDA-cleared, non-invasive treatment uses targeted magnetic pulses to stimulate areas of the brain involved in mood regulation, providing relief for patients who have not responded to medication or psychotherapy. Treatments are performed while patients remain awake, with no anesthesia or downtime, allowing a quick return to daily activities. Led by experienced clinicians, the center delivers personalized care focused on safe, effective, drug-free mental health treatment.

Frequently Asked Questions
Q1: What is TMS therapy offered at your Tinton Falls location?

A1: TMS therapy is a non-invasive treatment that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate nerve cells in the brain, primarily targeting areas involved in mood regulation. It is an FDA-approved treatment for major depressive disorder and obsessive-compulsive disorder.

Q2: Who is a good candidate for TMS therapy at 55 Gilbert St N #2203, Tinton Falls, NJ 07701?

A2: Individuals who have not responded adequately to antidepressant medications or are seeking an alternative to medication may be good candidates. A thorough evaluation by our clinical team at our Tinton Falls facility will determine suitability.

Q3: What can I expect during a TMS therapy session at your Tinton Falls clinic?

A3: During a session, you will sit comfortably while a magnetic coil is placed on your head. You will feel a tapping sensation and hear clicking sounds. Most patients can read, listen to music, or relax during the treatment. Sessions typically last 20-40 minutes.

Q4: Is TMS therapy painful?

A4: TMS therapy is generally well-tolerated. Some patients experience mild discomfort at the treatment site, such as scalp discomfort or a headache, which usually subsides quickly. Our team at 55 Gilbert St N #2203, Tinton Falls, NJ 07701, will work to ensure your comfort throughout the process.

Q5: How many TMS therapy sessions will I need at The TMS Center of New Jersey?

A5: The typical course of TMS therapy involves daily sessions, five days a week, for approximately 4-6 weeks. The exact number of sessions will be determined by your individual treatment plan developed by our specialists at our Tinton Falls location.
