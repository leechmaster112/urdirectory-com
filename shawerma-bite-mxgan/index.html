Shawerma Bite

Shawerma Bite is Mason, Ohio's premier Mediterranean restaurant, bringing authentic Middle Eastern and Mediterranean cuisine to the heart of Montgomery County. Located at 9329 Mason Montgomery Rd, Mason, OH 45040, this best-in-class local restaurant specializes in traditional shawerma prepared with meticulous care and authenticity. The shawerma preparation begins over 24 hours before grilling, with carefully selected meat marinated in specialty sauces until fully saturated, then placed on a vertical skewer for slow roasting to perfection. Whether served on Shawerma Bite's signature smoked rice, atop fresh salads, in customizable bowls, or wrapped in pita bread or tortilla, every dish showcases the genuine flavors that make shawerma the best fast-casual item in the entire Middle East and Mediterranean region. With extensive vegetarian options including authentic falafel made with traditional methods, fresh hummus, baba ghanoush, and a variety of premium toppings and sauces, Shawerma Bite offers something delicious for every palate. What sets Shawerma Bite apart as the best restaurant in Mason, OH, is their unwavering commitment to authenticity and quality. Every protein option—from chicken shawerma and steak shawerma to gyro, shish tawook, and traditional falafel—is prepared with the same dedication to genuine Mediterranean flavors. Customers can customize their meals with fresh premium toppings including lettuce, tomatoes, onions, pickles, parsley, and jalapeños, plus specialty additions like cauliflower, tabbouleh, cornslaw, fattoush, hummus, and their famous garlic sauce. The restaurant also offers convenient sides including hummus with pita bread, garlic and chips, baba ghanoush, and seasoned french fries, along with full catering services for events of any size. Whether you're looking for a quick lunch, family dinner, or catering for your next event, Shawerma Bite's Mason location delivers the authentic Mediterranean grill experience that has made them a regional favorite. Whether you're dining in, taking out, or enjoying convenient food delivery through DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Grubhub, Shawerma Bite delivers an unparalleled Mediterranean dining experience to Mason and surrounding communities. Open Monday through Thursday from 11am to 9pm, Friday and Saturday from 11am to 10pm, and Sunday from 12pm to 9pm, Shawerma Bite invites you to experience the real taste of shawerma and discover why they're considered the best restaurant in Mason, OH.
