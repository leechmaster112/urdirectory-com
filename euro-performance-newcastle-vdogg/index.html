Frequently Asked Questions

What specific European car brands do you service at your Cardiff workshop?

Our workshop at 2/22 Munibung Rd specialises in a comprehensive range of European performance brands. This includes, but is not limited to, Audi, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Volkswagen, Skoda, Mini, and Porsche. Our technicians are trained specifically to work on the unique engineering of these vehicles.

Do you perform logbook servicing that maintains my vehicle’s manufacturer warranty?

Absolutely. We perform complete logbook servicing that adheres strictly to the manufacturer's specifications and schedules. Using quality parts and fluids, our certified technicians ensure your warranty remains valid while providing a more personalised and often more cost-effective alternative to a main dealer.

What kind of performance upgrades can you install at your Munibung Rd facility?

Our Cardiff workshop is equipped to handle a variety of performance enhancements tailored to European vehicles. This includes ECU remapping and chip tuning, performance exhaust systems, upgraded suspension components, and high-performance brake upgrades. We consult with you to understand your driving goals and recommend the best modifications for your specific vehicle.

How do I schedule a service or repair for my car?

Scheduling service is easy. You can call us directly at +61432553905 to speak with one of our advisors who will find a convenient time for you. Alternatively, you can visit our website at https://euronewcastle.com/ to learn more about our services and contact us online.

Do you offer loan cars or courtesy transfers for customers?

Yes, we offer a complimentary courtesy transfer service within the local Cardiff area to help minimise disruption to your day while your vehicle is being serviced at our Munibung Rd workshop. Please inquire about the availability of our loan car service when you book your appointment.
