Our Comprehensive Services for Nanaimo Residents

We offer a wide range of dental services designed to address all aspects of oral health. Whether you're seeking routine check-ups or more specialized care, our experienced team at Harewood Dental Clinic is equipped to handle your needs.

Dental Examinations and Cleanings

Regular dental examinations and professional cleanings are the foundation of good oral health. At our Nanaimo clinic, we provide thorough oral assessments to detect potential issues early, when they are most treatable. Our gentle cleaning procedures remove plaque and tartar buildup, helping to prevent gum disease and keep your teeth looking their best.

Fillings and Crowns

If you experience a tooth decay or damage, our skilled dentists can restore your tooth's function and appearance with expertly placed fillings or durable crowns. We utilize modern materials and techniques to ensure comfortable, long-lasting results.

Root Canal Therapy

When the inner structures of a tooth become infected or inflamed, root canal therapy can save the tooth and relieve pain. Our team is experienced in performing this procedure with precision and care, often minimizing discomfort through effective anesthesia and modern techniques.

Teeth Whitening

For those looking to enhance their smile's brightness, we offer professional teeth whitening treatments. These procedures are customized to achieve beautiful, natural-looking results while ensuring your comfort and safety.

Oral Surgery

In some cases, minor oral surgeries may be necessary to address issues like impacted teeth or gum disease. Our clinic is equipped to perform these procedures in-house, providing convenient and expert care for our Nanaimo patients.

Dental Implants

Missing teeth can affect your ability to eat, speak, and smile confidently. Dental implants offer a permanent solution to tooth loss. We provide implant services designed to restore both the function and aesthetics of your smile, tailored to your specific needs.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Beyond restorative care, we also offer various cosmetic dental services to enhance the appearance of your smile. From veneers to bonding, these treatments can address chips, gaps, discoloration, and other minor imperfections, boosting your confidence and overall smile aesthetics.

Dental Emergencies

Evenings, weekends, or holidays don't always align with regular office hours. That's why we understand the importance of addressing dental emergencies promptly. While our regular schedule applies, we encourage patients experiencing severe dental pain or trauma to contact our Nanaimo clinic during business hours.
