Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) for our Pittsburgh Studio

Q1: What is the difference between 3D and 4D ultrasound?

A: 3D ultrasound provides a three-dimensional still image of your baby, allowing you to see facial features clearly. 4D ultrasound builds on this by adding motion, giving you real-time video of your baby moving and interacting.

Q2: How early can I get an ultrasound at your Pittsburgh studio?

A: Typically, a good 3D or 4D image can be obtained starting around week 14-16 of pregnancy. However, optimal visualization often occurs between weeks 26-30. Please call our Pittsburgh location to discuss your specific situation.

Q3: Is the 3D/4D ultrasound safe for my baby?

A: Yes. The pulsed wave technology used for our 3D and 4D ultrasounds at our Pittsburgh studio emits very low levels of energy, well within the safety guidelines established by the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and the American Institute of Ultrasound in Medicine (AIUM). This type of ultrasound has been used safely for decades in medical diagnostics.

Q4: Do I need a doctor's prescription for a 3D/4D ultrasound at your studio?

A: While a 3D/4D ultrasound is considered an elective service, we operate under the guidance of healthcare providers. We require a referral from your doctor or a midwife. This ensures that the examination is appropriate and can be used for both bonding and any necessary medical assessment.

Q5: What should I expect during my appointment at the studio on Penn Ave?

A: Your appointment will typically last about 45 minutes. You will lie comfortably on an examination table while our trained technologist applies a warm gel to your abdomen and performs the scan. You'll be able to see the images on a monitor right away. We encourage bringing your camera to capture the precious moments. After the scan, you can purchase high-quality digital images or DVDs of your baby's unique features.
