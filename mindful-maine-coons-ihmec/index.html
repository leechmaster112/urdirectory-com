Frequently Asked Questions
Q1: What are WCF champion bloodlines and titled ancestry?

A1: WCF champion bloodlines refer to cats whose ancestors have achieved championship status in shows recognized by the World Cat Federation (WCF). Titled ancestry indicates that our breeding cats and their predecessors have earned specific titles through rigorous evaluation of their conformation, temperament, and health, demonstrating a high standard of breed quality.

Q2: How can I arrange a visit to see the kittens at 20660 Dawn Ct, Murrieta, CA 92562?

A2: We welcome prospective owners to visit us at our Murrieta location by appointment. Please contact us via our website or phone to schedule a convenient time to meet our cats and learn more about our available kittens.

Q3: What health guarantees do you offer for your Maine Coon kittens?

A3: All our kittens are vet-checked, vaccinated, and dewormed prior to going to their new homes. We also provide a health guarantee against congenital defects. Detailed information about our health protocols and guarantees is available on our website.

Q4: How are the kittens socialized at Mindful Maine Coons?

A4: Our kittens are raised in a home environment and are exposed to daily handling, various sights and sounds, and gentle interaction with our family from a very young age. This comprehensive socialization helps them develop into confident, well-adjusted, and affectionate companions.

Q5: What is the process for purchasing a kitten from Mindful Maine Coons?

A5: The process typically begins with an inquiry through our website or phone. We then discuss your needs and lifestyle to ensure a good match. If you decide to proceed, a deposit is required to reserve a kitten. We then keep you updated with photos and information until your kitten is ready to join your family.
