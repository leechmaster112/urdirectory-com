Hebert Counseling

Hebert Counseling is a trusted therapy practice proudly serving Richardson with compassionate, professional mental health services tailored to individuals, couples, and families. We specialize in drug addiction therapy and alcohol addiction therapy, providing supportive, evidence-based care to help clients achieve lasting recovery and personal growth. Our approach focuses on understanding each client’s unique needs while providing a safe, supportive, and judgment-free environment. If you’re looking for professional therapy services in Richardson, trust Hebert Counseling for compassionate guidance, effective treatment, and a path toward lasting well-being.
