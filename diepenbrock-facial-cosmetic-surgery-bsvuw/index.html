Our Services

At Diepenbrock Facial Cosmetic Surgery, we offer a comprehensive range of facial rejuvenation procedures designed to address various concerns and enhance your natural beauty. Our focus is on providing personalized solutions for patients seeking facial enhancement in the Fort Wayne area.

1. Facelift (Rhytidoplasty)

A facelift is a surgical procedure designed to lift and tighten the skin of the lower face and neck. Dr. Ryan D performs facelifts to address jowls, sagging jowls, deep nasolabial folds, and a weak jawline. The procedure involves lifting the underlying facial muscles and tissues, resulting in a more youthful and refreshed appearance. We utilize advanced techniques to ensure natural-looking results with minimal scarring.

2. Eyelid Surgery (Blepharoplasty)

Blepharoplasty addresses excess skin, fat, and muscle in the upper and lower eyelids. This procedure can significantly improve the appearance of the eyes, making the face look more alert and youthful. Dr. Ryan D performs both upper and lower eyelid surgery with precision, removing excess skin and fat to create a more open and refreshed eye area.

3. Facelift with Neck Lift

Often performed in conjunction with a facelift, a neck lift targets the platysma muscle and the jowl area. This procedure lifts and tightens the neck skin and underlying tissues, eliminating bands in the neck and smoothing the jawline. The result is a comprehensive facial rejuvenation that addresses both the upper and lower face.

4. Brow Lift (Forehead Lift)

A brow lift corrects a drooping forehead and hooded eyelids, which can make a person look tired or angry. This procedure lifts the eyebrows and smooths the forehead, creating a more youthful and expressive upper face. Dr. Ryan D uses endoscopic techniques to minimize scarring and ensure precise results.

5. Facelift with Fat Grafting

To enhance the natural contours of the face and ensure long-lasting results, we offer fat grafting in conjunction with facelifts. Fat is harvested from areas like the abdomen or thighs and carefully re-injected into the face to restore volume, smooth wrinkles, and improve overall facial harmony.

6. Facelift with Ear Surgery (Otoplasty)

For patients concerned about the position of their ears, we offer otoplasty as part of a facial rejuvenation plan. This procedure can correct protruding ears or asymmetry, creating a more balanced and harmonious facial profile.

7. Facelift with Chin Augmentation

A chin augmentation can enhance the definition of the jawline and balance the facial features. We offer both surgical and non-surgical options for chin enhancement, tailored to each patient's specific needs and aesthetic goals.

8. Facelift with Dermal Fillers

To complement surgical facial rejuvenation, we utilize dermal fillers to restore volume, smooth wrinkles, and enhance facial contours. These non-surgical treatments can be performed before or after surgery to achieve optimal facial harmony.

9. Facelift with Laser Skin Resurfacing

Laser skin resurfacing can improve skin texture, tone, and elasticity, making it an excellent complement to surgical facial rejuvenation. This procedure helps minimize fine lines, wrinkles, and scars, resulting in smoother, more youthful-looking skin.

10. Facelift with Botox Injections

Botox injections can help reduce the appearance of dynamic wrinkles, such as forehead lines and crow's feet. When used in conjunction with a facelift, Botox can help maintain a smooth, natural-looking appearance.
