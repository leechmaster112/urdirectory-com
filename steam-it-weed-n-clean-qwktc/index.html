Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Do you use any chemicals for weed control in Vancouver?

A1: Absolutely not! Steam-It! Weed-N-Clean exclusively uses high-heat, low-pressure steam technology. This method effectively kills weeds by cooking and dehydrating them without introducing any chemicals into your Vancouver property or the surrounding environment.

Q2: How does steam kill weeds effectively?

A2: Our specialized steam equipment heats water to a very high temperature. This superheated steam penetrates the soil and plant material, reaching the roots of the weeds. The intense heat cooks the plant cells, denaturing proteins and disrupting the cellular structure, ultimately killing the weed and preventing future growth. This process is highly effective against a wide range of weeds common in Vancouver.
