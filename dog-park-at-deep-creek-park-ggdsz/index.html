Dog Park at Deep Creek Park

Open daily 6 a.m. to 6 p.m., the fully fenced dog park at Deep Creek Park is one of Chesapeake's better-equipped off-leash stops, with water access inside the fence plus seating and waste bags for owners. A one-mile perimeter trail connects to the Dismal Swamp Canal Trail, and the surrounding park adds an accessible playground, picnic shelters, and lighted ball fields. Entry is free. Hours, photos, and directions are on its Doggie Park Near Me listing.
