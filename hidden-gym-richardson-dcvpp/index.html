Hidden Gym Richardson

Hidden Gym Richardson is a full-service gym in Richardson, Texas serving Richardson, Dallas, Plano, Garland and nearby communities. Members have access to strength and powerlifting equipment, cardio, functional training, HIIT tools, personal training and full locker rooms. The location is also home to Mezger Martial Arts, led by UFC and combat-sports veteran Guy Mezger, with MMA and martial arts training for beginners and experienced athletes. Hidden Gym offers flexible single-club and multi-club membership options plus free trial opportunities for people seeking a serious, supportive training environment.
