One Emergency Dentist - Dental Clinic

One Emergency Dentist Manchester is a prominent dental clinic based at 283 Middleton Road, Prestwich, Manchester, M8 4LY, United Kingdom. As part of the One Emergency Dentist network, this Manchester location is committed to providing exceptional emergency and cosmetic dental care around the clock, operating 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. Whether patients are dealing with a sudden toothache in the middle of the night or seeking a complete smile makeover, this dental clinic ensures expert treatment is always within reach. The practice holds an impressive 4.9-star rating on Google Reviews, reflecting the consistently high standard of care delivered to patients across Greater Manchester. The dental clinic is staffed by a team of more than 20 experienced dentists who collectively bring over 80 years of combined expertise to the practice. The team is led by several distinguished professionals, including Dr. Mustafa, an Invisalign provider specialising in smile makeovers and restorative dentistry; Dr. Amir, a cosmetic dentist with particular skill in veneers and teeth whitening; Dr. Wakkas, a surgical dentist experienced in oral surgery, cosmetic dentistry, and dental implants; and Dr. Zain, an emergency dentist who specialises in dental trauma and pain management. Together, this dedicated team serves over 1,000 happy patients each month, handling everything from routine emergency cases to complex cosmetic transformations. A defining feature of One Emergency Dentist Manchester is its hassle-free, no-registration policy. Patients do not need to be registered with the practice to receive treatment, and walk-in appointments are always welcome. In many instances, patients can be seen in as little as 15 minutes, which is essential when dealing with urgent dental emergencies where every moment counts. The clinic further supports accessibility by offering a reduced emergency consultation fee of just £20, discounted from the standard £50, making it easier for anyone experiencing dental distress to seek immediate professional help. The comprehensive range of emergency dental services provided at this Manchester dental clinic includes treatment for toothache, broken or chipped teeth, lost fillings or crowns, dental abscesses, trauma, wisdom tooth pain, lost implants or veneers, root canal treatment, and tooth extraction. Same-day appointments are available for patients experiencing dental pain, ensuring that urgent issues are addressed swiftly and effectively without unnecessary delays. In addition to its emergency services, One Emergency Dentist Manchester offers a full suite of cosmetic dentistry treatments aimed at helping patients achieve the smile they have always wanted. Available cosmetic options include professional teeth whitening, custom porcelain veneers, composite bonding, and Invisalign clear aligners. The clinic's before-and-after smile gallery showcases remarkable patient transformations, including composite bonding procedures completed in just two visits and porcelain veneer restorations achieved over five visits. Flexible payment plans, including 0% interest financing on cosmetic treatments, make these services affordable and accessible to a broad range of patients. One Emergency Dentist Manchester serves an extensive area across Greater Manchester, covering neighbourhoods and towns including Rochdale, Oldham, Blackley, Eccles, Radcliffe, Bolton, Prestwich, Ancoats, Bury, Chadderton, Failsworth, Swinton, Salford, Cheetham Hill, Crumpsall, and Middleton. This wide service area ensures that residents throughout the region have convenient access to high-quality dental care, whether they need emergency treatment or cosmetic enhancement. Conveniently located just minutes from Manchester city centre, the clinic operates Monday through Sunday from 07:00 to 23:00, with emergency dental services available beyond these standard hours. The practice takes particular pride in creating a warm, welcoming, and judgement-free environment, especially for nervous patients or those who may have avoided the dentist for an extended period. The team is passionate about making dentistry more accessible, less intimidating, and genuinely comfortable for every patient who walks through the door. To schedule an appointment, patients can contact the clinic directly at 07427 722427, book online through the website, or submit an appointment request via the online contact form.
