Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: How do I apply to adopt a French Bulldog puppy from Kingdom Frenchies?

A: We believe in placing our puppies in homes where they will be cherished and cared for properly. Prospective families interested in adopting from Kingdom Frenchies should first visit our website to learn about our breeding standards and the application process specific to our Millersburg location. We typically require an application to review the family's experience with dogs, living situation, and commitment level before considering an application.

Q2: What health guarantees do you offer on your puppies?

A: The health and well-being of our puppies are paramount. Kingdom Frenchies provides a health guarantee on our puppies born at our Millersburg facility. This guarantee covers congenital defects and ensures that we stand behind the quality of our breeding program. Specific details regarding the guarantee period and conditions are outlined for families who inquire directly through our website or phone line about puppies available in the Millersburg area.

Q3: Do you perform health testing on your breeding French Bulldogs?

A: Absolutely. Responsible breeding requires a commitment to genetic health. At Kingdom Frenchies, we conduct thorough health testing on our breeding stock for conditions commonly associated with the French Bulldog breed. This includes screening for brachycephalic traits, patellar luxation, eye problems, and other hereditary conditions. Our testing protocols are designed to minimize the risk of passing on genetic health issues to our puppies, ensuring healthier litters for families in our Millersburg community.

Q4: Are your French Bulldog puppies available for delivery outside of Millersburg?

A: While our primary focus and service area is families residing near our Millersburg location, we understand that sometimes prospective owners live further away. We do offer delivery services for puppies, subject to our policies and the specific circumstances of each sale. However, due to our commitment to local families and the careful handling required for French Bulldog puppies, we prioritize placements within our immediate service area first. Please inquire directly via our website or phone for details on delivery options for puppies available from our Millersburg base.

Q5: What can you tell me about raising a French Bulldog puppy?

A: French Bulldogs are known for being intelligent, playful, and adaptable companions. Raising a French Bulldog puppy requires commitment, positive reinforcement training, and socialization from an early age. We provide detailed information and guidance to families adopting from us, drawing upon our experience raising our own Frenchies at our Millersburg home. We are available to answer questions and offer support during the puppy-raising journey for families in the Millersburg area.
