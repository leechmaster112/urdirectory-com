Tropix Pools

Tropix Pools is a Dallas-based company specializing in comprehensive pool and outdoor living services. Established in 1998, they have grown to become a premier provider in the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex, offering a range of services from routine maintenance to custom new pool construction. The company prides itself on its CPO (Certified Pool Operator) certified technicians, customer-focused approach, and dedication to quality craftsmanship. Services Offered Tropix Pools offers a wide variety of services for residential and commercial pools, including: 1. Pool Maintenance and Cleaning: Full-Service Monthly Plans: Includes chemical balancing, debris removal (skimming and vacuuming), brushing walls and tiles, cleaning skimmer and pump baskets, backwashing filters, and equipment checks. Chemical-Only Service: For customers who prefer to handle the physical cleaning themselves but want professional chemical balancing. Specialty Services: Such as deep filter cleaning and acid washing. 2. Pool Repair and Equipment Service: Equipment Repairs: Diagnosing, repairing, and replacing various pool equipment, including pumps, filters, heaters, and salt systems. Leak Detection: Professional services to identify and fix leaks. Tile and Coping Repair: Services to repair or replace damaged pool tiles and coping. Pool Plastering and Replastering: Repairing cracks and other structural damage. 3. New Pool Construction: Custom Pool Design and Build: A complete service for designing and constructing new, custom-built swimming pools. Award-Winning Design Consultation: Working with clients to create their dream backyard oasis. Project Management: On-site managers to keep clients informed throughout the construction process. 4. Pool Remodeling and Renovation: Full Pool Renovation: Updating the look and function of existing pools with new plaster, tile, coping, and features. Water Features: Installation of features like waterfalls and fountains. LED Lighting: Upgrading pool lighting to modern, energy-efficient LED systems. 5. Outdoor Living: Backyard Transformations: Designing and building comprehensive outdoor living spaces that complement the pool. Features: Including outdoor kitchens, pavilions, pergolas, fire pits, and turf installation.
