Dog Park at William Davies Park

Three separate play areas set the dog park at William Davies Park apart, giving small and large dogs their own fenced space with extra room to spread out. Wooded paths and shade trees keep parts of the park cooler in Charlotte's humid summers, and water stations with bowls are on site. Open 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. with free entry, serving south Charlotte out toward Pineville and Matthews. Hours, photos, and directions are on its Doggie Park Near Me listing.
