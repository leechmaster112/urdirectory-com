What is Click-Through Rate (CTR) Optimization?

CTR is the ratio of the number of clicks on your ad to the number of times your ad is shown (impressions). A higher CTR generally signals to search engines (like Google) that your ad is relevant and appealing to users searching for your products or services. While a high CTR doesn't guarantee top ad rankings, it's a positive ranking factor and often leads to lower costs per click and better overall return on investment (ROI). Webido CTR focuses on optimizing your ads, keywords, landing pages, and targeting to achieve the best possible CTR for your specific business goals, all managed from our Austin location.
