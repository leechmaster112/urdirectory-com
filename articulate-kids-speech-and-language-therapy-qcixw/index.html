Our Assessment Process

The journey often begins with a thorough assessment. We conduct detailed evaluations to understand the child's specific difficulties. This may involve parent questionnaires, observations, standardized testing, and analysis of speech and language samples. Our assessments are designed to be child-friendly and informative, providing a clear picture of the child's strengths and areas for development.

Our Therapy Approach

Based on the assessment findings, our therapists develop personalized treatment plans. Our therapy sessions are engaging and fun, using play-based and evidence-based techniques to target specific goals. We focus on building foundational skills, improving articulation and fluency, expanding vocabulary and grammar, enhancing pragmatic (social) language skills, and developing strategies for effective communication. Therapy is delivered one-on-one with the child, and we provide parents with practical strategies and resources to support progress at home.
