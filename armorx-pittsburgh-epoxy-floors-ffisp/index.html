Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of epoxy flooring do you offer in Pittsburgh?

A1: We offer a comprehensive range of epoxy flooring options for Pittsburgh clients, including solid color epoxy, metallic epoxy, flake epoxy (with various aggregate sizes), stained concrete epoxy, and custom color blends. We can discuss the best option for your specific project and location.

Q2: How long does an epoxy floor installation typically take in your Pittsburgh location?

A2: The duration of an epoxy floor installation depends on the size of the area, the complexity of the design, and the specific product chosen. Generally, the actual installation process takes 1-3 days, but the full curing time for the epoxy can take 5-10 days before heavy traffic or vehicle use is recommended.

Q3: Is epoxy flooring suitable for high-traffic areas like retail stores or restaurants in Pittsburgh?

A3: Absolutely. Epoxy flooring is exceptionally durable and highly resistant to heavy traffic, spills, and chemicals, making it an excellent choice for retail stores, restaurants, showrooms, and other high-traffic commercial spaces in Pittsburgh.

Q4: Can epoxy flooring be installed over existing cracks or imperfections in the concrete at 241 Bascom Ave?

A4: While minor imperfections might be somewhat concealed, significant cracks or uneven areas in the concrete substrate need to be repaired before applying epoxy. Our team at 241 Bascom Ave will assess your concrete thoroughly and recommend necessary repairs to ensure a smooth, durable, and professional finish.

Q5: How do I maintain my newly installed epoxy floor in Pittsburgh?

A5: Maintaining an epoxy floor is relatively simple. Regular sweeping or dust mopping is sufficient for light cleaning. For spills, prompt wiping is recommended. Using appropriate cleaners (usually a pH-neutral cleaner) will help preserve the finish. Avoid harsh solvents, abrasive tools, or dragging heavy equipment, as these can potentially damage the surface.
