Frequently Asked Questions

A2: A standard basic manicure typically takes around 20-30 minutes, while our deluxe manicure usually takes 30-45 minutes. Pedicures vary depending on the chosen service; a basic pedicure might take 30-45 minutes, and a deluxe or specialized pedicure can take 45-60 minutes or more.

A3: Absolutely! We offer a wide variety of nail designs to suit any special occasion. From classic French manicures and solid colors to intricate nail art, glitter effects, ombre gradients, and seasonal designs, our skilled technicians can create the perfect look for your event.
