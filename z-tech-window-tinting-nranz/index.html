2. How long does a typical window tint installation take at Z Tech Window Tinting?

3. Why is professional installation important for window tinting?

Professional installation at our facility ensures the film is applied without bubbles, wrinkles, or debris trapped underneath. Our technicians have the expertise to handle the precise cutting and sealing required for a clean, durable, and legal installation that protects your vehicle's interior.

4. How do I care for my newly tinted windows after leaving your shop?

We recommend avoiding rolling down your windows and not washing the car for about three to five days after the service at 9825 W 55th St #D. This allows the adhesive to cure completely. After that, clean with a soft cloth and a non-ammonia-based cleaner.
