Frequently Asked Questions

1. What types of pool fences do you install in Las Vegas?

We specialize in installing various types of compliant pool fences, including vinyl-coated aluminum, solid vinyl, and glass privacy fences. Each style offers different aesthetic options while meeting safety standards.

2. How long does a typical pool fence installation take?

Most standard pool fence installations in Las Vegas take between 1-3 days, depending on the size of the pool, the complexity of the design, and site conditions. We provide a detailed timeline after your initial consultation.

3. Do I need a permit for a new pool fence in Las Vegas?

Yes, in most cases, a permit is required for new pool fence installation or replacement to ensure compliance with local safety codes. We handle the permit application process as part of our service.

4. What maintenance is required for a pool fence?

Maintenance is minimal but recommended. Regular cleaning to remove dirt and debris, checking for loose hardware, and inspecting for any signs of damage will help maintain your fence's appearance and integrity. We can provide specific care instructions for your fence type.

5. Can you repair existing pool fences?

Absolutely. We offer repair services for existing pool fences. Whether it's a broken picket, damaged gate latch, or post issues, our team can assess the problem and provide the necessary repairs to ensure your fence remains safe and functional.
