GC Fixings Ltd

GC Fixings Ltd is a family-run UPVC and glazing specialist serving homeowners across Ipswich, Copdock, Sproughton, Capel St Mary, Hadleigh, Hintlesham, Bramford, Burstall, and throughout Suffolk and Essex. With over 25 years of hands-on experience, owner Gareth Canham personally manages every survey, quotation, and installation — giving customers the confidence of dealing directly with a skilled professional from first contact through to completion. Window installation and replacement glazing form the heart of the business, with services covering double glazed window installation, A-rated energy efficient windows, misted unit repairs, and UPVC window frame repairs. Whether upgrading an entire property or addressing a single failed unit, every installation is carried out to the highest standard with quality materials designed for long-term performance. Door installation services span UPVC door fitting, external door replacement, and double glazed door installation, helping homeowners across Suffolk improve security, thermal efficiency, and kerb appeal in one straightforward upgrade. Conservatory design and installation is also available alongside conservatory roof replacement, conservatory leak repairs, and inefficient glazing panel upgrades that breathe new life into existing structures. The roofline division covers fascia installation, soffit replacement, bargeboard fitting, guttering, downpipe installation, and complete roofline upgrades — protecting properties from water ingress and weathering damage. Exterior cladding installation and residential gutter cleaning round out a comprehensive exterior home improvement service built around quality craftsmanship and genuine local expertise. GC Fixings Ltd combines family-run reliability with the experience and skill of a seasoned trade professional. Call 07754 096570 or visit gcfixings.co.uk for a free, no-obligation survey and quote.
