Q and A Impact Windows and Doors Kendall - Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What exactly are impact windows and doors?

A: Impact windows and doors are specially designed to resist high-velocity projectiles and extreme wind pressures associated with hurricanes and severe storms. They are constructed with strong frames and impact-resistant glass, often laminated or tempered, to prevent shattering and maintain structural integrity during a storm.

Q: Why should I consider installing impact windows and doors?

A: Impact windows and doors offer multiple benefits crucial for Miami homeowners. Primarily, they provide vital protection against hurricane damage, safeguarding your property, belongings, and loved ones. They also enhance home security, reduce noise pollution from the bustling Miami environment, and can significantly lower your energy bills by providing superior insulation against the hot climate.

Q: How long does a typical impact window or door installation take?

A: The duration depends on the scope of the project. A simple window replacement might take a few hours, while a full room or a more complex door installation could take a full day or longer. Our team will provide a specific timeline during your free consultation at our Kendall location.

Q: Do you offer repair services for existing impact windows and doors?

A: Yes, absolutely. Over time, even the most durable impact windows and doors can develop issues like broken seals, damaged frames, or malfunctioning hardware. Our technicians are trained to diagnose and repair a wide range of problems specific to impact products, helping you restore their functionality and protection.

Q: Are your products hurricane-proof?

A: Our impact windows and doors are specifically designed and installed to meet or exceed Florida's stringent building codes for hurricane resistance. While no product is completely "hurricane-proof" in the absolute sense, they are engineered to withstand the intense pressures and forces generated by Category 1 through 5 hurricanes, providing critical protection for your Miami home or business.
