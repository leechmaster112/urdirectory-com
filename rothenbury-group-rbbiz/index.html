Rothenbury Group

Rothenbury Group is a privately held diversified holding company that builds, owns, and operates permanent businesses across the Americas. The group acquires and develops operating companies in real estate services, professional services, staffing, media, and technology, and holds them for the long term. Rothenbury provides its businesses with capital, shared infrastructure, and operating discipline, then gives accountable operators the room to grow. The strategy favors permanence over quick exits. Build durable companies, run them well, and compound value across the portfolio over many years. Rothenbury Group is headquartered in Panama City and works across the Americas with partners and operators who share a long horizon. The purpose of the group is to create lasting enterprises that serve real customer needs and stand on their own strength. Patient ownership, accountable operators, and businesses built to last define how Rothenbury Group works.
