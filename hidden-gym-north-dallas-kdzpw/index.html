Hidden Gym North Dallas

Hidden Gym North Dallas is a strength, performance and martial arts gym at 17390 Preston Road in Dallas, serving North Dallas, Addison, Plano, Richardson and surrounding communities. The facility includes a curated weight room, competitive powerlifting equipment, cardio, 30-yard turf, heavy bags, combat mats and an octagon. Training includes personal training, MMA, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, Muay Thai, Krav Maga and programs offered with McSweeney Martial Arts. Recovery amenities include infrared saunas, chiropractic care, massage therapy, physical therapy and 3D body scanning. Request a free pass to tour the gym and explore membership options.
