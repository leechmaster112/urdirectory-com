Integrated Care Systems

Integrated Care Systems, an Acelpa Health company, is an infusion suite in Bakersfield, CA. Our infusion suite is on Truxtun Avenue in downtown Bakersfield. We provide infusion therapy on-site and at home across Kern County. Therapies include immune globulin (IVIG and SCIG), intravenous antibiotics, total parenteral nutrition (TPN), enteral nutrition, inotropic therapy for heart failure, and other specialty infusions. Our nurses and pharmacists build an individual treatment plan for every patient and stay in regular contact about supplies and progress. ACHC accredited. Referrals are accepted from hospitals, clinics, and physician offices.
