Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What exactly does a 3D/4D ultrasound show?

A1: A 3D ultrasound creates still, three-dimensional images of your baby, while a 4D ultrasound adds motion, allowing you to see your baby moving in real-time. These advanced scans provide detailed views of your baby's face, body, and movements, offering a unique bonding experience.

Q2: When during pregnancy can I get a 3D/4D ultrasound at your Pittsburgh studio?

A2: The optimal time for a 3D/4D ultrasound at our Pittsburgh location is typically between 14 and 36 weeks of pregnancy. This timeframe allows for the best image quality, as the baby is developed enough for clear features but still has enough amniotic fluid for movement.

Q3: Is the 3D/4D ultrasound safe for my baby?

A3: Yes, the 3D/4D ultrasound is considered safe. We use only sound waves, similar to standard 2D ultrasounds, and strictly follow safety guidelines set by the FDA and AIUM. The intensity of the sound waves used for elective 3D/4D imaging is well within established safety limits.

Q4: Do you offer keepsake packages?

A4: Absolutely! We offer various keepsake packages at our Pittsburgh studio. These typically include professionally printed images on high-quality materials like canvas, metal, or acrylic, as well as digital downloads for personal use. You can learn more about our specific keepsake options by contacting us at (412) 444-8742.

Q5: Do I need a doctor's prescription for a 3D/4D ultrasound in Pittsburgh?

A5: While a standard 2D ultrasound requires a prescription from your healthcare provider, our 3D/4D ultrasounds are typically offered as an elective service without a prescription. However, we always recommend discussing your desire for a 3D/4D ultrasound with your doctor during your prenatal care.
