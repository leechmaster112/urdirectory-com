Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of vein treatments do you offer at your Brick location?

We provide a range of advanced treatments including sclerotherapy, endovenous laser treatment (EVLT), radiofrequency ablation (RFA), and others, tailored to address conditions like spider veins, varicose veins, and chronic venous insufficiency.

Q2: Is the treatment process at your Brick clinic painful?

Most of our minimally invasive procedures are performed with local anesthesia or numbing agents, ensuring patient comfort during the treatment. While some mild discomfort or pressure might be felt, significant pain is typically avoided.

Q3: How long is the recovery period after vein treatment at your Brick center?

Recovery varies depending on the specific procedure and individual factors. Most patients can resume light activities within a day or two, with full recovery typically taking a few weeks. Our team will provide detailed post-treatment care instructions.

Q4: Are the treatments at your Brick location safe?

Yes, safety is a top priority at Metro Vein Centers Brick. Our procedures are performed by experienced vascular specialists using FDA-approved technologies in a state-of-the-art facility, following strict safety protocols.

Q5: Do I need a referral to schedule a vein consultation at your Brick office?

While a referral is not always mandatory, we welcome patients who may have been referred by their primary care physician. You can also schedule an initial consultation directly with our specialists at our Brick location.
