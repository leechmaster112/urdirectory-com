Big Heart ABA Virginia

Big Heart ABA Virginia offers compassionate, evidence-based autism therapy services for children and families in Sterling, VA and throughout Northern Virginia. Our team specializes in providing an experienced In-Home ABA Therapist, allowing children to receive effective ASD Therapy in the comfort of their own home. We begin with detailed functional behavior assessments to understand each child’s needs and strengths. From there, our specialists develop personalized behavior intervention plans focused on positive behavior modification, communication growth, and daily living skills. Our programs include Autism skills training and social skills training designed to help children improve interactions, independence, and confidence. We emphasize early intervention for autism, offering structured behavioral therapy for autism that supports meaningful developmental progress. Big Heart ABA Virginia also supports families through compassionate approaches to special needs childcare, creating a supportive environment where children can learn, grow, and thrive.
