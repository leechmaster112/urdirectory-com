delme Member Diving - Snorkeling & Scuba Diving Center

Member Diving is a Swiss diving school in Koh Samui, Thailand, offering exclusive scuba diving and snorkeling experiences for beginners and certified divers. With small group sizes (maximum 4 students per instructor), we guarantee personalized training, safety, and comfort at every step. Our professional scuba instructors have years of experience and know the best dive sites in the Gulf of Thailand, including Koh Tao, Sail Rock, and Koh Samui’s vibrant coral reefs. Whether you want to try your first Discover Scuba Diving experience, earn your SSI diving certification, or enjoy a family-friendly snorkeling tour, we provide the perfect balance of safety, fun, and adventure with Samui's most comfortable dive catamaran.

Frequently Asked Questions:

Q1: What types of diving courses does Member Diving offer at this location?

A1: At our Bophut facility, we offer a range of scuba diving courses from beginner to advanced levels, as well as guided snorkeling tours.

Q2: Are there any age restrictions for snorkeling or scuba diving?

A2: Generally, participants for snorkeling should be able to swim, and for scuba diving, there is typically a minimum age requirement of 10 years old, with parental consent for minors.

Q3: What is the best time of year for diving and snorkeling in Koh Samui?

A3: The waters around Koh Samui are generally excellent for diving and snorkeling year-round, with peak visibility often occurring during the drier months.

Q4: Do I need to have prior diving experience to join a snorkeling trip?

A4: No prior experience is necessary for our snorkeling trips. Our guides will provide all the necessary instructions and equipment.

Q5: How can I book a diving or snorkeling excursion at your Bophut location?

A5: You can book your excursions directly through our website at https://member-diving.com/en/ or by calling us at +66 80 149 0444.
