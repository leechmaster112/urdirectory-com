Camp Netimus For Girls

Located in Milford, PA, Camp Netimus for Girls was founded in 1930 and continues to provide an exciting and enriching summer camp experience for girls ages 7–16. We proudly serve families throughout the New York Tri-State Area and the Philadelphia Metro Area, offering a wide range of engaging activities including horseback riding, swimming, and more.
