Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What services do you offer at your Hackensack location?

We provide comprehensive vein treatment services including sclerotherapy, laser treatments, radiofrequency ablation, and microphlebectomy for both cosmetic and medical vein concerns.

Q: How long is the recovery period after vein treatment?

Most patients experience minimal downtime. You may return to normal activities within a day or two, with full recovery typically completed within one to two weeks depending on the procedure.

Q: Do I need a referral to schedule an appointment?

While referrals are not required, we welcome patients who have questions or concerns about their vein health and are happy to discuss treatment options during your initial consultation.

Q: What conditions do you treat at your Hackensack center?

We address a wide range of conditions including spider veins, varicose veins, chronic venous insufficiency, venous pain, and other venous disorders affecting the legs.

Q: How often should I schedule maintenance after treatment?

Most patients require minimal maintenance after completing their initial treatment course. We recommend annual check-ups to monitor your vein health and address any new concerns promptly.
