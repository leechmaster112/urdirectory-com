Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do you offer jet ski rentals for individuals?

A: Yes, we offer jet ski rentals for individuals at our location in Surfside. We provide various sizes and types of jet skis to suit different skill levels and preferences.

Q: What safety equipment is provided?

A: We provide all necessary safety equipment, including life jackets, helmets, and instructional gear. Safety is our top priority for all guests at Jetski’s World Miami.

Q: Can I rent a jet ski for a whole day?

A: Absolutely! We offer daily jet ski rentals for guests staying in the area. You can enjoy the water for as long as you like, subject to our operating hours.

Q: Do you offer guided tours?

A: Yes, we offer guided jet ski tours for those who prefer a more structured adventure. Our experienced guides will take you to the best spots in the Miami waters.

Q: What is your cancellation policy?

A: We require 24 hours notice for cancellations. If you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, we may charge a fee equivalent to the first hour of your rental.
