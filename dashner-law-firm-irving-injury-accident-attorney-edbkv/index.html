Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I get started with Dashner Law Firm?

A: To get started, simply contact us at (972) 635-4460 or visit our website at https://www.dashnerlaw.com/. We will schedule a free consultation to discuss your case and determine if we can assist you.

Q: What types of accident cases do you handle?

A: We handle a variety of accident cases, including car accidents, workplace injuries, slip and fall incidents, medical malpractice, and pedestrian accidents. If you've been injured due to someone else's negligence, we can help.

Q: How long do I have to file a claim?

A: The time limit to file a claim, known as the statute of limitations, varies by case. In most personal injury cases in Texas, the statute of limitations is two years from the date of the accident. It's crucial to contact us as soon as possible to protect your rights.

Q: Do I need to pay anything upfront to hire you?

A: No, we work on a contingency fee basis. This means you don't pay anything unless we win your case. Our fee is a percentage of the settlement or court award, and we only get paid if we secure a favorable outcome for you.

Q: How do you handle insurance companies?

A: We handle insurance companies directly, leveraging our experience to negotiate on your behalf. We ensure that your rights are protected and that you receive a fair settlement that covers all your damages.
