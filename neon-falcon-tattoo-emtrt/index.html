Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Do I need to book an appointment at Neon Falcon Tattoo?

We highly recommend booking an appointment to ensure you have dedicated time with an artist. Walk-ins may be accommodated if an artist is available, but scheduling in advance is appreciated.

Q2: How do I choose an artist at Neon Falcon Tattoo?

Visit our website https://www.neonfalcontattoo.com/ to view artist portfolios and bios. Discuss your ideas directly with artists during your consultation to find the best fit for your vision.

Q3: What is the cost of a tattoo at Neon Falcon Tattoo?

Prices vary based on size, complexity, detail, and the artist's time. We offer competitive rates and can provide a more accurate estimate during your consultation at our Lakewood studio.

Q4: How long does the tattoo process take?

The initial session typically takes several hours, depending on the design's complexity. Larger or more intricate pieces may require multiple sessions scheduled in advance.

Q5: Do you offer touch-ups or aftercare instructions?

Yes, we provide thorough aftercare instructions to help your tattoo heal properly. Minor touch-ups may be offered at a discounted rate within the first few months if needed.
