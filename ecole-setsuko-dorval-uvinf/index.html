Ecole Setsuko - Dorval

Ecole Setsuko is a leading institution in Dorval and the West Island of Montreal, dedicated to providing comprehensive massage therapy courses and holistic health education. Our accredited programs offer hands-on training in techniques like Swedish Massage and Shiatsu, ensuring that our students are well-prepared for successful careers. We emphasize personalized mentorship, small class sizes, and a holistic approach that integrates the mind, body, and spirit. With a focus on career readiness, our graduates are equipped with the skills and knowledge needed to excel. Join our community and transform your passion for wellness into a fulfilling profession. Enroll today and start your journey towards a rewarding career in holistic health and massage therapy!
