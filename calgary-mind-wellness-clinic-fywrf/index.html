Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What services do you offer at your Calgary clinic?

We provide individual therapy sessions, counseling services, and mental health assessments for adults and adolescents seeking support for various concerns including stress, anxiety, depression, relationship issues, and personal growth.

Q2: How can I schedule an appointment at your Calgary location?

You can schedule an appointment by calling our phone number or by booking online through our website. Our team will assist you in finding a suitable time that fits your schedule.

Q3: Are your therapists licensed professionals?

Yes, all our therapists and mental health professionals practicing at our Calgary clinic are licensed and highly qualified in their respective fields, ensuring you receive expert care.

Q4: What types of therapy are available?

We offer various therapeutic approaches including Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavioural Therapy (DBT), mindfulness-based therapies, and other evidence-based practices tailored to individual needs.

Q5: How long does a typical therapy session last?

Typically, our therapy sessions last approximately 50 minutes. The duration and frequency of sessions are determined based on individual needs and treatment goals.
