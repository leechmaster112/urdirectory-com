Location

Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care - Far North Central, located at 18206 Blanco Rd Suite 104, San Antonio, TX 78258, proudly serves the residents of this community. We are the first Petfolk clinic in this specific area, established and led by a dedicated team of expert veterinarians committed to providing exceptional care for your beloved pets.

At our Far North Central location, we combine comprehensive veterinary care with essential urgent services. Our facility is designed to be a one-stop solution for all your pet's health needs, from routine check-ups and vaccinations to unexpected illnesses or injuries.

Our Veterinary Services include:

Routine Care: We offer thorough physical examinations, core and non-core vaccinations, preventative parasite control (fleas, ticks, worms), and dental care recommendations. Laboratory Testing: On-site capabilities allow for quick analysis of routine blood work and urinalysis, aiding in prompt diagnosis. Health Monitoring: We emphasize preventative medicine and provide guidance on maintaining your pet's long-term health and wellness.

Our Urgent Care Services are available for pets experiencing sudden illness or distress outside of regular veterinary hours. We handle conditions such as trauma, acute vomiting or diarrhea, minor wounds, and other non-life-threatening emergencies.

Our team of experienced veterinarians is passionate about animal health and dedicated to providing compassionate, high-quality care tailored to the needs of pets and their owners in our immediate community. We strive to build lasting relationships based on trust and excellent pet healthcare outcomes.

For more information about our services or to schedule an appointment, please visit our website or contact us directly.
