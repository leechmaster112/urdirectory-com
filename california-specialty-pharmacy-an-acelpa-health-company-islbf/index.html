California Specialty Pharmacy, an Acelpa Health company

California Specialty Pharmacy (CSP), an Acelpa Health company, provides personalized specialty pharmacy and infusion therapy services in Arcadia, CA, and throughout San Gabriel Valley communities. Our experienced pharmacists and nurses specialize in immune globulin (IVIG and SCIG), IV antibiotics, parenteral nutrition (TPN), biologic therapies, and other complex infusions. We offer convenient treatment options in our outpatient infusion suites or in the comfort of patients' homes for those managing acute, chronic, complex, and rare conditions. Working closely with patients and their healthcare providers, our team coordinates care and provides ongoing clinical support throughout treatment. CSP is ACHC and URAC accredited.
