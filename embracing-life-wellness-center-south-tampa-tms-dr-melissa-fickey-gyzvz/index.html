What is TMS therapy and how is it performed at your Tampa location?

TMS, or Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation, is an FDA-cleared, non-invasive treatment for depression. At our clinic at 3333 W Kennedy Blvd #106, a trained technician administers the treatment by delivering gentle magnetic pulses to specific areas of the brain involved in mood regulation. You remain awake and alert in a comfortable chair during the approximately 20-minute session, and you can drive yourself home or back to work immediately afterward.

Who is a good candidate for TMS at Embracing Life Wellness Center?

TMS therapy is an excellent option for adults who have been diagnosed with Major Depressive Disorder and have not experienced satisfactory improvement from previous antidepressant medications. Dr. Melissa Fickey conducts a thorough evaluation at our South Tampa office to determine if TMS is the right treatment path for your specific situation.
