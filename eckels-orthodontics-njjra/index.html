What is the best age for a child to have an orthodontic evaluation?

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends an evaluation by age 7, which we provide at our practice.

How long do orthodontic treatments typically take?

Treatment time varies per patient, but most orthodontic treatments average between 18 to 24 months.
