Frequently Asked Questions:
Q1: What is Ceramic Pro and how does it protect my vehicle?

Ceramic Pro is an advanced nanotechnology-based ceramic coating that forms a semi-permanent, durable, and hydrophobic layer on your vehicle's paintwork. It provides superior protection against scratches, chemical stains, UV damage, and environmental contaminants, while also enhancing gloss and making your vehicle easier to clean.

Q2: How long does Ceramic Pro protection last?

The longevity of Ceramic Pro protection varies depending on the specific package applied. Our Ceramic Pro Gold package, for instance, comes with a lifetime warranty, offering the most comprehensive and long-lasting protection available. Other packages offer excellent durability for several years.

Q3: Is paint correction necessary before applying Ceramic Pro?

Yes, paint correction is a crucial step in the Ceramic Pro application process. It involves removing imperfections like swirl marks, scratches, and oxidation to ensure a flawless surface for the coating to bond to, maximizing its protective capabilities and aesthetic results.

Q4: How do I maintain a vehicle with Ceramic Pro coating?

Maintaining a Ceramic Pro coated vehicle is significantly easier than a conventionally waxed vehicle. Regular washing with pH-neutral soaps is recommended. The hydrophobic properties of the coating cause water and dirt to bead up and roll off, reducing the need for aggressive cleaning and making washing a quicker and more effective process.

Q5: Can Ceramic Pro protect against rock chips and deep scratches?

While Ceramic Pro provides excellent resistance to minor scratches and swirl marks, it is not designed to prevent damage from significant impacts like rock chips or deep gouges. For protection against such impacts, consider combining Ceramic Pro with a paint protection film.
