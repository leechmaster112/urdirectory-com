Q2: How long does a typical laser treatment session take?

Q3: Is laser treatment painful?

Laser treatments can cause some sensation, often described as a warm tingling or a snapping feeling on the skin. Our Webster team uses advanced laser technology and may apply a topical anesthetic cream beforehand if needed to ensure your comfort during the procedure. Many clients find the experience manageable with proper preparation.

The number of laser treatment sessions required depends on several factors, including the area being treated, hair color (for hair removal), skin type, and the specific condition being addressed (for skin rejuvenation). Q5: Are there any side effects or downtime associated with laser treatments?

Laser treatments are generally well-tolerated, but like any cosmetic procedure, they may have some temporary side effects. These can include redness, mild swelling, or a sensation similar to a sunburn immediately after the treatment at our Webster salon. Our team will provide you with comprehensive post-treatment care instructions to help manage any potential side effects and ensure a smooth recovery. Downtime is typically minimal, allowing you to resume your regular activities shortly after your appointment at 1900 Empire Blvd Suite 150.
