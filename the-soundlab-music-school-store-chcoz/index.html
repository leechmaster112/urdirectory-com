Location

The SoundLab Music School & Store, located at 1 Chapel St, St Kilda VIC 3182, is your premier destination for musical education and instrument acquisition right here in our vibrant St Kilda community. At The SoundLab, our mission is clear: to help you turn up your musical ability. Whether you're a complete beginner picking up the guitar for the first time, a dedicated student looking to refine your skills on drums, piano, or any other instrument, or an experienced musician seeking new gear or expert advice, we are here for you.

We offer instruction in a wide range of instruments including guitar, bass, drums, piano, keyboard, vocals, and more. As part of our store, we pride ourselves on offering a carefully curated selection of quality musical instruments, amplifiers, recording equipment, sheet music, books, and accessories. From entry-level starter gear to professional-grade equipment, we have everything you need to find your sound. Our knowledgeable staff are always on hand to offer expert advice, helping you navigate the vast world of music technology and find the perfect tools for your creative journey.

We understand that your instruments need to perform at their best. That's why our store also features professional repair and maintenance services. From simple setup and cleaning to complex electronic repairs and tuning, our technicians ensure your gear is reliable and ready to shine. We are committed to keeping your musical experience smooth and enjoyable.
