Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What types of dental services do you offer at your West Valley City location?

A: At Stonehaven Dental in West Valley City, we offer a comprehensive range of dental services including preventative care (examinations, cleanings, X-rays), restorative treatments (fillings, crowns, bridges, dentures), endodontics (root canals), periodontics (gum disease treatment), and cosmetic dentistry (teeth whitening, veneers, dental implants).

Q: Do you accept new patients from the West Valley City area?

A: Yes, we welcome new patients from the West Valley City area. You can schedule an initial consultation by calling our office at (801) 895-2788. During this visit, we will discuss your dental needs and get you started on the path to optimal oral health.

Q: What should I bring to my first dental appointment?

A: For your first appointment, please bring your identification, your dental insurance information (if applicable), and any relevant medical history forms. If you have completed new patient forms online beforehand, that will expedite your visit.

Q: Are payment plans available for dental services?

A: We accept most major dental insurance plans and offer various payment options, including cash, checks, and major credit cards. We also provide financing options through CareCredit for patients seeking payment plans. Please inquire about our financial policies when scheduling your appointment.

Q: How often should I schedule dental check-ups?

A: We generally recommend scheduling dental check-ups and cleanings every six months for most patients. However, the frequency may vary depending on your individual oral health needs, risk factors, and existing dental conditions. Our team will determine the appropriate schedule for you during your initial examination.
