Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What types of dental services do you offer at your West Valley City location?

A: At our Stonehaven Dental location in West Valley City, we offer a comprehensive range of dental services including dental examinations, cleanings, fillings, crowns, root canals, teeth whitening, cosmetic dentistry, and emergency dental care.

Q: Does Stonehaven Dental provide emergency dental services?

A: Yes, we understand that dental emergencies can happen unexpectedly. Our team at Stonehaven Dental is prepared to assist you with urgent dental issues. Please call our office at (801) 895-2788 during business hours, or seek immediate care if the situation is severe.

Q: How often should I schedule dental check-ups at Stonehaven Dental?

A: We generally recommend scheduling dental check-ups every six months for most patients. However, the frequency may vary based on individual needs, so we will determine the best schedule for you during your initial examination.

Q: Does Stonehaven Dental offer payment plans for dental treatments?

A: Yes, we understand that dental care can be a significant investment. We offer various payment options and work with patients to find a plan that fits their budget. Please inquire about our financing options when you schedule your appointment.

Q: What should I bring to my first dental appointment at Stonehaven Dental?

A: For your first visit, please bring your identification, dental insurance information (if applicable), a list of any current medications you are taking, and your medical history. We will also ask you to complete some new patient forms beforehand via our website.
