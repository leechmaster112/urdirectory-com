E S ELECTRICAL (Midlands) Ltd

ES Electrical is a premier commercial and industrial electrical contractor based in Northampton, serving the wider Midlands region with high-capacity technical solutions. Managed by Eli Stan, the firm specializes in high-complexity infrastructure projects, ranging from industrial electrician services for warehouse lighting upgrades to the installation of data, intercom, and fire alarm systems for large-scale 200-flat residential blocks. As a versatile commercial electrician, ES Electrical is uniquely positioned to handle diverse project scales, from a high-margin fuse board replacement or fuse box replacement to massive warehouse lighting replacements and complete office fit-outs. The company is a regional leader in the renewable energy transition, offering a specialized focus on EV charger installation for both private and corporate clients. Their expertise includes large-scale commercial EV charger installation projects, with contract values ranging from £1,500 to over £100,000. Through a strategic partnership with sister business Green Revolution, Eli provides a comprehensive "full-package" capability that integrates traditional electrical work with solar panels and heat pumps. This technical depth has earned the company a stellar reputation, evidenced by 64 five-star reviews and extensive experience in demanding environments such as schools, hospitals, and industrial facilities. Operating from a primary base in Northampton, ES Electrical provides rapid-response services across a broad 40-mile radius throughout the Midlands. This extensive service area includes key commercial hubs such as Milton Keynes, Wellingborough, Kettering, Daventry, Rugby, and Bedford. Whether delivering a local Northampton shop fit-out or managing a major industrial contract across the wider region, ES Electrical leverages a scalable workforce and deep technical authority to ensure every project meets the highest standards of safety and operational efficiency.
