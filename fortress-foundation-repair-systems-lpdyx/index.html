Common Questions About Foundation Repair in Rockwall

Q1: How do I know if my foundation needs repair?

Look for signs like interior or exterior cracks wider than 1/8 inch, sticking doors and windows, cracks in the basement or crawl space walls, uneven floors or foundations, gaps between walls and floors, and visible soil movement around the house.

Q2: Is foundation repair typically covered by homeowner's insurance?

Coverage varies significantly by policy and cause. Foundation repairs are often excluded, especially if the damage is due to general settling or neglect. It's best to review your specific policy or contact your insurance provider directly.

Q3: How long does a typical foundation repair project take?

The duration depends on the scope of the problem, the chosen repair method, and site conditions. Minor slab leveling might take a day or two, while more extensive pier installation could take several days to a week. Our team will provide a specific timeline after inspection.

Q4: Do you offer emergency services for foundation issues?

Yes, foundation problems can sometimes be urgent. While we strive to accommodate all appointments promptly, we recommend contacting us as soon as you notice significant issues to prevent further damage.

Q5: What kind of warranty do your foundation repairs come with?

We stand behind the quality of our work and typically offer warranties on both materials and labor for the foundation repair systems we install. The specific warranty details will be discussed with you during the project.
