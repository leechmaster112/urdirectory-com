Kids Connect Therapy, LLC

Kids Connect Therapy, LLC is a trusted provider of ABA therapy in Concord, offering compassionate, evidence-based services to help children with autism and developmental challenges reach their full potential. Our experienced team specializes in applied behavior analysis, autism behavioral therapy, developmental therapy, and individualized treatment plans designed to improve communication, social skills, learning, and daily living skills. We provide comprehensive home based ABA services, early intervention therapy, and convenient telehealth therapy to meet the unique needs of every child and family. Our personalized approach focuses on building positive behaviors, reducing challenging behaviors, and supporting long-term developmental success in comfortable and familiar environments. In addition to direct therapy services, Kids Connect Therapy, LLC offers autism parent coaching to empower families with effective strategies and ongoing support throughout their child's journey. We also assist families by providing guidance following an autism diagnosis and creating individualized treatment plans based on each child's strengths and needs. As a trusted ABA therapy provider in Concord, we are committed to delivering high-quality behavioral therapy that helps children thrive at home, in school, and in their communities.

Frequently Asked Questions:

What specific ABA therapy techniques are used at Kids Connect Therapy, LLC? Our therapists employ a range of evidence-based ABA strategies, including discrete trial training (DTT), pivotal response treatment (PRT), and positive behavior support (PBS), all adapted to the individual learning style of each child at our Concord location.

How does Kids Connect Therapy, LLC assess a child's needs? We begin with a comprehensive assessment conducted by our qualified professionals at 5011 Weddington Rd Suite 30 to identify specific strengths and areas for development, which then informs the creation of a personalized therapy plan.

What age range does Kids Connect Therapy, LLC serve? We specialize in providing ABA therapy for children, focusing on early intervention and ongoing support to maximize developmental gains.

Can parents be involved in the therapy process? Absolutely. Parent training and collaboration are integral components of our ABA approach, ensuring consistency and generalization of learned skills into the home environment.

What are the primary goals of the ABA therapy offered? Our primary goals are to enhance communication abilities, improve social engagement, develop adaptive daily living skills, and reduce challenging behaviors, all with the ultimate aim of increasing independence and quality of life for the children we serve.
