Water Backup: Sewage or wastewater backing up into sinks, toilets, or basement floor drains.

Gurgling Sounds: Unusual gurgling noises from plumbing fixtures.

Wet Spots or Sinkholes: Unusual damp areas or depressions in your yard.

High Water Bills: Unexpectedly high water bills, potentially indicating a hidden leak.

Q3: What causes sewer lines to fail in New York?

A3: Several factors can contribute to sewer line failures in New York:

Age and Material: Older pipes made of materials like clay or cast iron are more susceptible to cracking and corrosion.

Tree Root Invasion: Tree roots seeking moisture can infiltrate and damage pipes.

Ground Shift: Soil movement due to construction, excavation, or natural settling can stress and damage pipes.

Pipe Collapse: Can be caused by shifting soil, tree roots, or external pressure.

Corrosion: Internal corrosion, especially in older metal pipes.

Physical Damage: Accidental damage during digging or landscaping activities.

Regular inspections can help identify potential issues before they lead to major failures in New York.
