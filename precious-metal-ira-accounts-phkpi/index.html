Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What exactly is a Precious Metal IRA?

A: A Precious Metal IRA is a type of Self-Directed Individual Retirement Account that allows you to invest your retirement funds in physical precious metals, such as gold, silver, platinum, or palladium, held in an approved depository. This differs from traditional IRAs which typically invest in stocks, bonds, or mutual funds.

Q2: Can I open a Precious Metal IRA at Precious Metal IRA Accounts' Miami Beach location?

A: Yes, we offer guidance and support for opening Self-Directed Precious Metal IRAs. While the specific paperwork and account setup might often be initiated online or via phone, our team at 1521 Alton Road #90 in Miami Beach can provide detailed local consultation and answer any questions specific to your situation in the area.

Q3: What types of precious metals are allowed in an IRA?

A: Generally, IRS-approved precious metals for IRAs include gold and silver bullion bars or coins meeting specific fineness requirements, certain American Eagle coins, and some other government-minted coins. Platinum and palladium also qualify under specific conditions. We provide detailed information on eligible metals at our Miami Beach office.

Q4: How does the process of funding a Precious Metal IRA work?

A: Funding typically involves a direct rollover or transfer from an existing retirement plan (like a 401(k) or traditional IRA) to your new Self-Directed Precious Metal IRA. We can advise on the best methods to avoid tax penalties and ensure a smooth transition for clients based out of our 1521 Alton Road #90 location.

Q5: How are the precious metals stored?

A: Physical precious metals held within a Precious Metal IRA must be stored in an IRS-approved depository. We partner with reputable, secure depositories to hold the metals on behalf of our clients. Our team at 1521 Alton Road #90 ensures that the chosen depository meets all regulatory standards.
