Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What kind of tours do you offer at your museum on Las Vegas Blvd?

Our tours provide an interactive experience showcasing our collection of celebrity and historical figures. Tours include guided commentary, interactive photo opportunities, and are designed to be engaging for all ages.

Q2: What are your regular business hours at 3377 Las Vegas Blvd S?

We are typically open Tuesday through Sunday, from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. We recommend checking our website closer to your planned visit for the most current hours, as they may occasionally change.

Q3: Are there different ticket options available for your tours?

Yes, we offer individual ticket purchases online for single admission. We also provide special rates for group bookings (groups of 8 or more) and package deals for multi-day visits or combined attractions. You can find all ticketing options on our website.

We are committed to accessibility. Our museum features wheelchair access throughout, including elevators and accessible restrooms. Please contact us in advance if you have specific accessibility needs so we can ensure a comfortable visit.

Q5: Can I make a reservation for a private tour at your location?

Absolutely! We offer private tours for individuals, small groups, or special occasions. You can reserve a private tour directly through our website or by calling our office. Private tours can be customized to focus on specific interests.
