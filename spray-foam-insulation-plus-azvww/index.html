Our primary insulation product is spray foam. This advanced material is applied as a liquid and expands significantly, filling every gap and crevice within walls, attics, crawl spaces, and other cavities. Once cured, it forms a seamless, airtight barrier that significantly outperforms traditional insulation materials in terms of energy efficiency, air sealing, and overall thermal performance.

We primarily utilize two types of spray foam insulation:

Open-Cell Spray Foam: This type is typically used in areas that are not subject to high moisture levels, such as interior walls, attics (when used over a vapor barrier), and crawl spaces. It provides excellent air sealing and sound dampening properties at a competitive cost. Closed-Cell Spray Foam: This denser, more rigid foam is ideal for applications requiring higher R-values, structural support, and moisture resistance. It acts as both an insulator and a vapor barrier, making it suitable for exterior applications, roof decks, below-grade areas, and crawl spaces where moisture control is critical. Closed-cell foam also offers superior strength and can help reduce noise transmission.
