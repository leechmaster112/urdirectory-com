Our dedicated team understands that pets are cherished family members, and we approach each visit with the care and attention your furry friends deserve. We offer a wide range of veterinary services designed to keep your pets healthy and happy throughout their lives.

Comprehensive Veterinary Services

At our Kennesaw location, we provide thorough preventive care, including comprehensive physical examinations, tailored vaccination programs, and essential parasite control. We offer specialized care for common concerns such as dental disease prevention and treatment, strategic weight management programs, and age-appropriate senior pet care. Our facility is equipped to handle routine procedures like nail trims and ear cleanings, as well as more complex surgeries when necessary.

Urgent Care Services

Recognizing that pet emergencies don't always occur during regular business hours, our Kennesaw center provides accessible urgent care services. We are equipped to address sudden illnesses or injuries, offering prompt attention to stabilize your pet's condition before referring to specialists if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What are your regular business hours?

A: Our Kennesaw location is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Saturday from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM, and Sunday from Noon to 4:00 PM. We strive to accommodate pet owners' schedules while providing quality care.

Q2: Do you offer pet boarding services?

A: Yes, we provide clean, comfortable boarding accommodations for dogs and cats at our Kennesaw facility. Our boarding area is designed to minimize stress and ensure your pet's safety during their stay.

Q3: How do I prepare for a routine wellness exam?

A: For routine visits, please bring your pet's complete medical history, including previous vaccination records and a list of current medications. We recommend bringing any samples of discharge or unusual findings for our team's review.

Q4: What should I do in case of a pet emergency outside your hours?

A: For after-hours emergencies, please contact our referral partner, Northside Veterinary Specialists at (770) 404-8937. They provide emergency care 24/7 and can coordinate with our Kennesaw facility if needed.

Q5: Do you offer mobile veterinary services?

A: Our Kennesaw location primarily operates from our clinic. However, we can coordinate house calls for established patients with chronic conditions requiring frequent monitoring.
