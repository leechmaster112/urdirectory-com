Q2: How do you determine the value of my Arlington injury claim?

We carefully evaluate your case by considering factors such as the severity of your injuries, medical expenses incurred, lost wages, potential future medical needs, pain and suffering, and any other relevant damages specific to your situation.

Q3: What is the time limit to file a personal injury claim in Arlington?

In Texas, the statute of limitations for personal injury claims is generally two years from the date of the accident. It is crucial to consult with us promptly to ensure your claim is filed within this time frame.

Q4: What should I do immediately after an injury in Arlington?

Seek medical attention for your injuries if possible. Report the incident to the relevant authorities (e.g., police for accidents, employer for workplace injuries). Document the scene, gather witness information if available, and contact us as soon as you can to discuss your options.
