Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is axe throwing? Axe throwing is a fun and challenging activity where participants throw wooden axes at a target. It's a popular sport and entertainment option enjoyed by people of all ages.

Q2: Do I need prior experience to try axe throwing? Absolutely not! We offer instruction for beginners of all ages. Our experienced staff will teach you the basics, including proper grip, stance, and throwing technique.

Q3: How many people can book a session at once? We can accommodate groups of all sizes at our location on Hooper Ave. Whether you're a party of two or a large group, we have space for you!

Q4: Is axe throwing safe? Safety is our top priority at Axe Throwing Toms River NJ. We use high-quality, blunt-tipped axes and have a well-designed target area. Our trained staff supervise all sessions to ensure a safe and fun experience.

Q5: Can I bring my own axe? For safety and consistency, we recommend using our axes. However, if you have your own axe, please contact us in advance to discuss specifications and safety requirements.
