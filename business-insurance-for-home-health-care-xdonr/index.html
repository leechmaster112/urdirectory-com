Business Insurance For Home Health Care

Home health care agencies face a unique mix of clinical, professional, and in-home safety risks every day. Nationwide Business Insurance offers specialized home health care business insurance designed to protect your caregivers, your clients, and your agency’s reputation while you deliver care in homes, assisted living settings, and community environments. From medication reminders and post-surgery support to long-term personal care, a slip-and-fall in a client’s home, a driving accident between visits, or an allegation of negligence can quickly turn into a costly claim. Nationwide’s home health care insurance solutions can be tailored to include general liability, professional liability (errors & omissions), workers’ compensation, commercial auto, abuse and molestation coverage, cyber liability for protected health information, and business property and income protection for your office and records. Nationwide +1 Whether you operate a small personal care agency or manage a growing team of nurses, therapists, and home health aides, Nationwide pairs industry-focused coverage with risk control resources and responsive claims service. The result: you can meet contract and state requirements, support your staff, and keep your focus where it belongs—on safe, consistent patient care in every home you visit.
