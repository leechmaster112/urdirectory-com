Our Core Services, focused entirely on the contractor community in the Phoenix area, include:

1. Commercial General Liability (CGL): This is the cornerstone of contractor insurance. It protects your business from claims of bodily injury, property damage, personal injury (like libel or slander), and advertising injury incurred during the course of your work within your local projects.

2. Workers' Compensation Insurance: Ensuring your valued employees are protected in case of job-related injuries or illnesses is critical. We help source competitive workers' comp policies tailored to the specific risks present in your trade, crucial for compliance and peace of mind here in Arizona.

3. Property Insurance: Your tools, equipment, vehicles, and potentially your business premises are vital assets. We help secure property insurance that covers damage from events like fire, theft, vandalism, or natural disasters affecting your operations in the Phoenix metro area.

4. Business Auto Insurance: If your contracting business involves company vehicles, whether trucks, vans, or other specialized equipment, we provide specialized auto insurance policies designed for your unique needs, covering liability and physical damage.

5. Professional Liability (Errors & Omissions): For contractors involved in design, project management, or providing specific services, this insurance protects against claims alleging negligence, mistakes, or failure to perform professional duties that result in financial loss to clients.

6. Cyber Liability Insurance: In today's connected world, protecting your business data and your clients' information is paramount. Cyber Liability insurance helps cover costs associated with data breaches, network security failures, and related liabilities.

7. Inland Marine Insurance: This covers the transportation and storage of materials and equipment used in your projects, protecting against loss or damage while they are in transit or temporarily off the project site.

8. Business Owner's Policy (BOP): Often, we can bundle several essential coverages (like CGL and Property) into a streamlined Business Owner's Policy, offering contractors a convenient and cost-effective insurance solution tailored for local businesses.

9. Commercial Auto Insurance: Beyond standard auto coverage, we offer specialized commercial auto policies that address the specific risks associated with vehicles used regularly for business purposes, including bobtail coverage.
