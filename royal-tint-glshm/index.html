2. How long does a typical window tint installation take at your Rocklin shop?

The duration depends on the vehicle, but most standard cars and trucks are completed within a few hours at our Rocklin service bay. We will provide you with a precise time estimate when you schedule your appointment.

3. Is window tint legal for vehicles serviced in Rocklin?

Yes, we install window tint that complies with California state laws. Our experts at the Rocklin location will ensure your tint meets all visible light transmission (VLT) requirements for your specific vehicle type.

4. How do I care for my newly tinted windows after leaving your Rocklin facility?

We advise clients to avoid rolling down their windows and cleaning the glass for at least three to five days after installation. After that period, use a soft, non-abrasive cloth and ammonia-free cleaner to maintain the film's clarity and integrity.
