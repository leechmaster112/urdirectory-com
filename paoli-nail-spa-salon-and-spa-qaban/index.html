Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):

Q: Do you offer nail services for both men and women?

A: Absolutely! At Paoli Nail Spa, we welcome all clients. Our skilled technicians are experienced in providing quality manicures and pedicures for everyone, regardless of gender.

Q: How long does a typical manicure or pedicure take?

A: A standard manicure usually takes about 30-45 minutes, while a pedicure can range from 45 minutes to an hour, depending on the specific treatment chosen.

Q: Do you provide nail care advice and maintenance tips?

A: Yes, we always take the time to educate our clients on proper nail care. We can provide tips on maintaining your nails between salon visits and recommend products that suit your needs.

Q: Can I purchase your nail products elsewhere?

A: While we recommend our specific products for optimal results and salon consistency, many of our quality items are available for purchase online or at other retail locations. However, for the best experience and product matching, we suggest discussing your preferences with us.

Q: What forms of payment do you accept?

A: We accept major credit cards, debit cards, and cash for your convenience.
