Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What types of vehicles do you service?

We service all makes and models of passenger vehicles, including domestic, import, and luxury brands. Our expertise covers cars, trucks, and SUVs of all sizes.

Q: Do you offer mobile services?

No, we provide services exclusively from our shop at 114 SW 9th St. We recommend bringing your vehicle to our facility for the best service experience.

Q: What preventative maintenance services do you offer?

We offer a comprehensive range of preventative maintenance services including oil changes, multi-point inspections, filter replacements, tire rotations, and fluid checks to keep your vehicle running smoothly.

Q: How long does a typical oil change take?

A standard oil change typically takes 30-45 minutes, depending on the type of oil and filter required. We'll provide you with an accurate estimate before starting any work.

Q: Do you provide loaner vehicles?

Yes, we have a selection of loaner vehicles available for your convenience while your car is being serviced. Please ask at the time of scheduling your appointment.
