Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What types of water heaters do you install and repair?

We specialize in the installation, repair, and maintenance of various types of water heaters commonly found in Las Vegas homes and businesses, including traditional tank-style heaters and energy-efficient tankless models.

Q: How long does a typical water heater last?

On average, traditional tank water heaters last 8-10 years, while tankless models can last 15 years or more with proper maintenance. We can advise you on the expected lifespan of your specific unit.

Q: Do you offer emergency water heater repair services?

Yes, we understand that a broken water heater can be disruptive. We strive to provide prompt emergency repair services for our Las Vegas customers facing urgent issues.

Q: Can you help me choose the right water heater for my home?

Absolutely. We can assess your hot water needs, discuss your preferences (tank vs. tankless), and recommend the most suitable and efficient water heater option for your situation at your Las Vegas residence.

Q: How often should I schedule maintenance for my water heater?

We recommend scheduling professional maintenance for your water heater at least once a year to ensure optimal performance, prolong its lifespan, and prevent potential issues.
