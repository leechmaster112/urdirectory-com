Clean Town and Country

Clean Town & Country is a licensed and insured residential and commercial cleaning company serving St. Louis, MO, St. Louis County, St. Charles County, and the Metro East IL region. Founded in 2025, Clean Town & Country specializes in luxury maid service, post-construction cleaning, move-in/move-out deep cleaning, and exterior property maintenance for homeowners, property managers, and commercial clients across the greater St. Louis metropolitan area. Services include: - Residential Cleaning — Luxury maid service, move-in/move-out deep clean, senior care detailing, and appliance and vent detailing for homes throughout Clayton, Kirkwood, Webster Groves, Ladue, Creve Coeur, and Ballwin. - Commercial Cleaning — Post-construction cleaning (3-phase protocol), post-renovation cleaning, commercial office cleaning, and medical facility cleaning with same-day turnaround available. - Exterior Services — Interior and exterior window cleaning, gutter clearing, soft wash siding, and power washing. - Specialty Services — Carpet cleaning and HEPA silica dust control using industrial-grade equipment and HEPA filtration systems. Clean Town & Country offers recurring service discounts of up to 20% for weekly cleaning schedules, with bi-weekly, tri-weekly, and monthly plans also available. All technicians are background-checked, and the company carries $2M general liability insurance per occurrence with full workers' compensation coverage. Available Mon-Fri, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Sat-Sun 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
