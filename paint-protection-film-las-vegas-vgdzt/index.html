Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is paint protection film and how does it work?

Paint protection film is a durable, transparent urethane layer applied to the exterior painted surfaces of your vehicle. It acts as an invisible shield, absorbing impacts from road debris like rocks and gravel to prevent chips and scratches. The advanced film we use at our Henderson location features self-healing technology, where minor swirl marks and light scratches disappear with exposure to heat.

2. Why should I choose your Henderson location for my paint protection film installation?

Our facility at 7585 Commercial Way suite h is equipped with a state-of-the-art installation bay designed for precision work. Our installers are highly trained and certified, ensuring a perfect application with clean edges and seamless results. We take the time to prepare your vehicle properly and use custom computer-precise patterns for an exact fit.

3. How long does a professional paint protection film installation take at your shop?

The duration of an installation depends on the coverage you select. A full front-end protection package typically takes one to two days. A full vehicle wrap can take several days to complete. We provide accurate time estimates during your consultation to ensure your convenience.

4. How do I care for my vehicle after the film is applied at your Henderson facility?

After installation, we provide you with detailed care instructions. Generally, we recommend waiting at least one week before washing your vehicle. Use only gentle, ammonia-free cleaners and avoid automated car washes with abrasive brushes. The film is very low maintenance and can be waxed or sealed once fully cured to enhance its glossy finish.

5. What if the film on my car gets damaged? Can it be repaired or replaced?

Yes. While our high-quality film is extremely durable, significant damage can occur. The great advantage is that the film takes the damage, not your vehicle's paint. Individual panels or sections of the film can be removed and replaced at our Henderson location, which is a much more cost-effective solution than repairing and repainting the actual body of your car.
