For many in our Cary community, the journey to finding effective relief from depression has been long and challenging. Traditional treatments, while helpful for some, don't work for everyone. This is where Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) therapy offers a distinct path. It's a non-invasive procedure that utilizes a magnetic field, similar to those used in MRI machines, to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. The goal is to reawaken dormant pathways and improve communication between brain cells, which can lead to a significant reduction in depressive symptoms.

The NeuroStar TMS system is designed for comfort and precision. During your treatment sessions at our 1110 SE Cary Pkwy 207 Suite 3 facility, you will be seated comfortably. A specialized device will be placed against your head, delivering brief magnetic pulses. You'll remain awake and alert throughout the session, and most individuals report being able to resume their normal daily activities immediately afterward, including driving themselves home. This convenience is a significant factor for many of our patients who are seeking effective treatment without disrupting their lives more than necessary.

Understanding the practicalities is key before you begin. Treatment typically involves a series of sessions, usually five days a week for several weeks. Frequently Asked Questions about TMS Therapy at TMS of the Carolinas:

Q: What is the typical duration of a TMS therapy session at your Cary location?

A: A standard NeuroStar TMS therapy session at our 1110 SE Cary Pkwy 207 Suite 3, Cary, NC 27518 facility typically lasts about 20 minutes. You remain awake and alert throughout the treatment.

Q: Will I experience any side effects from NeuroStar TMS therapy?

A: The most common side effect reported is mild headache or scalp discomfort during or shortly after treatment. These are generally temporary and can often be managed. Unlike many antidepressant medications, TMS therapy does not typically cause weight gain, nausea, or sedation.

Q: How many TMS therapy sessions will I likely need for major depressive disorder?

A: The standard course of treatment for major depressive disorder with NeuroStar TMS involves daily sessions, five days a week, for approximately 4 to 6 weeks. The exact number of sessions can vary based on individual response and your doctor's recommendation.

Q: Can I drive myself home after a TMS therapy session?

A: Yes, most patients can drive themselves to and from their TMS therapy appointments because the treatment is non-sedating and does not impair cognitive function. You can usually resume your normal daily activities immediately after a session.
