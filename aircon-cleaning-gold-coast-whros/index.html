Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How often should I get my aircon cleaned in New York?

A: For most residential units in New York, we recommend professional cleaning at least once a year. However, if you have allergies, live in a high-pollution area, or notice reduced efficiency or strange odors, more frequent cleaning (e.g., twice a year) may be beneficial.

Q2: What does a typical aircon cleaning service include?

A: Our standard service includes a thorough inspection, cleaning of internal components (ducts, coils, evaporator/condenser, fan blades), sanitizing the system, and a final check to ensure optimal performance. We focus on removing dust, debris, mold, and bacteria.

Q3: How long does an average aircon cleaning take?

A: The duration depends on the size of the unit, its condition, and the scope of the cleaning required. Typically, a standard residential cleaning can take between 1 to 3 hours to complete.

Q4: Do you clean window air conditioners?

A: Yes, we provide cleaning services for window units commonly found in apartments and smaller spaces within New York. We clean the internal components effectively to improve airflow and efficiency.

Q5: Is it necessary to clean my aircon if I'm not experiencing any problems?

A: Absolutely. Regular cleaning prevents the buildup of dust, allergens, and microbes, which can degrade performance, increase energy costs, and negatively impact indoor air quality over time, even if you don't notice immediate issues.
