Renu Poly Epoxy Floors & Refinishing

Renu Poly — Garage Floors Reinvented Renu Poly is Michigan’s premier destination for custom garage floors, offering durable, stylish, and high-performance garage flooring solutions that outlast traditional epoxy. Based in Clinton Township and serving Metro Detroit and surrounding communities, Renu Poly delivers garage floor coatings specially engineered to stand up to heavy use, moisture, and harsh weather—while transforming your space with real beauty and lasting function. What We Do At Renu Poly, we specialize in installing superior concrete coatings that go far beyond ordinary garage epoxy. Our advanced system uses a polyurea base coat, decorative flake broadcasts if desired, and a UV-stable polyaspartic top coat. The end result? Floors that are moisture resistant, slip-resistant, stain-resistant, antimicrobial, and built to perform in all conditions. Our coatings are four times stronger than traditional epoxy, won’t yellow or fade, and are backed by a limited lifetime residential warranty. Why Choose Renu Poly over Regular Epoxy? Speedy installation: While epoxy typically takes 5-7 days to cure, Renu Poly’s system can be installed in just one day. Superior durability: Our polyurea/polyaspartic system is made to expand and contract with temperature changes, reducing cracking, peeling or failing—common problems with standard epoxy. Custom finishes: Want decorative flakes? A color blend that matches your vehicles or décor? A smooth showroom finish? We offer many options to make your garage, patio, basement, or commercial space uniquely yours. Long-term peace of mind: With our lifetime warranty, your investment is protected. We’re not just coating a floor—we’re helping you upgrade your space in a way that lasts. Our Process Free Consultation & Detailed Quote We begin with a no-cost, no-obligation consultation. We review your space, discuss your style preferences, whether you want a showroom-smooth look or something more textured/slip-resistant, and whether surface repairs are needed. You get a transparent, written estimate with no hidden fees. Scheduling & Preparation Once you accept the estimate, we schedule the installation at a time that works for you. Our team provides guidance on preparing the area—clearing it out, ensuring access, etc. Surface Preparation Proper prep is the foundation of long-lasting finishes. We use industrial diamond grinders, dust-controlled vacuums, and high-strength patching to ensure the concrete surface is clean, sound, and ready. We don’t paint over—we build something that adheres right and holds strong. Application Apply a polyurea base coat for strength Broadcast flakes if requested, for texture and style Finish with a polyaspartic protective top coat that seals, protects, and resists UV, chemicals, oil, and wear Cure & Use The floor is walkable in 4-6 hours, ready for vehicle traffic in about 24 hours, and fully chemical resistant in roughly 5-7 days. That’s dramatically faster than many epoxy systems.
