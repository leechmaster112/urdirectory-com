Taj Mahal Seattle

Since 1982, Taj Mahal has offered authentic spiritual and cultural goods inspired by India's rich traditions. Each item is selected with respect for devotion, craftsmanship, and heritage. Over 40 Years in Seattle — We've Moved, Come Find Us! The journey of Taj Mahal Emporium from a humble spice store in 1982 to a vibrant showcase of Indian culture is a testament to the enduring appeal of artisanal craftsmanship. Today, we offer a diverse array of handicrafts, textiles, statues, and incense, preserving and celebrating the rich tapestry of India's cultural heritage. Every product narrates a tale, embodying the artistry and skill inherited from successive generations. It is crucial that we continue this 40-plus-year legacy, and we need your support in making that happen. "New Spot, new vibes! Come See our new location.. Two blocks from Pikes Place Market"
