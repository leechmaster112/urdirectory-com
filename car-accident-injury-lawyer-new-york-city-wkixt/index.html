Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What types of car accident injuries do you handle?

We handle all types of injuries sustained in car accidents, including whiplash, broken bones, burns, head injuries, spinal cord injuries, and traumatic brain injuries.

Q: How soon should I contact a lawyer after a car accident?

It's crucial to contact a lawyer as soon as possible after your accident. New York law requires reporting accidents to the DMV and insurance companies within specific timeframes, and evidence can be lost over time.

Q: Do I have to pay anything upfront for your services?

No. Our firm operates on a contingency fee basis, meaning you only pay attorney's fees if we successfully settle or win your case.

Q: How long does a car accident case typically take?

The duration varies significantly based on factors like the severity of injuries, insurance cooperation, and the need for extensive medical treatment. Simple cases may settle in weeks, while complex ones can take a year or more.

Q: What if the other driver doesn't have insurance?

We can utilize New York's no-fault insurance system and potentially pursue the at-fault driver's underinsured motorist coverage or their personal assets to ensure you receive the compensation you deserve.
