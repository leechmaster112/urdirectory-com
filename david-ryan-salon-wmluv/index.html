Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What services does David Ryan Salon offer at the Trophy Club location?

We specialize in luxury hair services including precision cuts, coloring (including highlights, lowlights, and root touch-ups), keratin treatments, deep conditioning, scalp treatments, beard and mustache grooming, and nail care.

Q2: Are you accepting walk-in clients?

We recommend booking an appointment online or by phone to ensure we can provide you with the full attention and time required for your desired service. Walk-ins are welcome if we have available appointments.

Q3: Do you offer haircuts for men?

Absolutely! Our skilled stylists are experienced in providing expert men's haircuts, including fades, straight edges, and traditional cuts, ensuring a look that suits your style and face shape.

Q4: What is your cancellation policy?

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment. This helps us accommodate other clients and manage our schedule effectively.

Q5: Do you provide styling services for special occasions?

Yes, we offer specialized styling services perfect for weddings, dates, photoshoots, and other special events. We can create intricate updos, sleek straight styles, or voluminous looks tailored to your needs.
