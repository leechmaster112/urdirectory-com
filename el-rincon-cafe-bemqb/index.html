El Rincon Cafe

El Rincon Cafe is a fine dining restaurant located in the heart of Santa Fe at 125 Washington Ave. For over 10 years, we’ve been serving elevated Northern New Mexico cuisine in an intimate, welcoming atmosphere. Our menu highlights locally inspired dishes, prepared with fresh ingredients and authentic regional flavors. Guests can enjoy a curated selection of wine and beer, making El Rincon Cafe a perfect choice for date nights, celebrations, or a relaxed evening in downtown Santa Fe. Whether you’re a local or visiting from nearby cities like Albuquerque or Denver, or major destinations such as Phoenix, Dallas, Austin, Los Angeles, Miami, Atlanta, or New York, El Rincon Cafe offers a memorable dining experience rooted in Santa Fe tradition. We proudly serve both English- and Spanish-speaking guests and accept cash, credit cards, debit cards, and Apple Pay. Join us Tuesday through Sunday for an unforgettable taste of Northern New Mexico fine dining.
