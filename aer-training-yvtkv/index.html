AER+ Training

AER+ Training delivers Ofqual-regulated first aid courses and HSE-compliant workplace safety training from its base at the Innovation Centre, Knowledge Gateway, Colchester, Essex. Serving businesses and organisations across North Essex, Suffolk, and the wider East of England, AER+ Training brings fully equipped, instructor-led sessions directly to your premises — eliminating downtime and travel costs with a complete mobile training setup including manikins, AEDs, and all required course materials. Accredited programmes span Emergency First Aid at Work (EFAW), First Aid at Work (FAW), Paediatric First Aid, CPR and AED operation, and mental health first aid — each meeting current Health and Safety Executive guidelines and satisfying employer legal duties under the Health and Safety (First Aid) Regulations 1981. The signature 3-Year Compliance Plan consolidates staff certification renewals into one managed schedule, ensuring businesses in Colchester, Chelmsford, Ipswich, and surrounding areas never face a lapse in workplace first aid compliance. As a registered social enterprise, AER+ Training reinvests profits into community lifesaving initiatives across the East Region through its "The Big Plan" programme, funding the development of public rescue clubs and CPR awareness campaigns. Every booking directly supports a broader mission to reduce out-of-hospital cardiac arrest mortality rates across Essex and Suffolk.
