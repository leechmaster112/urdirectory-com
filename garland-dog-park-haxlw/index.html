Garland Dog Park

Garland Dog Park is a 3-acre off-leash facility at 901 S Glenbrook Dr featuring three rotating dog paddocks with double-gated entries and six-foot fencing. Separate areas for small and large dogs, a water fountain, and shaded seating round out the park. Hours, photos, and directions are on its Doggie Park Near Me listing.
