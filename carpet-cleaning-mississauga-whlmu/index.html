Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: How long does a typical carpet cleaning job take?

A: The duration of a carpet cleaning job depends on the size of the area and the level of soiling. A standard room might take 1-2 hours, while larger areas or heavily soiled carpets could require more time. Our team will provide a specific estimate after inspecting your carpets.

Q2: Do you offer any guarantees on your cleaning?

A: Yes, we stand behind the quality of our work. We typically offer a satisfaction guarantee, allowing you to request a touch-up if you notice any missed spots or areas within a certain timeframe after our service.

Q3: What types of stains can your cleaning process remove?

A: Our deep cleaning process is effective on a wide range of common stains, including dirt, dust, pollen, pet accidents, spills (like coffee, juice, or minor oil stains), and general soil buildup. The effectiveness can vary slightly depending on the age and severity of the stain.

Q4: Is your cleaning process safe for homes with children or pets?

A: Absolutely. We use professional-grade equipment and eco-friendly cleaning solutions that are safe for homes. While carpets are drying, we recommend limiting access to allow them to dry completely, usually within 6-8 hours. Once dry, your carpets will be safe for children and pets to walk on.

Q5: How often should carpets be professionally cleaned?

A: The frequency depends on factors like foot traffic, presence of pets, smoking, and the climate. As a general guideline, many experts recommend professional carpet cleaning every 12-18 months. However, high-traffic areas or areas prone to spills might benefit from cleaning more frequently.
