Waterview Marble & Granite

Waterview Marble & Granite is a family-owned custom stone fabrication and installation company in Franklin, Massachusetts. Since 2003, we have served homeowners, builders, and designers throughout Franklin, Norfolk County, and the greater Boston area with high-quality countertops for kitchens, bathrooms, fireplaces, and commercial projects. We work with granite, marble, quartz, and quartzite, and we manage every part of the process in-house, including measuring, templating, fabrication, and installation. That means no middlemen, no showroom markups, and a more direct experience for every customer. Our focus is on craftsmanship, reliability, and fast, precise service from the first consultation to the final installation.
