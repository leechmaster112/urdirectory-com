Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is a medical procedure that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. Specifically, it targets areas of the brain that are underactive in individuals with depression. The magnetic pulses are delivered through a device placed on the scalp, and the procedure is performed in a comfortable, outpatient setting at our 1806 N George St clinic. Unlike electroconvulsive therapy (ECT), TMS is non-invasive, meaning it does not require surgery or anesthesia, and it generally has fewer side effects.

The magnetic pulses are precisely targeted to specific areas of the brain involved in mood regulation, and the therapy works by helping to rebalance brain activity.
