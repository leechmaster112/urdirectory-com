Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What days and times are you open?

A1: We are open for lunch Monday through Friday from 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Our dinner service runs from 4:00 PM to 9:00 PM Monday through Thursday, and from 4:00 PM to 10:00 PM on Friday and Saturday. We offer brunch on Sundays from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Please check our website https://www.driftwoodnovi.com/ for the most current hours.

Q2: Do you take reservations?

A2: Yes, we recommend making a reservation, especially for dinner on weekends or for larger parties. You can easily book your table by calling us at (248) 668-9005 or using the reservation link on our website.

Q3: What is on the typical menu?

A3: Our menu features a variety of American classics for lunch, dinner, and brunch. Expect items like burgers, sandwiches, salads, soups, seafood, chicken, and pasta dishes, prepared with quality ingredients. We also offer a range of appetizers, desserts, and, of course, a full bar. Check our website for the full menu specific to our Novi location.

Q4: Is there parking available?

A4: Yes, we offer convenient parking for our guests. We have a dedicated parking lot located directly at our restaurant address, 1103 E Lake Dr, Novi, MI, 48377. Street parking may also be available depending on the time of day.

Q5: Can I place an order for pickup or delivery?

A5: Yes! You can place orders for pickup or delivery through third-party services like DoorDash, Grubhub, or Uber Eats. You can also call us at (248) 668-9005 to inquire about ordering directly or to place a pickup order.
