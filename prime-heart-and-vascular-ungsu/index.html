Comprehensive Cardiology Services

Cardiovascular health is fundamental to overall well-being. Our cardiology services at 6201 Dallas Parkway, Plano, are designed to address a wide spectrum of heart conditions:

Preventive Cardiology: We emphasize proactive heart health. Through thorough evaluations, risk factor assessment, and personalized lifestyle modifications, we help you prevent heart disease. This includes stress testing, echocardiograms, and lipid profile management. Diagnosis and Monitoring: Utilizing cutting-edge tools like stress echocardiography, coronary calcium scoring, and Holter monitor testing, our team accurately diagnoses heart rhythm disorders (arrhythmias) and structural heart issues. Heart Disease Management: We provide comprehensive care for coronary artery disease, heart failure, and other serious cardiac conditions. Our approach includes medically managed therapies tailored to each patient's needs. Cardiac Rehabilitation: We offer structured programs designed to help patients recover from heart events, improve cardiovascular fitness, and reduce the risk of future problems.

Advanced Vascular Care

Our vascular specialists focus on the health of the arteries and veins outside the heart. We diagnose and treat conditions that can lead to serious complications if left unmanaged:

Diabetic Vascular Disease: We specialize in the assessment and management of vascular complications arising from diabetes, including peripheral artery disease (PAD) and related foot complications. Peripheral Artery Disease (PAD): Our team utilizes state-of-the-art imaging and minimally invasive techniques to diagnose and treat narrowed arteries that can cause pain, numbness, or difficulty walking. Varicose Veins and Venous Insufficiency: We offer effective treatments for problematic veins, including sclerotherapy, laser therapy, and advanced procedures to alleviate symptoms and improve appearance. Preventive Vascular Screening: We provide risk assessments and screening services to identify vascular issues early, focusing on risk factors like high blood pressure, high cholesterol, and smoking.
