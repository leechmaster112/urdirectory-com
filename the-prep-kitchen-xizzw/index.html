The Prep Kitchen

The Prep Kitchen is a Dallas-based meal prep service providing fresh, chef-prepared meals for busy professionals, families, athletes and people working toward specific nutrition goals. Choose ready-to-eat breakfast, lunch and dinner, high-protein meals, macro-friendly meals, GLP-1-friendly options, keto and low-carb meals, weight-management meals, custom build-your-own meals, bulk proteins and sides, snacks and weekly bundles. Every menu item includes clear nutrition and allergen information. Order once or subscribe for weekly meal prep, with pickup throughout Dallas–Fort Worth and local delivery where available.
