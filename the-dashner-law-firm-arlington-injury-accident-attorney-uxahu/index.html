Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How long do I have to file a personal injury claim in Arlington, Texas?

A1: In Arlington, as in the rest of Texas, the statute of limitations for most personal injury claims is two years from the date of the injury. It is crucial to consult with an Arlington injury attorney promptly to ensure your claim is filed within this time frame.

Q2: What types of injuries do you handle?

A2: We handle a wide range of injuries resulting from the negligence of others. This includes, but is not limited to, whiplash, broken bones, burns, spinal cord injuries, traumatic brain injuries (TBI), soft tissue injuries, and injuries sustained in workplace accidents or medical malpractice situations.

Q3: Do I need to go to court for my case?

A3: Many personal injury cases in Arlington are resolved through settlement negotiations with the insurance company before reaching court. However, if a fair settlement cannot be reached, our firm is fully prepared and experienced to represent you in court to fight for your entitled compensation.

Q4: How much will my case cost?

A4: We operate on a contingency fee basis. This means our attorney's fees are contingent upon successfully obtaining a settlement or verdict in your case. You won't owe any legal fees unless we win or secure a favorable settlement for you.

Q5: What should I do immediately after an injury in Arlington?

A5: After sustaining an injury in Arlington, prioritize your health and safety. Seek immediate medical attention if necessary. Document the scene if it's safe to do so (take photos, gather witness contact information). Report the incident to the relevant parties (e.g., property owner, employer, police) and contact an Arlington injury attorney as soon as possible to discuss your options.
