George Herkes & Son Joinery (Est. 1989)

George Herkes & Son Joinery has been a trusted name in carpentry and joinery across Tranent, East Lothian and the wider Edinburgh area since 1989. With over three decades of hands-on experience, this established father-and-son team delivers skilled craftsmanship across a comprehensive range of residential and commercial joinery projects - from large-scale structural work to detailed finishing trades. Specialising in loft conversions, garage conversions, and timber kit extensions, the business brings structural joinery expertise to every build. Kitchen fitting, bathroom refurbishment, and bespoke fitted wardrobes and storage solutions are undertaken to exacting standards, with a keen eye for detail and quality materials throughout. Internal and external door supply and installation, sash window repair and replacement, hardwood and laminate flooring, skirting boards, architrave, and staircase work are all carried out with the same level of care. Covering Tranent, Musselburgh, Prestonpans, Haddington, Dalkeith, and surrounding East Lothian communities, the team also serves clients across Edinburgh and the Lothians for larger projects. Whether it's a full property renovation, a single room fit-out, or routine joinery maintenance and repairs, every job receives the same commitment to quality workmanship and reliable service. Free estimates available - call 07860 211815 or visit ghsjoinery.co.uk to get started.
