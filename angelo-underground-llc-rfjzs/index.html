Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What types of excavation services do you offer?

Angelo Underground LLC offers a comprehensive range of excavation services, including commercial and residential excavation, trenching for utilities and foundations, site preparation, utility installation and relocation, and specialized services like basement excavation. We tailor our services to meet the specific requirements of each project.

Do you handle permits for excavation projects?

Yes, we understand the complexities of permits and regulations. Angelo Underground LLC can assist with the permit process for many projects, helping you navigate local requirements and ensuring all necessary documentation is handled efficiently to keep your project moving smoothly.

How long does a typical excavation or trenching project take?

The timeline for an excavation or trenching project varies significantly based on factors like project size, site conditions, material availability, and specific project requirements. We provide accurate estimates after assessing your project. Our focus is on efficient project management to meet agreed-upon timelines.

Is trenching safe, and do you follow safety protocols?

Safety is paramount in all our operations. Angelo Underground LLC strictly adheres to industry-leading safety protocols and regulations. We provide comprehensive safety training to our team, utilize proper equipment, and implement measures to prevent hazards like cave-ins and utility strikes, ensuring a safe work environment for everyone involved.

Do you offer emergency excavation services?

Absolutely. Emergencies such as unexpected sinkholes, blocked drains, or utility line breaks require immediate attention. Angelo Underground LLC offers prompt emergency excavation services. We are available to respond quickly and effectively to mitigate damage and restore normalcy as efficiently as possible.
