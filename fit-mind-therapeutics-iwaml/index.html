Fit Mind Therapeutics

At FitMind Therapeutics:Advanced Behavioral Health, we specialize in holistic, patient-centered mental health care for individuals ages 16 and older. Our services include psychiatric evaluations, medication management, and Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS), a non-invasive, FDA approved treatment for depression, anxiety, and OCD – especially beneficial for patients who are unable to tolerate or do not respond well to traditional medications. We are committed to individualized, culturally-sensitive care to support each patient’s mental journey.
