Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):

Q1: What is neurologically focused chiropractic care?

A: Neurologically focused chiropractic care at Revival Family Chiropractic emphasizes the vital connection between the spine and the nervous system. We use precise adjustments to remove interference (subluxations) to nerve pathways, thereby optimizing nervous system function and allowing your body's innate healing capabilities to work effectively.

Q2: What conditions can chiropractic care help with at your Charlotte location?

A: While not a medical diagnosis or treatment center, our Charlotte practice uses chiropractic care to support the body's natural healing processes for various conditions. This includes musculoskeletal pain (back, neck, joint pain), headaches and migraines, sciatica, sports injuries, and overall wellness optimization. We focus on improving nervous system function.

Q3: Is chiropractic care safe for children and seniors at your Charlotte practice?

A: Yes, gentle chiropractic techniques are safe and beneficial for patients of all ages, including infants, children, and seniors, at our Charlotte location. We adapt our approach based on individual needs to provide safe and effective care tailored to each patient's unique condition and health goals.

Q4: How long does a typical chiropractic visit take at Revival Family Chiropractic?

A: Your first visit to our Charlotte practice usually takes about 60-90 minutes, allowing ample time for a thorough consultation, examination, and initial treatment plan discussion. Subsequent adjustment visits typically range from 15-30 minutes, depending on your specific care plan.

Q5: Do I need a referral to start chiropractic care at your Charlotte center?

A: No, you can schedule your first appointment directly with Revival Family Chiropractic at our Charlotte location without needing a referral from another healthcare provider. We encourage everyone in our community to experience the benefits of chiropractic care.
