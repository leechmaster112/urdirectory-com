We are dedicated to serving the 1300 NE 56th Avenue Unit, Des Moines, Iowa 57040 community with unparalleled care. FAQ: How is soft washing different from pressure washing? Soft washing uses low pressure combined with specialized cleaning solutions to break down dirt, grime, and organic growth, making it ideal for delicate surfaces like siding. Pressure washing uses high-pressure water streams that can potentially damage your home's exterior. Will soft washing damage my siding? Absolutely not. Our trained technicians use precisely calibrated low-pressure water and eco-friendly detergents, ensuring your siding is cleaned effectively and safely without any risk of damage. What kind of dirt does American Softwash remove from siding? We specialize in removing common culprits like green and black algae, mildew, mold, dirt, cobwebs, and general atmospheric staining that accumulate on siding over time. Do you clean all types of siding? Yes, our soft washing technique is safe and effective for a wide range of siding materials commonly found on homes and businesses in Des Moines, including vinyl, stucco, wood, and more. How often should I have my siding soft washed? The frequency depends on your property's environment, but typically, an annual soft wash can help maintain your siding's appearance and prevent the buildup of damaging contaminants.
