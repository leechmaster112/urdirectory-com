At our Macon center, we begin the journey to better hearing with thorough evaluations. We assess not only your hearing thresholds across different frequencies but also speech understanding abilities in quiet and noisy environments. Choosing the right hearing aid is a highly individual decision. We believe in a collaborative approach. After your evaluation, our team discusses your specific hearing needs, lifestyle, communication challenges, and aesthetic preferences.
