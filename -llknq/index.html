Frequently Asked Questions:

Q1: What types of books can I find at The Cozy Corner Bookstore?

A1: We offer a diverse range of genres including fiction, non-fiction, children's books, young adult, mystery, romance, and more. We also have a selection of gently used books.

A2: Yes, we regularly host local author signings and book club meetings. Please check our website or stop by for our event schedule.
