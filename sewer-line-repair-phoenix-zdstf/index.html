Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What are the signs that I need sewer line repair?

Common signs include slow drains, gurgling sounds from drains, sewage odors inside or outside your home, water pooling near the foundation, and lush patches of grass over buried pipe breaks.

Q2: How long does a typical sewer line repair take?

The duration depends on the extent of the damage and the repair method chosen. Minor repairs might take just a few hours, while more complex replacements could require a day or two. We always provide a clear timeline after inspection.

Q3: Do you offer emergency sewer repair services?

Yes, sewer line emergencies can cause significant damage. We understand the importance of prompt action and are available to address urgent situations at 515 E Grant St, Phoenix, AZ 85004.

Q4: What is the difference between sewer repair and sewer replacement?

Sewer repair involves fixing damaged sections of the existing pipe, often using methods like patching or relining. Replacement involves removing the old pipe and installing a new one, typically when the pipe is beyond repair or outdated.
