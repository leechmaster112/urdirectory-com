Garcia-Windsor, P.C.

Garcia-Windsor, P.C. is a bilingual divorce and family law firm based in the Bishop Arts District of Dallas, Texas. Clients searching for experienced divorce lawyers in Dallas rely on our team for contested and uncontested divorce, high-asset divorce, and division of community and separate property. As a Dallas family law attorney team, we also represent parents in child custody and conservatorship cases, child support calculation, modification and enforcement, spousal maintenance, protective and restraining orders, prenuptial and postnuptial agreements, grandparent and stepparent adoption, common-law marriage and legal separation. Founding attorney Isabela A. Garcia is a Super Lawyers honoree and serves as a consulting attorney for the Mexican Consulate General of Dallas, helping Spanish-speaking families navigate Texas family courts. Every consultation and every step of a case is available in English or Spanish. Se habla español. Garcia-Windsor, P.C. serves Dallas, Oak Cliff, Irving, Grand Prairie, Garland, Mesquite, Arlington and communities across Dallas, Collin, Denton, Tarrant, Ellis, Kaufman and Rockwall counties. If you need a divorce attorney in Dallas, TX, call our office to schedule a confidential consultation.
