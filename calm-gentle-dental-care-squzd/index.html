Calm & Gentle Dental Care

Calm & Gentle Dental Care is a family-friendly dental practice based in Tonbridge and Rustington, Kent, creating beautiful smiles since 2004. Led by Dr. Pieter Claassen, the award-winning team — including Associate Dentist Dr. Fariba Abtahi, dental hygienists Alan Pearson and Christina Youngman, Lead Dental Nurse Jade Northen, and Head Receptionist Gemma Keemer — provides a full range of general, cosmetic, and restorative dental services. From routine exams, fillings, and hygiene care to CEREC crowns, veneers, dental implants, teeth whitening, and facial aesthetics, the practice combines modern technology like digital 3D X-rays and CBCT scans with a genuinely calm, patient-first approach. Winners of the 2022 Doctify Patient Voice Award and members of the British Dental Association, they offer free consultations, emergency care, and children's routine checks at no extra cost when a parent is a regular patient. Both clinics are set in beautiful historical buildings and are open Monday through Friday, with Saturday appointments available for select treatments. The practice places a strong emphasis on supporting anxious patients, making it a trusted choice for those who have long avoided the dentist. As highlighted by patients like Peter Freeman, who went from a lifetime of dental anxiety to genuinely enjoying his regular checkups, the team's patience and reassuring manner make a real difference. Calm & Gentle Dental Care also offers skin care services at their Tonbridge location, straight teeth options including braces and aligners, custom mouth guards for sports and teeth grinding, and oral surgery with a painless approach to extractions. The practice provides complimentary consultations and flexible financing options for complete treatment plans, ensuring quality dental and aesthetic care remains accessible to patients of all ages across Kent. Beyond clinical care, the practice is committed to transparency and community trust, maintaining clear policies around safeguarding for children and adults at risk, and encouraging open feedback through their comment and complaints process. Their affiliation with the General Dental Council and Working Feedback further underscores their dedication to accountability and high professional standards. Patients also benefit from the convenience of early morning appointments starting at 07:45, minimal wait times, and easy access across two locations — reachable by phone at 01903 444120 for Rustington and +44 1732 353402 for Tonbridge, or by email at info@calmandgentledentalcare.co.uk. With a satisfaction guarantee and a focus on using high-quality materials and the latest sterilisation techniques, Calm & Gentle Dental Care continues to set itself apart as a practice where clinical excellence and genuine compassion go hand in hand.
