Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. Specifically, it targets areas of the brain that are underactive in individuals experiencing depression. This targeted stimulation can help to rebalance brain activity, leading to a reduction in depressive symptoms. Unlike some other treatments, TMS therapy does not involve surgery or anesthesia, and most patients can resume their normal activities immediately after a session.
