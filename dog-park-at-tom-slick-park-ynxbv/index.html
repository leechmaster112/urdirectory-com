Dog Park at Tom Slick Park

The Dog Park at Tom Slick Park is a 1-acre fenced off-leash facility on San Antonio's west side with separate areas for small and large dogs. It features benches, picnic tables, a water fountain for dogs and people, a doggie shower, and agility equipment, plus a second fenced enclosure near the Timber Creek entrance adjacent to a pavilion and playground. Hours, photos, and directions are on the park's Doggie Park Near Me listing.
