Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What specific vein conditions do you treat at your Macomb center?

We treat a range of conditions, including spider veins, telangiectasias, reticulated veins, and prominent veins on the face (telangiectasia). We also address symptomatic varicose veins, which can cause pain, swelling, heaviness, or skin changes in the legs.

Q2: What are the typical treatment options available for spider veins and varicose veins in Macomb?

Common treatments include sclerotherapy (injection treatment), endovenous laser treatment (EVLT), radiofrequency ablation (RFA), and surface laser treatments. The specific option recommended depends on the type, size, and severity of the veins.

Q3: Are the treatments performed at your Macomb location painful?

We utilize various techniques to ensure patient comfort. Most procedures involve local anesthesia or numbing agents. While some mild discomfort or a heating sensation might be felt during the treatment, our goal is to minimize pain and ensure a tolerable experience.

Q4: How long is the recovery period after vein treatment at your Macomb facility?

Recovery is typically quick. Most patients can resume light activities and return to work the same day or the next day. We may recommend avoiding strenuous activities, heavy lifting, and prolonged standing for a short period to optimize healing.

Q5: Is there a risk of complications with vein treatments at your Macomb center?

As with any medical procedure, there are potential risks, although they are generally low when performed by experienced professionals using appropriate technology. These can include bruising, swelling, skin discoloration, numbness, or the possibility of blood clots. Our team discusses these potential risks during your consultation.
