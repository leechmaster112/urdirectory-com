What is TMS Therapy?

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is a safe and effective treatment for depression. Using focused magnetic pulses, TMS gently stimulates specific areas of the brain involved in mood regulation. Unlike some other treatments, TMS does not require surgery, anesthesia, or the removal of any hair from the treatment area. At our Cary location, we utilize this technology to offer a targeted approach to managing depression symptoms.
