Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care - Fort Mill

At our Fort Mill clinic, we understand that your pets are family. That's why we offer a comprehensive range of veterinary services designed to keep your furry friends healthy and happy. From routine wellness exams and vaccinations to dental care and surgical procedures, we are here to address all your pet's medical needs with the highest level of care and compassion.

Our facility is equipped with state-of-the-art diagnostic tools and treatment options. We pride ourselves on providing thorough and personalized care, ensuring that each pet receives the attention and treatment they deserve. Our veterinarians are trained to handle a wide array of conditions, from minor illnesses and injuries to more complex health issues.

In addition to our regular veterinary services, Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care offers emergency care for pets in need. Our commitment to 24/7 availability means that even outside of regular business hours, you can bring your pet in for urgent attention. We understand that emergencies don't wait, and we are dedicated to being there for you when they do.

We also provide specialized services such as grooming, boarding, and nutritional counseling. Our goal is to make every visit as comfortable and stress-free as possible for both you and your pet. We believe in building strong relationships with our clients, ensuring that you feel confident and informed about the care your pets receive.

Whether your pet needs a routine check-up or emergency treatment, our team at Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care is here to help. We are committed to providing the best possible care for your pets, right here in Fort Mill.
