Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is probate?

A: Probate is the legal process after a person's death where their estate (assets and liabilities) is officially recognized, managed, and distributed according to their will or Texas intestacy laws.

Q2: Do I need a lawyer for probate?

A: While it is possible to handle probate without legal representation, the process can be complex and legally demanding. Having an experienced probate attorney in Laredo ensures your rights are protected and the process is handled correctly.

Q3: How long does probate take in Laredo, Texas?

A: The duration of probate varies significantly depending on the estate's complexity, whether there are disputes, and the specific circumstances. Our firm works efficiently to complete the process as quickly as possible while adhering to legal requirements.

Q4: What are the costs associated with probate services?

A: Attorney fees are typically based on an hourly rate or a flat fee for specific services. Additional costs may include court fees, appraisal fees, and other administrative expenses. We provide transparent cost estimates during your initial consultation.

Q5: Can I be an executor or administrator without a lawyer?

A: Yes, you can be appointed as executor or administrator without legal counsel. However, the role carries significant responsibilities, and legal guidance helps ensure compliance with Texas law and protects your interests.
