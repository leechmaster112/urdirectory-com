Morton Avenue Dog Park

Morton Avenue Dog Park is a fenced off-leash area in Louisville's Highlands neighborhood, free to the public and adjacent to Patterson Playground. Dogs play on a mulch surface under shade trees, with water, waste bags, communal toys, and bench seating for owners; street parking is available nearby. Hours, photos, and directions are on the park's Doggie Park Near Me listing.

Frequently Asked Questions for Morton Avenue Dog Park:

Q: Is there an entrance fee to use Morton Avenue Dog Park?

A: No, Morton Avenue Dog Park is a free-to-use facility for all Louisville residents.

Q: What are the hours of operation for the dog park?

A: The park is open during daylight hours, ensuring your dog can enjoy playtime safely.

Q: Are there separate areas for large and small dogs?

A: Morton Avenue Dog Park is designed as a single, fenced area suitable for dogs of all sizes to interact.

Q: What amenities are available at the park?

A: The primary amenity is the secure, fenced off-leash area, providing ample space for dogs to run and play.

Q: Can I bring my dog to the park if they are not yet vaccinated?

A: While we encourage all dogs to be up-to-date on vaccinations for their safety and the safety of others, it is a personal decision for owners to make based on their veterinarian's advice.
