Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of employment issues do you handle for clients in Toronto?

We primarily handle matters related to employment law in Ontario, focusing on issues like wrongful dismissal, constructive dismissal, disputes over pay deductions, claims of workplace harassment or discrimination, and questions concerning employment standards such as vacation pay and termination notice.

Q2: What constitutes "wrongful dismissal" in Ontario?

Wrongful dismissal occurs when an employer terminates an employee's contract without providing sufficient reason, without cause, or fails to provide the statutory minimum notice or pay in lieu as mandated by the Ontario Employment Standards Act or the common law.

Q3: How long do I have to file a claim after being dismissed?

The time limit for filing a claim related to wrongful dismissal or other employment disputes in Ontario is generally two years from the date of termination or the date you became aware of the act or omission forming the basis of your claim.

Q4: Can I get reinstated if I was wrongfully dismissed?

In some cases, particularly if the employer's actions are deemed sufficiently egregious, reinstatement can be part of the remedy. However, this is often negotiated and depends on the specific circumstances. Our firm will advise you on the most appropriate course of action, which may include reinstatement, compensation, or both.

Q5: What should I do immediately after being fired?

It is advisable to gather all relevant documents (employment contract, performance reviews, HR communications, pay stubs, etc.). Document any communication regarding your termination.
