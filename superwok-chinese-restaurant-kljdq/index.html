Location

SuperWok Chinese Restaurant invites you to experience the authentic taste of China right here in Virginia Beach. Located at 5312 Kemps River Dr, our restaurant specializes in serving classic Chinese dishes prepared with traditional recipes and fresh ingredients. We pride ourselves on offering a diverse menu featuring popular standard Chinese favorites alongside satisfying combo plates designed for sharing or individual enjoyment.

Our counter-service setting ensures a quick and efficient dining experience, perfect for those seeking a delicious meal without the formalities of a full-service restaurant. Whether you're craving savory entrees, flavorful appetizers, or a comforting side dish, our dedicated kitchen is committed to bringing you quality Chinese cuisine that satisfies your cravings.

At SuperWok, we focus on delivering the essence of Chinese flavors in a straightforward, no-frills environment. Our location at 5312 Kemps River Dr makes us easily accessible to residents and visitors alike, providing a convenient spot to enjoy a satisfying meal. We are committed to serving the Virginia Beach community with delicious food and excellent service.

For more information about our menu or to make a reservation, please visit our website at superwokvb.com or call us at (757) 938-9158.
