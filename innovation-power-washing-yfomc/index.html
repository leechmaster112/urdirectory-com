Our Core Services

At Innovation Power Washing, we offer a comprehensive range of exterior cleaning solutions designed to tackle various surfaces and stubborn dirt:

Pressure Washing: Effectively remove built-up dirt, grime, mold, mildew, and algae from driveways, sidewalks, decks, patios, siding, and more. Our powerful yet controlled water pressure restores surfaces to their original condition.

Deck & Patio Cleaning: Reclaim the beauty of your outdoor living spaces. We remove accumulated dirt, sand, stains, and moss, leaving decks and patios clean, sanitized, and ready for relaxation.

Exterior House Washing: Protect your home's siding, brick, and other exterior materials from the damaging effects of accumulated dirt and environmental contaminants. A thorough wash improves appearance and can even help prevent premature deterioration.

Window Cleaning: More than just streak-free glass. We meticulously clean windows, including sills and frames, ensuring crystal clarity and optimal visibility for your home or business.

Soft Washing: A gentler cleaning method using lower pressure and specialized solutions. Ideal for sensitive surfaces like vinyl siding, wood, gutters, and landscaping elements, ensuring thorough cleaning without causing damage.
