What are the typical recovery timelines for procedures at Goel Plastic Surgery?

Recovery varies significantly by procedure. For instance, less invasive facial treatments might involve just a few days of downtime, while more extensive body contouring can require several weeks of careful rest and adherence to post-operative instructions. We provide detailed recovery plans tailored to your specific procedure during your consultation.

How do I know if I'm a good candidate for a specific procedure?

A thorough consultation with our highly trained surgeons is the best way to determine candidacy. We assess your overall health, individual anatomy, and aesthetic goals to ensure the chosen procedure is both safe and likely to yield the results you desire.
