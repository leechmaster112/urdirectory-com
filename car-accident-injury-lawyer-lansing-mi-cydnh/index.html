Michigan no-fault basics and why a lawyer still matters in Lansing Michigan uses a no-fault auto insurance system, which means your own policy’s Personal Injury Protection (PIP) coverage typically pays your crash-related medical bills and some wage-loss benefits, no matter who caused the wreck. But after Michigan’s recent auto insurance reforms, the fine print matters a lot: Drivers can now choose among several PIP medical limits, from unlimited down to capped options, instead of everyone automatically having unlimited lifetime medical coverage.
