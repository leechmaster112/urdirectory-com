Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Do I need prior experience to rent a jet ski?

A1: No prior experience is required. We offer guided tours and lessons for beginners. However, rentals are only available to those who are certified or accompanied by a guide.

Q2: What safety measures do you have in place?

A2: Safety is our top priority. All our jet skis are equipped with life jackets, and our staff ensures that guests are properly trained and briefed before any ride. We also conduct regular equipment checks.

Q3: Can I customize my jet ski tour?

A3: Yes! We offer customizable tours depending on your preferences and interests. Let us know what you're looking for, and we'll tailor your experience accordingly.

Q4: What is the age limit for jet ski rentals?

A4: Guests must be at least 18 years old to rent a jet ski independently. Minors must be accompanied by an adult or a guide.

Q5: Are there any discounts for group bookings?

A5: Yes, we offer special discounts for group bookings. Contact us at (786) 790-5465 or visit our website https://jetskisworldmiami.com/ for more details.
