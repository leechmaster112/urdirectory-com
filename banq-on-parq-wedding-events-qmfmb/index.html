Banq on Parq Wedding & Events

Banq on Parq is a stunning, one-of-a-kind event venue located in the heart of downtown Plainfield, NJ. Housed in a beautifully restored 1920s bank building — once part of a neighborhood known as the "Hollywood of the East" — this multi-level space seamlessly blends historic opulence with modern versatility. Original brass, gold, and marble details set the tone for an atmosphere that is both grand and entirely adaptable. The main ballroom features soaring 25-foot ceilings, industrial architectural accents, and floor-to-ceiling glass doors that bathe the space in natural light, accommodating up to 300 guests. Whether you're envisioning a wedding, corporate event, art exhibition, or private celebration, Banq on Parq's open, flexible layout can be styled and transformed to bring any vision to life. As a fully DIY event space available seven days a week, clients have complete creative control — bring your own caterer, your own vendors, and your own style. Standard rentals include 8 hours for setup and breakdown, with extensions available for larger productions. Part of the By Landmark family of premier venues, Banq on Parq is where history meets limitless possibility.
