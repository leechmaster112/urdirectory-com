Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What types of employment law issues do you handle for employees in Toronto?

A1: We handle a wide range of employee-related employment law issues in Toronto, including wrongful dismissal, constructive dismissal, workplace harassment and discrimination claims, disputes over pay and benefits, issues related to workplace safety, and complaints to the Ontario Human Rights Commission or the Ontario Labour Relations Board.

Q2: Can you help me if I was dismissed without notice or pay in Toronto?

A2: Yes, if you were dismissed without notice or pay, this could potentially constitute wrongful dismissal. We can assess your situation based on your employment contract, length of service, and Ontario's Employment Standards Act to advise you on your entitlements and explore options for pursuing a claim.

Q3: How long do I have to file a complaint for workplace harassment in Toronto?

A3: Time limits are crucial in employment law claims. Generally, for claims related to the Ontario Human Rights Code (like harassment), you typically have one year from the date you became aware of the violation to file a formal complaint with the Ontario Human Rights Commission or commence a lawsuit. It's essential to seek legal advice promptly.

Q4: Do you represent employers in Toronto facing employment disputes?

A4: Yes, we provide services to employers located in Toronto. We advise on compliance with employment standards and human rights legislation, help draft and review employment contracts, represent clients in disputes with employees or government bodies like the OLRB or OHRC, and assist with developing fair workplace policies.

Q5: What should I expect during my first consultation at your Toronto office?

A5: During your first consultation at our Toronto office (45 St Clair Ave W), we will listen carefully to your situation and employment history. We will ask relevant questions to understand the nature of your issue. We will then provide an initial assessment of your legal rights and options, answer your questions to the best of our ability, and advise whether we can assist you and what the next steps might involve.
