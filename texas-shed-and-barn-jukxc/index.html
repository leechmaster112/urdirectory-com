Texas Shed and Barn

Texas Shed and Barn is a trusted shed builder serving Montgomery and surrounding areas, specializing in high-quality portable buildings and custom outdoor structures for residential, agricultural, and commercial properties. We are committed to delivering durable craftsmanship, premium materials, and personalized solutions to meet your storage and workspace needs. Our selection includes custom sheds, storage buildings, workshops, barns, cabins, and metal buildings designed to provide secure, functional, and long-lasting space. Whether you need extra storage, a dedicated workspace, or a versatile backyard structure, we offer a variety of designs and customization options to fit your property. Texas Shed and Barn also builds carports, boat covers, and RV caves to help protect your vehicles and equipment from the elements. Looking for a personal retreat? We create custom she sheds and man caves that provide comfortable, functional spaces for hobbies, relaxation, or entertainment. Whether you're searching for a reliable shed, a spacious barn, a durable metal building, or a custom workshop, Texas Shed and Barn is your trusted choice in Montgomery for quality construction, exceptional service, and outdoor building solutions built to last.
