Our Assessment Process

Our assessment begins with a comprehensive consultation to understand your child's developmental history and specific communication challenges. We then conduct formal evaluations using standardised tools to measure areas such as:

Receptive and expressive language skills Speech sound production Reading and writing abilities Pragmatic language (social communication)

These assessments help us create a detailed profile of your child's strengths and areas for development.

Therapy Approaches

Our therapy sessions are designed to be engaging and effective. We use evidence-based techniques including:

Articulation therapy for speech sound disorders Language enrichment activities for vocabulary and grammar development Pragmatic intervention to improve conversational skills Oral motor exercises when appropriate

Therapy is delivered either one-on-one with your child or through parent coaching sessions to ensure skills are practiced consistently at home.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What age of children do you work with?

We provide services for children from 2 to 11 years old in Petts Wood. Our assessments and therapy approaches are specifically designed for this developmental stage.

Q2: How long does therapy typically last?

The duration of therapy varies depending on your child's specific needs. Most children show significant progress within 6-12 months of consistent therapy sessions at our Petts Wood location.

Q3: Do you offer school consultations?

Yes, we can provide school consultations at your child's local primary school in Petts Wood. Our therapists work collaboratively with educators to support classroom communication.

Q4: What should I bring to my initial assessment?

Please bring your child's birth certificate or passport, any previous assessment reports, and a list of medications. We recommend bringing healthy snacks and drinks for your child.
