The Albion Summit Hotel

The Albion is a landmark wedding and event venue in the heart of Summit, NJ, where historic grandeur meets modern elegance. Originally established in 1868 as an urban cultural center, this iconic property has been thoughtfully reimagined by By Landmark, blending its storied architectural heritage with a sophisticated, contemporary hospitality experience. The Summit Grand ballroom accommodates up to 200 guests for indoor ceremonies and receptions, featuring a dramatic two-story double-sided fireplace, eight antique brass geometric chandeliers, and an open kitchen for cocktail hour. The venue's charming historic exterior and lush grounds provide a stunning backdrop for portraits and wedding party photos. With 100 stylish onsite hotel rooms and suites, The Albion is the ultimate all-in-one wedding weekend destination — from rehearsal dinners and ceremonies to after-parties in the intimate Whiskey Lounge. Our award-winning chefs deliver a farm-to-table culinary experience, and dedicated "Memorymakers" are with you every step of the way to ensure a seamless, stress-free celebration. Timeless charm. Modern luxury. Unforgettable memories.
