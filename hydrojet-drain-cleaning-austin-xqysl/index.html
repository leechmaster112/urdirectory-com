Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is hydro jetting, and how does it work?

A1: Hydro jetting is a professional drain cleaning technique that uses high-pressure, hot water to clear drain clogs. Our specialized equipment pushes water through your drain pipe at high velocity, effectively breaking down and flushing away grease, hair, soap scum, paper, roots, and other common blockages.

Q2: How long does a typical hydro jetting service take?

A2: The duration of a hydro jetting service depends on the severity of the clog and the pipe's length. Generally, most residential jobs can be completed within 30 minutes to an hour. We always provide a clear estimate before starting work.

Q3: Is hydro jetting safe for my pipes?

A3: Yes, hydro jetting is generally safe for most standard PVC and cast iron pipes when performed by experienced professionals. Our equipment is calibrated to the correct pressure, and we assess your specific pipe type before treatment to ensure safety.

Q4: Can you help with clogged sewer lines?

A4: Absolutely. Hydro jetting is one of the most effective methods for clearing clogged sewer lines. It can handle significant blockages in main drains without the need for invasive digging or potentially damaging chemicals.

Q5: Do I need to be home for the service?

A5: While we appreciate having you present for minor jobs, for larger projects or sewer line cleaning, we may ask you to be available to discuss the issue and grant access. We will confirm any requirements before scheduling your appointment.
