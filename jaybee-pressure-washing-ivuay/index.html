Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve the Lagrange, GA area and surrounding communities. Please contact us for specific service availability based on your location.

How long does a typical pressure washing job take?

The duration depends on the size of the area, the level of dirt and debris, and the specific services requested. We provide a timeline estimate during your free consultation.

Do you use environmentally friendly cleaning solutions?

We prioritize using effective yet safe cleaning solutions. We can discuss environmentally conscious options available for your project.

Is pressure washing safe for all surfaces?

While pressure washing is powerful, our trained team uses appropriate techniques and equipment for various surfaces. We always conduct a pre-inspection and recommend against washing certain delicate materials (like wood with peeling paint) unless specified otherwise.

Do I need to be home during the service?

While not mandatory, we appreciate the opportunity to discuss any specific instructions or concerns with you before starting the job.
