Location

Kildare Road Medical Centre, located at 36 Kildare Road, Blacktown, NSW, 2148, has been a cornerstone of primary healthcare in the Blacktown area since its doors opened in April 2010. Following a comprehensive refurbishment, the centre was established to provide high-quality, patient-focused medical services to the residents of Blacktown. The dedicated team at Kildare Road Medical Centre is committed to delivering comprehensive healthcare in a comfortable and accessible environment.

Our Services

At Kildare Road Medical Centre, we offer a wide range of primary healthcare services tailored to meet the needs of our patients living in Blacktown. Our experienced doctors provide general medical consultations for adults and children, addressing common illnesses, injuries, and preventative health matters. We focus on building long-term relationships with our patients to ensure continuity of care.

We offer comprehensive health assessments, including regular check-ups, health screenings, and vaccination programs. Our team is dedicated to staying updated with the latest medical advancements to provide evidence-based care. We also manage chronic conditions such as asthma, diabetes, hypertension, and cardiovascular issues, working closely with patients to develop personalized treatment plans.

For minor ailments and injuries, our centre provides prompt treatment for conditions like colds, flu, respiratory infections, skin issues, and minor trauma. We emphasize preventative healthcare, offering lifestyle advice, health promotion programs, and guidance on maintaining overall wellness. Our commitment extends to providing compassionate care for women's health, including antenatal care and general gynaecological consultations.

We understand the importance of accessible healthcare, and our centre is equipped to handle routine procedures and minor surgeries when medically indicated. Our focus is always on providing thorough, patient-centred care within our Blacktown community.

Our Commitment to Patient Care

At Kildare Road Medical Centre, we pride ourselves on creating a welcoming atmosphere where patients feel comfortable discussing their health concerns. Our experienced medical team takes the time to listen carefully, explain diagnoses clearly, and involve patients in shared decision-making about their treatment. We believe in treating every patient with respect and dignity, regardless of age or background.

We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of clinical practice and infection control. Our well-maintained facility ensures a clean and comfortable environment for all appointments. We strive to minimize waiting times and offer flexible appointment scheduling to accommodate the busy lives of our Blacktown patients.
