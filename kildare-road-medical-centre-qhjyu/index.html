Location

Kildare Road Medical Centre, located at 36 Kildare Road, Blacktown, NSW, 2148, has been a cornerstone of primary healthcare in the Blacktown community since its doors opened in April 2010. Following a comprehensive refurbishment, the centre was established to provide a modern, comfortable, and efficient medical facility dedicated to the health and well-being of its local patients.

Our mission is simple: to deliver high-quality, patient-focused medical care tailored to the needs of individuals and families residing in the Blacktown area. We pride ourselves on maintaining a welcoming and professional environment where patients feel comfortable discussing their health concerns with our experienced and compassionate medical team.

At Kildare Road Medical Centre, we offer a comprehensive range of general medical services designed to address the health needs of our diverse patient base. Our experienced doctors provide thorough health assessments, diagnose and manage acute and chronic illnesses, and offer evidence-based treatments. We are committed to staying abreast of the latest medical advancements to ensure our patients receive the best possible care for their specific conditions.

Well-child care is a vital part of our service. Our team includes paediatricians experienced in the care of infants, children, and adolescents. We offer comprehensive check-ups, vaccinations, developmental assessments, and guidance on nutrition, safety, and healthy habits to support children's growth and development from birth through adolescence.

For adult patients, we provide comprehensive health management, including preventative screenings, management of chronic conditions such as hypertension, diabetes, asthma, and arthritis, and treatment of common acute illnesses. Our focus is on building long-term relationships with patients to ensure continuity of care and effective health management over time.

We understand the importance of accessible healthcare and strive to provide convenient appointment scheduling. Our experienced administrative staff works efficiently to accommodate patient needs, ensuring a smooth registration process and clear communication regarding appointments and results.

Our modern facility is equipped with the latest diagnostic tools and technology to support our clinical services. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of clinical practice, infection control, and patient confidentiality in line with all relevant healthcare regulations.

Whether you require a routine health check, management of an ongoing condition, or treatment for an acute illness, our dedicated team at Kildare Road Medical Centre is here to provide expert medical care close to home. We are focused solely on serving the health needs of the Blacktown community from our single, dedicated location at 36 Kildare Road.

We encourage you to contact us to learn more about our services or to schedule an appointment with one of our experienced doctors. Your health is our priority.
