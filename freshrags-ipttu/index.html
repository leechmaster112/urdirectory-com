Frequently Asked Questions

What types of printing methods do you offer?

We offer screen printing, digital printing, and heat transfer printing. Each method has its advantages depending on the design complexity, quantity, and fabric type. Our team can help you choose the best option for your project.

Do you offer design services?

Yes! We can help you brainstorm ideas, sketch concepts, or even assist with simple digital design creation. For more complex designs, we recommend providing a clear file if you have one ready.

What is your minimum order quantity?

We strive to be flexible! While we can handle small orders, bulk quantities generally yield the best pricing. Our team will provide you with specific minimums and pricing tiers during your quote request.

How long does production typically take?

Production times vary based on order size, design complexity, and printing method. Generally, standard orders are completed within 3-5 business days. Rush services are often available for an additional fee. We always provide clear turnaround estimates.

Do you ship outside of Batavia, OH?

Absolutely! We ship custom t-shirts nationwide. Shipping costs and times will depend on your location and the delivery method chosen. We'll provide you with all necessary details during checkout.
