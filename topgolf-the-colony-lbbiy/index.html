Topgolf The Colony

Topgolf The Colony is a DFW favorite for golf, games, and game-day energy. Tee off from 102 climate-controlled bays across three floors with Toptracer ball-tracking, then catch the action on our giant HD Spectacular screen, then dive into our new arcade. Charge up at our EV stations, gather at the terrace fire pits, and play classics like Giant Jenga, Giant Connect 4, foosball, and shuffleboard. A full menu of shareable plates and signature cocktails keeps the table happy, while semi-private and private event spaces plus an indoor stage make hosting birthdays, corporate outings, and celebrations easy — open year-round, rain or shine.

Frequently Asked Questions for Topgolf The Colony:

Q: As a first-time visitor, what kind of golf games can I expect to play?

A: For newcomers, Topgolf The Colony offers a range of accessible golf games like Topgolf's signature Lifetime Achievement, where you aim for various targets. Our bay monitors guide you through each game with simple scoring, making it fun for everyone.

Q: Do I need to bring my own golf clubs?

A: Not at all! Topgolf The Colony provides complimentary golf clubs in various sizes for both left-handed and right-handed players in every bay.

Q: Can I order food and drinks while playing in the hitting bay?

A: Absolutely. Our servers are ready to take your food and drink orders directly to your bay, ensuring you never miss a moment of the action or conversation.

Q: Is Topgolf The Colony suitable for private events?

A: Yes, we offer private event spaces and can accommodate gatherings of various sizes, from birthday parties to corporate outings, all within our unique setting at 3760 Blair Oaks Drive.
