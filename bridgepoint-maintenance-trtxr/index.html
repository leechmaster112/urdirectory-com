Bridgepoint Maintenance

Bridgepoint Maintenance is a property maintenance and general contracting company that keeps residential and commercial buildings running for owners, landlords, and property managers. The team responds to emergency repairs around the clock and handles routine upkeep, unit turnovers, and make ready work between tenants. Services span plumbing, electrical, heating and cooling, carpentry, drywall, painting, flooring, appliance repair, and complete unit renovations. Every job is coordinated from the first call through the final walkthrough by a single point of contact, so owners always know the status of their property and the cost before work begins. Bridgepoint Maintenance works across portfolios of every size, from a single rental unit to large multi unit residential and commercial properties. Clients rely on the company to protect the value of their assets, keep tenants comfortable and safe, and resolve issues quickly before they grow into expensive repairs. Clear communication, documented work, transparent pricing, and dependable scheduling are the standard on every project.
