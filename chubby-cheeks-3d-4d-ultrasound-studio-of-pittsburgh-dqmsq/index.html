Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What types of ultrasound services do you offer at your Pittsburgh location?

A1: At our Chubby Cheeks 3D/4D Ultrasound Studio located at 224 Penn Ave Suite 2C, Pittsburgh, PA 15221, we primarily offer elective 3D and 4D ultrasounds for viewing your baby's features. We also provide standard 2D ultrasounds when requested by a healthcare provider for diagnostic purposes.

Q2: How early can I schedule a 3D/4D ultrasound appointment at your Pittsburgh studio?

A2: We typically recommend scheduling a 3D/4D ultrasound appointment between 14 and 26 weeks of pregnancy for the best image quality. This is usually the period when the baby's features are most developed and clear. Please call our Pittsburgh location at (412) 444-8742 to schedule your appointment.

Q3: Do I need a doctor's note to get a 3D/4D ultrasound at your Pittsburgh studio?

A3: While a 3D/4D ultrasound is an elective, non-diagnostic service focused on viewing your baby, we do require a valid pregnancy confirmation (such as a recent ultrasound report or doctor's note indicating gestational age) to schedule your appointment at our Pittsburgh studio.

Q4: What should I expect during my 3D/4D ultrasound appointment at 224 Penn Ave?

A4: Your appointment will typically last about 45 minutes. You will lie on an examination table, and a sonographer will apply a warm gel to your abdomen and use a transducer to capture images. The baby's position will greatly influence the quality and detail of the images shown on the screen in real-time.

Q5: Can I bring family members with me to my ultrasound appointment at your Pittsburgh studio?

A5: Absolutely! We encourage bringing partners, significant others, and other family members to share in the special experience of seeing your baby through ultrasound at our Pittsburgh location. We provide a comfortable waiting area and look forward to welcoming your family.
