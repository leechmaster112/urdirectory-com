FAQ

Q: What kind of customization options are available for my outdoor kitchen?

A: We offer extensive customization, including a wide range of grill types and brands, countertop materials like granite and concrete, built-in appliances such as refrigerators and side burners, and integrated seating and bar areas, all tailored to your specific needs and aesthetic preferences.

Q: How long does the process of building a custom outdoor kitchen typically take?

A: The timeline varies based on the complexity and size of the project, but generally, from initial consultation and design to final construction, a custom outdoor kitchen can take anywhere from 4 to 12 weeks.

Q: Do you handle all aspects of the outdoor kitchen build, including utilities?

A: Yes, we manage the entire project from start to finish, which includes coordinating and overseeing necessary utility work such as gas lines, electrical connections, and plumbing, ensuring a fully functional and safe outdoor kitchen.

Q: What materials do you recommend for outdoor kitchens in the Fort Myers climate?

A: We prioritize durable, weather-resistant materials suited for our local climate, recommending options like stainless steel for appliances and cabinetry, and robust countertop materials like granite or concrete that can withstand sun, humidity, and occasional rain.

Q: Can you design an outdoor kitchen that incorporates a specific cooking style, like a pizza oven or smoker?

A: Absolutely. We specialize in integrating various cooking elements, from high-end grills and smokers to wood-fired pizza ovens and hibachi-style griddles, creating a custom culinary experience tailored to your passion.
