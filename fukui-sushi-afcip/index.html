Location

Fukui Sushi, located at 1612 Bayview Ave, Toronto, ON M4G 3B7, invites you to experience the pinnacle of Japanese culinary art right here in the heart of Toronto. Our establishment is dedicated to serving the finest sushi and authentic Japanese cuisine, crafted with passion, precision, and the highest quality ingredients. We take pride in offering an unforgettable dining experience that transports you straight to the essence of Japan.

At Fukui Sushi, we specialize in creating exquisite sushi, sashimi, and other traditional Japanese dishes. Our skilled chefs, trained in the time-honored techniques of Edomae-style sushi, meticulously prepare each piece to ensure unparalleled freshness, flavor, and texture. Whether you are a sushi connoisseur or a curious first-timer, our menu offers a diverse selection to satisfy any palate.

For those seeking an intimate and personalized dining experience, we offer Omakase, the chef's choice presentation. This unique service allows our culinary experts to guide you through a carefully curated selection of their finest creations, ensuring a memorable journey through Japan's most cherished flavors. Beyond sushi, our menu features a range of other Japanese delights, including hot dishes, appetizers, and desserts, all prepared with the same commitment to quality and authenticity.

Our atmosphere at 1612 Bayview Ave is designed to be both welcoming and elegant, providing the perfect setting for a special occasion, a romantic dinner, or a casual gathering with friends and family. We believe that dining is not just about the food but also about the experience, and we strive to make every visit to Fukui Sushi a cherished one.

Discover the art of sushi and the warmth of Japanese hospitality at Fukui Sushi. We look forward to serving you.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What types of sushi do you offer? We offer a wide variety of sushi, including traditional rolls, nigiri, sashimi, and other Japanese seafood delicacies. Our menu features the freshest ingredients, sourced daily to ensure optimal quality.

2. Do you offer an Omakase experience? Yes, we offer an Omakase service where our chef curates a personalized selection of their finest dishes. This experience is available for individuals and groups and provides a unique journey through Japanese culinary art.

3. What are your business hours? Fukui Sushi is open for lunch and dinner, seven days a week. Our hours may vary, so please check our website or give us a call at (416) 487-3388 for the most up-to-date information.

4. Do you take reservations? We recommend making a reservation, especially for the Omakase experience or during peak dining times. You can book your table online via our website or by calling us directly.

5. Is Fukui Sushi accessible for people with disabilities? We are committed to providing a comfortable dining experience for all our guests. Please call us at (416) 487-3388 to inquire about accessibility features at our location on Bayview Ave.
