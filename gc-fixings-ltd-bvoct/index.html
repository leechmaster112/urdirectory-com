GC Fixings Ltd

GC Fixings Ltd is a trusted, family-run UPVC installation specialist based in Ipswich, Suffolk, with over 25 years of experience serving homeowners across Suffolk and Essex. Owner Gareth Canham personally handles every survey, free quotation and installation, ensuring a reliable, professional finish on every job. Services include double glazed window installation, replacement glazing, A-rated energy efficient windows, misted unit repairs, UPVC window frame repairs, UPVC door installation, external door replacement and double glazed door fitting. GC Fixings also designs and installs UPVC conservatories, replaces conservatory roofs, repairs conservatory leaks and replaces inefficient glazing panels. The roofline division covers full fascia and soffit installation, bargeboard replacement, guttering, downpipe installation and complete roofline upgrades. Exterior cladding installation and residential gutter cleaning complete the service range. Call today for a free, no-obligation quote.
