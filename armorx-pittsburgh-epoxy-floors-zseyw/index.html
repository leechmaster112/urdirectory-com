Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: How long does an epoxy floor installation typically take at your Pittsburgh location?

A: The timeline for an epoxy floor project depends on the size of the area, the complexity of the design, and the specific type of epoxy chosen. Generally, the actual installation process takes 1-2 days. However, the full curing time for the epoxy to reach its maximum strength and durability is typically 5-7 days, during which foot traffic and heavy equipment should be restricted.

Q2: Is epoxy flooring suitable for residential garages in Pittsburgh?

A: Absolutely! Epoxy flooring is an excellent choice for Pittsburgh garages. It provides incredible durability against oil spills, tire marks, and heavy vehicle traffic. It's easy to clean, available in numerous attractive styles, and can significantly increase the value of your home.

Q3: Can you match a specific color or pattern for my epoxy floor project at 241 Bascom Ave?

A: Yes, we can work with you to achieve a specific look. We offer a wide range of base colors and additives (like metallics, flake, and stains). For the most accurate match, we recommend providing a physical sample or a detailed color code. Our team can discuss possibilities and help you find the closest match or create a custom design.

Q4: How durable is commercial epoxy flooring, especially for a Pittsburgh restaurant?

A: Commercial-grade epoxy flooring from ArmorX Pittsburgh Epoxy Floors is highly durable and specifically designed for high-traffic areas like restaurants. It offers excellent resistance to chemicals, spills (common in food service), heavy foot traffic, and impacts. With proper maintenance, it provides a long-lasting, hygienic, and attractive floor surface.

Q5: Do you handle the preparation of the existing floor before applying epoxy at your Pittsburgh location?

A: Yes, thorough surface preparation is crucial for a successful epoxy installation. Our process typically includes cleaning the concrete substrate, repairing any cracks or damage, and ensuring the surface is smooth and free from contaminants. This preparation step is included in our standard installation process here at 241 Bascom Ave.
