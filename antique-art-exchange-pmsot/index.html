Antique & Art Exchange

Antique & Art Exchange is San Francisco's premier source for antique furniture, antique mirrors, and English and Continental decorative arts — trusted by West Coast and international collectors for over 28 years. Our 2,600 sq. ft. showroom inside the SF Design Center carries ceramics, books, accessories, and one-of-a-kind pieces you won't find anywhere else in the city. A private 8,000 sq. ft. warehouse near the Flower Market means far deeper inventory than our showroom floor suggests. Open M–F 9am–4pm, Sat 10am–3pm, and by appointment - whether you're starting your search or finishing it, we're the right stop.
