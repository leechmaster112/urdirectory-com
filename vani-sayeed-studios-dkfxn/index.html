Vani Sayeed Studios

Vani Sayeed Studios is an award-winning, multi-disciplined interior design firm located in Newton, Massachusetts, serving discerning residential and commercial clients throughout Greater Boston and beyond for over a decade. Founded by interior designer and fine artist Vani Sayeed, the studio brings a distinctive globally-inspired perspective to every project, creating masterfully layered spaces that seamlessly blend architectural elegance with rich cultural heritage. With credentials including a BFA in interior design from the prestigious Sir J.J. School of Art in Mumbai and an MFA in design from the University of Iowa, Vani's cross-cultural background infuses each project with sophisticated design sensibility that honors classic traditions while embracing innovation. The studio's work has been featured in prominent publications including Architectural Digest, Elle Décor, House Beautiful, Boston Globe, and New England Home, and showcased on HGTV's "White Room Challenge." Specializing in turnkey interior design solutions, Vani Sayeed Studios offers comprehensive services ranging from residential renovations and custom kitchen design to fine art consulting and handmade rug design. The firm's unique artistic approach, where Vani's award-winning fine art practice directly influences the design process, results in thoughtfully composed spaces that are visually striking, deeply personal, and perfectly functional. The studio is renowned for its meticulous attention to detail, from hand-drawn scaled layouts to carefully curated custom elements including exclusive rug collections created in collaboration with Landry & Arcari. Each project is approached with core values of client focus, collaboration, and craftsmanship, creating interior environments that are synergistic with client personalities and lifestyle needs. The studio's exceptional design excellence has been recognized with multiple prestigious awards, including numerous PRISM Awards for Best Interior Design (2024, 2023, 2022, 2018, 2017, 2016) and IFDA-NE Design Showcase Awards, with Vani earning Interior Designer of the Year in 2023. Serving communities throughout Newton, Wellesley, Brookline, Cambridge, Chestnut Hill, and the Greater Boston area, Vani Sayeed Studios provides personalized design services for projects of all scales, from intimate studios to expansive estates and commercial properties. Whether you're seeking luxury interior design, custom rug creation, or complete home transformation, the studio's extensive knowledge base and global resources ensure spaces that are not only beautiful but timeless, inviting, and uniquely yours.
