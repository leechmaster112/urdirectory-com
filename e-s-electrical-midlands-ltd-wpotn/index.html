E S ELECTRICAL (Midlands) Ltd

E S ELECTRICAL (Midlands) Ltd delivers trusted electrical contracting services across Northampton, Northamptonshire, and the wider Midlands region. Based at Station Farm in Horton, the team brings extensive expertise in domestic and commercial electrical installations, rewiring, consumer unit upgrades, and fault finding — covering everything from new build wiring to full property refurbishments. With a reputation built on reliability, safety compliance, and quality workmanship, this Northampton-based electrical company holds the certifications and accreditations that give homeowners, landlords, and businesses complete peace of mind. All work is carried out to the latest 18th Edition IET Wiring Regulations and Part P Building Regulations standards, ensuring every project meets the highest safety requirements. Whether it's a routine electrical inspection, an EICR (Electrical Installation Condition Report), EV charger installation, outdoor lighting, or a full commercial fit-out, the skilled electricians at ES Electrical serve clients across Northampton, Wellingborough, Daventry, Milton Keynes, and surrounding areas of the East and West Midlands. Competitive pricing, prompt response times, and transparent communication make them a first-choice electrical contractor for both residential and commercial customers throughout the region.
