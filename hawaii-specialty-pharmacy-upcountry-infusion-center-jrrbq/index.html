Hawaii Specialty Pharmacy - Upcountry Infusion Center

Hawaii Specialty Pharmacy, an Acelpa Health company, is an infusion center on Kupaoa Street in Makawao, serving upcountry Maui and beyond. We provide infusion therapy on-site and at home. Therapies include immune globulin (IVIG and SCIG), biologic infusions, and oncology infusion support for conditions in neurology, gastroenterology, dermatology, rheumatology, and immunology. Our pharmacists and nurses build a treatment plan for every patient and support benefits verification and prior authorization. An on-call pharmacist is available 24 hours a day at 833.767.5663. Referrals are accepted from hospitals, clinics, and physician offices.

Frequently Asked Questions:

What types of specialty medications can I receive here?

Our Makawao infusion center is equipped to administer a range of specialty medications, focusing on those requiring careful handling and administration by trained professionals.

How long does a typical infusion session last?

Infusion durations vary depending on the specific medication and dosage prescribed by your physician. Our team will provide you with an estimated timeframe before your treatment begins.

Will I see the same nurse each visit?

While we strive for consistency, our priority is to ensure you receive care from a qualified and experienced nurse during each visit to our Kupaoa Street location.

What should I do if I experience any discomfort during my infusion?

Please inform your nurse immediately if you experience any discomfort or unusual symptoms. We are here to monitor you closely and address any concerns promptly.

Is there anything I need to bring for my first infusion appointment?

Please bring your insurance card, a list of current medications, and any necessary referral forms. Our team will guide you through the check-in process.
