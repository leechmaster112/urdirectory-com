FAQ:

What types of surfaces can Lancaster Power Wash Pros clean in Lancaster, PA 17603?

We expertly clean a wide range of exterior surfaces including vinyl siding, brick, stucco, wood decks, concrete driveways, patios, and even roofs using our specialized soft wash method.

How does soft washing differ from pressure washing, and when is each used?

Pressure washing uses high-force water to blast away grime, ideal for durable surfaces like concrete. Soft washing employs low pressure with specialized cleaning solutions to gently lift dirt and organic growth, perfect for more delicate materials like asphalt shingles or painted wood to prevent damage.

Will power washing damage my Lancaster home's exterior?

Not with Lancaster Power Wash Pros. We carefully assess each surface and employ the appropriate technique, whether it's high-pressure washing or gentle soft washing, to ensure a thorough clean without causing any harm.

Can power washing remove black streaks from my roof?

Absolutely. Those unsightly black streaks are often Gloeocapsa magma, a type of algae. Our soft wash service is specifically designed to effectively and safely eliminate this growth, restoring your roof's appearance and extending its lifespan.

How often should I have my Lancaster home power washed?

The frequency depends on your home's exposure to the elements and local conditions, but typically, a professional cleaning every 1-3 years can significantly improve curb appeal and prevent long-term damage from dirt, mold, and mildew accumulation.
