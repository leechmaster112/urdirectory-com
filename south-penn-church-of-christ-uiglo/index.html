South Penn Church of Christ

Discover the vibrant Church of Christ community at South Penn Church of Christ in Oklahoma City, where faith, fellowship, and service come together in a welcoming environment. As a dedicated Church of Christ congregation, we focus on spreading the gospel, serving our local community, and building one another up through meaningful worship and Bible-based teachings. Located at 11600 South Pennsylvania, our Church of Christ offers Sunday worship at 10 AM, with additional gatherings on Wednesday at 7 PM, providing opportunities for spiritual growth, prayer, and connection. Whether you're seeking a Church of Christ near me for inspiring sermons, family-friendly ministries, or community outreach programs, South Penn Church of Christ invites you to join our family of believers. Experience the joy of authentic worship, engage in Bible studies that deepen your faith, and participate in events that foster unity and service. With a commitment to New Testament principles and a heart for outreach, our Oklahoma City Church of Christ is here to support your spiritual journey—visit us today and become part of something truly uplifting!
