Continuum Rx

ContinuumRx, an Acelpa Health company, is a specialty pharmacy and infusion center in Chantilly, VA, located on Pleasant Valley Road. We provide infusion therapy on-site and at home across Northern Virginia. Our team administers immune globulin (IVIG and SCIG), IV antibiotics, parenteral nutrition (TPN), enteral nutrition, heart failure therapy, and biologic infusions. Before your first infusion, we support you with benefits verification and prior authorization, and our pharmacists and nurses build a care plan around your needs. Referring physicians stay informed at every stage of treatment. Hospitals, clinics, and physician offices can send referrals directly. Accredited by the Accreditation Commission for Health Care (ACHC).
