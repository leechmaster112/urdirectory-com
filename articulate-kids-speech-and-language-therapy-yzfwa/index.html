Welcome to Articulate Kids Speech and Language Therapy, your dedicated partner for supporting the communication development of children right here in New York.

At Articulate Kids, we understand that clear communication is the cornerstone of a child's social, emotional, and academic success. Our team of experienced and compassionate speech and language therapists is committed to providing high-quality, individualized therapy to help New York children overcome challenges with speech sounds, language comprehension and expression, and social communication skills.

We specialize in addressing a range of needs, including:

Articulation and Phonology: Helping children produce speech sounds accurately, whether they are learning English or another language. Language Delays and Disorders: Building vocabulary, grammar, comprehension, and expressive language skills for children who struggle to understand or use language effectively. Receptive and Expressive Language: Supporting children across the spectrum in developing the ability to understand spoken and written language and express their thoughts, ideas, and needs. Early Intervention: Providing timely support for toddlers and preschoolers to maximize developmental potential. Stuttering: Offering assessment and therapy techniques to help children manage and reduce disfluencies. Social Communication: Teaching pragmatic skills (how to use language in social contexts) to help children interact effectively with peers and adults.

Our approach is tailored to each child's unique needs. We work collaboratively with parents, caregivers, and educators to create a supportive environment for progress. We believe in making therapy engaging and fun, ensuring children feel empowered and motivated throughout their journey.

For families seeking expert speech and language therapy services specifically for their child in New York, please contact us today. We are here to answer your questions and discuss how our services can support your child's communication journey.
