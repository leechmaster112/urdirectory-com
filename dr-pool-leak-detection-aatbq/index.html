Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) - Orlando, FL 32789

Q1: How do you find leaks in pools in Orlando, FL 32789?

A1: We employ a scientific process using specialized equipment. This typically involves electronic leak detection (sniffer units), pressure testing, and flow rate analysis. We start by understanding your pool's type and symptoms, then apply the appropriate diagnostic method to accurately pinpoint the leak's location, even if it's hidden behind walls, coping, or liner folds.

Q2: How long does a typical pool leak detection service take in Orlando, FL 32789?

A2: The duration can vary depending on the pool size, complexity, and the nature of the leak. A simple residential pool might take 1-3 hours, while larger or commercial pools, or those with complex plumbing, could require a full day. We always provide an estimated timeframe after our initial assessment.

Q3: What are the signs that my Orlando, FL 32789 pool might have a leak?

A3: Common signs include consistently dropping water levels despite normal use, unusually high water bills, visible algae growth in specific areas (often indicating ground water intrusion), strange noises from the pump, or uneven water depth. However, sometimes leaks are internal and show no obvious signs, which is why professional detection is crucial.

Q4: What causes pool leaks in Orlando, FL 32789?

A4: Common causes include cracks or breaks in the pool shell (gunite, concrete, vinyl liner, or fiberglass), damaged plumbing lines (return jets, main drain, skimmer), leaking light fixtures, cracks in coping stones, or even damage to the pool liner or fabric. Orlando's specific soil conditions and freeze-thaw cycles can also contribute to structural stress over time.

Q5: What happens after you find the leak in my Orlando, FL 32789 pool?

A5: Once we locate the leak, we provide you with a detailed report explaining the findings. We do not perform repairs ourselves unless specifically requested and within our service scope (which typically includes minor crack repairs). Our primary service is detection. We will recommend the appropriate course of action, whether it's contacting a pool contractor for repairs or addressing the issue ourselves if qualified.
