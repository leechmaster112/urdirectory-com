Bark Park at Heritage Park

Bark Park at Heritage Park is Henderson's flagship off-leash dog area on S Racetrack Rd, featuring separate fenced sections for large and small dogs plus an agility course for dogs that need a challenge. It sits inside the larger Heritage Park complex run by the City of Henderson. Hours, photos, and directions are on the park's Doggie Park Near Me listing.

FAQ:

Q: What are the designated areas within Bark Park at Heritage Park?

A: Bark Park at Heritage Park features a distinct, secure area specifically designed for smaller breeds, ensuring they have a safe and less intimidating space to play, alongside a larger, open area for bigger and more energetic dogs.

Q: Is there shade available for dogs and owners?

A: Yes, efforts are made to provide shade throughout the park, offering respite from the Henderson sun for both our canine guests and their human companions.

Q: What kind of surfaces can dogs play on?

A: The park offers varied surfaces suitable for play, including open grassy areas for running and exploring.

Q: Are there separate entrances for different dog sizes?

A: While there are designated play zones, the park utilizes a double-gated entry system for overall security, which is beneficial for all sizes of dogs.

Q: What is the primary goal of Bark Park at Heritage Park's daily operations?

A: Our daily priority is ensuring a clean, safe, and enjoyable off-leash environment where dogs can socialize and exercise freely, and where owners can relax and connect.
