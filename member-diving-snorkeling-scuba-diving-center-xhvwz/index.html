Member Diving - Snorkeling & Scuba Diving Center

Member Diving is a Swiss diving school in Koh Samui, Thailand, offering exclusive scuba diving and snorkeling experiences for beginners and certified divers. With small group sizes (maximum 4 students per instructor), we guarantee personalized training, safety, and comfort at every step. Our professional scuba instructors have years of experience and know the best dive sites in the Gulf of Thailand, including Koh Tao, Sail Rock, and Koh Samui’s vibrant coral reefs. Whether you want to try your first Discover Scuba Diving experience, earn your SSI diving certification, or enjoy a family-friendly snorkeling tour, we provide the perfect balance of safety, fun, and adventure with Samui's most comfortable dive catamaran.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of diving courses do you offer at your Bophut location?

A1: We offer a range of PADI courses starting from the Open Water Diver certification for beginners. We also provide refresher courses, dive specialty courses (like Night Diving or Underwater Navigation), and equipment training, all conducted from our base in Bophut.

Q2: Are the dive sites accessible from your Bophut center?

A2: Yes, we organize dives and snorkeling trips to several excellent local dive sites easily accessible from Bophut. Our guides are familiar with the specific conditions and marine life in this area, ensuring a safe and rewarding experience.

Q3: Do you provide all the necessary diving equipment?

A3: Absolutely. We have a well-maintained selection of modern diving equipment, including tanks, regulators, wetsuits, and snorkeling gear. All equipment is regularly serviced and sanitized according to strict hygiene standards. Rental fees are clearly stated.

Q4: Is previous experience required to start scuba diving with Member Diving?

A4: No prior experience is needed. We welcome absolute beginners! Our Open Water Diver course is designed for anyone who is in good physical health. The course includes both classroom sessions and practical pool training before you go on your first scuba dive trip from Bophut.

Q5: What is the best time of year to dive in Bophut, Koh Samui?

A5: Koh Samui generally offers good diving conditions year-round. The water temperature is warm, and visibility is often excellent. While the peak season (December to April) offers the calmest seas and best visibility, diving can be enjoyed comfortably throughout the year from our Bophut location.
