Frequently Asked Questions

1. What types of massage do you offer at your Llanhilleth clinic?

We primarily offer classic Swedish massage and deep tissue massage at our Zen Therapies location on Railway St. These techniques are chosen for their effectiveness in promoting relaxation and addressing muscle tension specific to our clients' needs.

2. How long does a typical massage treatment take at Zen Therapies?

Typically, a standard massage session at our Abertillery clinic lasts between 30 and 60 minutes. We recommend booking a 60-minute session for a more comprehensive treatment experience.

3. Do I need to book an appointment in advance for a massage in Llanhilleth?

Yes, we recommend booking your appointment ahead of time. You can easily schedule your visit by calling us or using our website. This ensures that we can allocate the appropriate time for your treatment.

4. What should I expect during my first visit to Zen Therapies?

Upon your first visit to our clinic at 26 Railway St, you will be greeted by our friendly staff. We will take a moment to discuss your needs and preferences, then guide you to a private treatment room. Our therapists will explain the chosen massage technique and proceed with the treatment, ensuring your comfort throughout.

5. Can I request specific techniques during my massage appointment?

Absolutely. When booking your appointment, you can specify any preferences or areas of concern. Our therapists are trained in various techniques and will adapt their approach to meet your individual requirements during your visit to Zen Therapies.
