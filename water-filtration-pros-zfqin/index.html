Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the first step to getting a water filtration system at my property?

The first step is to contact Water Filtration Pros for a professional water analysis. Our pros will test your water to identify specific contaminants and discuss your goals. Based on the results, we will recommend the most effective filtration system for your situation.

2. What types of filtration systems do the Water Filtration Pros install?

We are experts in installing whole-house filtration systems, under-sink reverse osmosis units, and water softeners. Each system is chosen and installed by our pros to address the unique water quality challenges found at your location.

3. Do you service and maintain the systems you install?

Yes, professional maintenance is a key part of our service. Water Filtration Pros provides scheduled filter changes and system check-ups to ensure your water filtration system continues to operate at peak performance and efficiency.

4. How do I know if I need a water softener from Water Filtration Pros?

Signs of hard water include scale buildup on fixtures, spotty dishes, and dry skin. Our pros can perform a simple test to measure your water's hardness and definitively determine if a softener is the right solution for you.
