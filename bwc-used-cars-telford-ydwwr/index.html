BWC Used Cars Telford

BWC Used Cars Telford is a trusted used car dealer based in Telford, Shropshire, offering a carefully selected range of quality second-hand vehicles and a friendly, personalised service. Whether you are buying your first car or upgrading to a reliable family used vehicle, our regularly updated stock includes options to suit a wide range of budgets and lifestyles. Our experienced, close-knit team takes pride in preparing every car to a high standard, ensuring a smooth and straightforward buying experience. All vehicles undergo a full HPI check and a thorough pre-delivery inspection carried out by an independent garage, giving you confidence and peace of mind. Part exchange is always welcome. We proudly serve customers from Wellington, Ironbridge, Shifnal, Oakengates and the wider Shropshire area. Visit our Telford used car showroom or get in touch to arrange a test drive today.
