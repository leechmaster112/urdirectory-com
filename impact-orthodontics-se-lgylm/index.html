Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What age is recommended for a child's first orthodontic visit at Impact Orthodontics SE?

A1: We recommend that children have their first orthodontic evaluation around the age of seven. This early visit allows us to monitor their dental and jaw development and identify any potential issues that may require early intervention.

Q2: What types of orthodontic treatments do you offer for teens at 280 Midpark Way SE Suite 208?

A2: At our Midnapore location, we offer a range of treatments for teens, including traditional braces (metal and ceramic options) and clear aligner therapy. We will assess your teen's specific needs to recommend the most suitable treatment plan.

Q3: How long does orthodontic treatment typically last?

A3: The duration of orthodontic treatment varies depending on the complexity of the case and the individual patient's response. On average, treatment can range from 12 to 24 months. We will provide a more precise estimate after your initial consultation.

Q4: Will my child experience pain during orthodontic treatment?

A4: It is normal to experience some mild discomfort or soreness after braces are adjusted or when starting a new set of aligners. This is usually temporary and can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. Our team will provide guidance on managing any discomfort.

Q5: How can I schedule an appointment at Impact Orthodontics SE?

A5: You can schedule an appointment by visiting our website at https://impactortho.com or by calling us directly at (403) 256-7797. We look forward to hearing from you!
