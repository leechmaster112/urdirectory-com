Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What kind of tours do you offer from your Miami Beach location?

A: We specialize in private, full-hour sightseeing tours departing from our base at 1626 Collins Ave in Miami Beach. These tours provide a comprehensive aerial view of South Beach, the Miami coastline, and surrounding areas visible from our unique vantage point.

Q2: How long does the helicopter sightseeing tour last?

A: Our standard sightseeing tour is approximately 60 minutes in duration, offering ample time for scenic views and narration from our experienced pilots based out of Miami Beach.

Q3: Is there an age limit for flying with Aero Helicopter Tours of South Beach?

A: Generally, we require passengers to be at least 2 years old. However, specific weight and height requirements may apply for safety reasons. Infants must be accompanied by an adult. Please contact us directly for the most current policy details.

Q4: What should I wear or bring on my helicopter tour?

A: We recommend wearing comfortable, weather-appropriate clothing. Closed-toe shoes are suggested. Please bring sunscreen, a hat, and sunglasses, as the sun can be strong. We provide safety instructions and headsets for your comfort during the flight.

Q5: Can I take photos or videos during the flight?

A: Absolutely! We encourage you to capture the stunning views. We provide a complimentary digital photo and video package with every flight departing from our Miami Beach base. You can also purchase additional copies of your flight memories.
