Wheeler Memorial Dog Park

Wheeler Memorial Dog Park is a 1.5-acre fenced off-leash park in Columbus' Harrison West neighborhood, with separate small-dog and large-dog areas that rotate monthly so the turf can recover. The park includes hills, trees, paths, benches, picnic tables, a kiosk, acorn-lamp lighting, and a drinking fountain with a low catch bowl for small dogs. Hours, photos, and directions are on its Doggie Park Near Me listing.

Location

Wheeler Memorial Dog Park offers a dedicated, fenced sanctuary for your canine companions to roam and play off-leash. Spanning a generous 1.5 acres within Columbus's vibrant Harrison West neighborhood, this park is more than just an open space; it's a carefully curated environment designed for canine joy and owner peace of mind. What truly sets Wheeler Memorial apart day-to-day is the palpable sense of community that blossoms among its four-legged visitors and their humans. Each wagging tail and friendly bark contributes to a unique atmosphere, fostering spontaneous play sessions and a shared appreciation for this local gem. It's a place where dogs can truly be dogs, exploring, socializing, and burning energy in a safe, enclosed setting. You can find more details and connect with the park at https://doggieparknearme.com/parks/wheeler-memorial-dog-park-columbus-oh or by calling (614) 645-3111.
What are the hours of operation for Wheeler Memorial Dog Park? The park is open daily from dawn until dusk, allowing plenty of time for your dog to enjoy the space. Is there a separate area for small dogs? While the park is a unified 1.5-acre space, owners often self-regulate and ensure appropriate play dynamics between dogs of different sizes. Are there any rules regarding dog behavior at the park? Yes, all dogs must be licensed, vaccinated, and under voice control. Owners are responsible for their dog's behavior and for cleaning up after them. What kind of terrain can I expect at the park? The park primarily features grassy areas for running and playing, providing a natural and comfortable surface for dogs. Is there water available for dogs at Wheeler Memorial Dog Park? While there are no dedicated water fountains for dogs, it's recommended that owners bring their own portable water bowls and water for their pets.
